# pap apothican servant

## overview

In Revelations, the **Apothicon Servant** can finally be upgraded.

Important note:
- the base **Apothicon Servant** still comes from the **Mystery Box**
- this file only covers the **upgrade procedure**

To upgrade it, you must:
- get the base Apothicon Servant from the box
- shoot 5 Element 115 stones in the sky
- return to Pack-a-Punch inside the Apothicon
- Pack-a-Punch the weapon

## step 1 - get the base Apothicon Servant

Before doing anything else, you must pull the **Apothicon Servant** from the Mystery Box.

Important note:
- if you do not already have the weapon, the stone step does nothing useful

## step 2 - shoot the 5 Element 115 stones

There are 5 floating Element 115 stones around the map. You must shoot all 5 of them.

### stone 1 - Shangri-La
Located above the pyramid area in Shangri-La.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/stone1.jpg

### stone 2 - spawn
Located high in the sky to the right when facing the portal in spawn.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/stone2.jpg

### stone 3 - Mob of the Dead
Located to the left when facing the portal in Mob of the Dead.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/stone3.jpg

### stone 4 - Verruckt near the jump pad to Mob
Located near the jump pad leading from Verruckt to Mob of the Dead.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/stone4.jpg

### stone 5 - Verruckt near the portal
Located near the Verruckt portal area.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/stone5.jpg

## step 3 - upgrade it in the Apothicon

After shooting all 5 stones, go back inside the Apothicon and use Pack-a-Punch on the weapon.

### steps
1. Get the base Apothicon Servant from the box.
2. Shoot all 5 Element 115 stones.
3. Return to the Apothicon stomach.
4. Use Pack-a-Punch.
5. The Apothicon Servant becomes upgraded.

Related image: images/pap_apothican_servant/apothicanupado.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Get the Apothicon Servant from the Mystery Box.
2. Shoot the stone in Shangri-La.
3. Shoot the stone in spawn.
4. Shoot the stone in Mob of the Dead.
5. Shoot the 2 stones in Verruckt.
6. Return to the Apothicon.
7. Pack-a-Punch the weapon to upgrade it.
