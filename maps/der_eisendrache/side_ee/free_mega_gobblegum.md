# free mega gobblegum

## overview

Just like on Shadows of Evil, Der Eisendrache has a small easter egg that gives you a random Mega Gobblegum.

First, you must pick up a plant in the teleporter room. Then, after going back in time, you place that plant back on the same shelf. When you return to the present, the plant has grown and can be interacted with for the reward.

## steps

1. Go to the teleporter room, accessible from the underground pyramid area.
2. Find the plant on the shelf and pick it up.
3. Go back in time.
4. Place the plant back on the same shelf.
5. Return to the present.
6. Go back to the shelf.
7. Interact with the grown plant to receive a random Mega Gobblegum.

Important note:
- To go back in time, you can either progress the main easter egg steps that send you to the past, or use the Plunger setup route.
