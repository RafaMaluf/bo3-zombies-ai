# wisp

## overview

The Wisp is a special side easter egg tied to players who have already completed all the previous BO3 main easter eggs.

If at least one player in the lobby has done the full earlier quest chain, a glowing wisp appears on the truck in spawn.

This can later be used during the main easter egg to unlock extra benefits.

## step 1 - find the wisp

The Wisp appears on the truck in the spawn area.

### important note
- if you pick it up and then get hit by an enemy, you lose it for the rest of the game

Because of that, the best practice is:
- leave it there until the correct easter egg step

Related image:
- images/side_ees/wisplocation.jpg

## step 2 - place it in samantha's room

During the main easter egg, when S.O.P.H.I.A is waiting above the Kino teleporter and you are about to go to Samantha's room:
1. pick up the wisp
2. enter Samantha's room through the teleporter
3. place the wisp on the toy chest

## result

After doing this:
- all Mystery Box weapons come out **Pack-a-Punched**
- you can buy **Takeo's Katana** in Shangri-La, in the same area as the time-trial melee weapons
