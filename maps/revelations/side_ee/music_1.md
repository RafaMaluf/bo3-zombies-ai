# music 1

## overview

The secret song in Revelations is **The Gift**.

To activate it, interact with 3 teddy bears.

## teddy bear locations

- on a chair in Kino Der Toten in front of the stage
- on a chair in Verruckt next to the green tanks from Zetsubou
- on a stool in Mob of the Dead just after the Buried tunnel

## steps

1. Interact with the teddy in Kino.
2. Interact with the teddy in Verruckt.
3. Interact with the teddy in Mob of the Dead.
4. The Gift starts playing.
