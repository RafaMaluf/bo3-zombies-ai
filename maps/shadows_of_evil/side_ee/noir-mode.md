# noir mode

At the boxing gym, go to the second floor. Line yourself up to the right of the desk, crouch, aim at the portrait, and interact with it. This places a gray / noir filter over the game for the rest of the match.

This appears bugged on PC and worked properly on console. It is impossible to turn back off once activated.
