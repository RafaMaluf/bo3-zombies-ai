# general

Gorod Krovi is one of the densest and most feature-packed maps in Black Ops 3 Zombies. The map is set in a war-torn Stalingrad filled with dragons, manglers, valkyrie drones, mechanical systems, trials, buildables, and one of the more involved easter eggs in the game.

A good way to understand Gorod Krovi is to think of it as a map built around 3 major layers:

- **base setup**
- **special equipment and quest items**
- **main easter egg progression**

The map itself is mainly divided into these major areas:
- **Belinski Square** (spawn)
- **Department Store**
- **Infirmary**
- **Armory**
- **Tank Factory**
- **Supply Depot**
- **Dragon Command**

## core setup flow

A normal early-game progression on Gorod Krovi usually looks like this:

1. open the map and reach **Dragon Command**
2. turn on **power**
3. begin using the **code cylinders / Groph Pod** system
4. unlock **Pack-a-Punch**
5. start working on:
   - **Dragon Strike**
   - **Shield**
   - **Gauntlet of Siegfried**
   - **trials**
6. move into the main easter egg if desired

## power and Pack-a-Punch

Power is turned on in **Dragon Command**, where you also activate **S.O.P.H.I.A.**, which is essential if you want to do the easter egg.

Pack-a-Punch is not unlocked directly by power alone. You also need to use the **code cylinder** system:
- zombies can drop **blue**, **yellow**, or **red** code cylinders
- each color belongs to a specific machine
- using the correct machine launches a **Groph Pod**
- defending the pod gives you a **Network Circuit**
- once enough circuits are placed in the bunker underground, you can use the **dragon ride stations**
- the dragon takes you to the **Hatchery / Pack-a-Punch area**

So on Gorod Krovi, dragon travel is not just a visual gimmick. It is part of the core map progression.

## dragons and dragon travel

One of the defining mechanics of the map is flying on dragons. After the Pack-a-Punch route is unlocked, dragons can be called from 3 different stations:
- **Dragon Command**
- **Supply Depot**
- **Tank Factory**

These dragon rides are used for:
- reaching the **Hatchery**
- accessing **Pack-a-Punch**
- warming the **dragon egg**
- later side upgrades such as **Dragon Wings**

## major buildables and special equipment

Gorod Krovi has several important pieces of equipment that are more than optional extras.

### Fafnir Shield
The map’s shield is the **Guard of Fafnir**, a shield that can blast green fire and also protects you from dragon fire while held.

### Upgraded Shield
The shield can later be upgraded into **Tiamat’s Maw**, giving it better durability and stronger red fire.

### Dragon Strike
The **Dragon Strike Controller** lets you call a dragon fire attack onto a targeted area, similar to an air strike. It can also be upgraded.

### Gauntlet of Siegfried
The specialist weapon of the map is the **Gauntlet of Siegfried**, a small dragon mounted on your arm that works like a flamethrower and can also be sent out to attack.

### special gear
Gorod Krovi also has wearable gear pieces that give passive bonuses, such as:
- **Dragon Wings**
- **Mangler Helmet**
- **Valkyrie Helmet**

## enemy types and map identity

Gorod Krovi stands out because of how many special threats it mixes together:
- normal zombies
- dragons burning sections of the map
- **Russian Manglers**
- **Valkyrie Drones**
- mechs and heavy fire-based hazards

Because of that, the map often rewards equipment and positioning more than raw movement speed.

## trials

Near spawn, each player has a grave with trials. These are separated into tiers and give rewards such as:
- Max Ammo
- Pack-a-Punched weapon
- extra perk slot and free perk
- Gauntlet of Siegfried progression

Trials are useful both for normal survival and for certain side objectives.

## Ray Gun Mark 3 note

The **Ray Gun Mark 3** is not a quest weapon on Gorod Krovi. It comes from the **Mystery Box**.

Even so, it is one of the most useful weapons on the map, especially for:
- lockdowns
- Dragon Strike setup
- easter egg steps
- boss fight damage and crowd control

## main easter egg overview

The Gorod Krovi easter egg is one of the harder BO3 quests if the team is not prepared. It includes:
- multiple independent setup steps
- trophy collection
- escort-style objectives
- bomb defusal
- valve routing
- S.O.P.H.I.A. interaction
- boss fights against both a **dragon** and **Nikolai 1.0**

The easter egg is not just one straight line. Several early steps can be done in parallel, which is why organizing the information matters so much.

## practical note

Gorod Krovi can feel overwhelming at first because it has so many overlapping systems, but the map becomes much clearer when split into these sections:

- **power + Pack-a-Punch**
- **dragon travel**
- **shield**
- **Dragon Strike**
- **Gauntlet of Siegfried**
- **trials**
- **main easter egg**
- **special side gear and side easter eggs**

That is also the best way to structure the knowledge base for it.
