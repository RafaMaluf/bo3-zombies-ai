# 115 secret song

## Side easter egg - Secret Song

By activating 3 items on the map, you will be able to listen to the music "115". The three items are meteorite shards.

- The first one is right in the spawning room, beneath the stairs, near the window.

  Related image: images/source-guide/1c8f88c92524893e1201f15d49eeaa070d720282-19b8d3588a.webp
- On a shelf, above the stairs, in the room after the outside courtyard
