# main ee

## overview

The goal of the Der Eisendrache easter egg is to kill the older version of Dempsey from the Ultimis crew, who is being held inside a rocket headed for the Moon.

This easter egg can be completed with **1 to 4 players**.

Important bow requirement note from the guide:
- **Solo (Ranked): 1 upgraded bow**
- **2 players (Ranked): 2 upgraded bows**
- **3 players (Ranked): 3 upgraded bows**
- **4 players (Ranked or Unranked): all 4 bows**
- **Any game on Local: all 4 bows**
- **Any game on Unranked: all 4 bows**

Before starting, make sure you have:
- the **Wrath of the Ancients**
- the required number of **upgraded bows**
- the **Ragnarok DG-4**
- a good weapon setup for the boss fight

## step 1 - charge the teleporter with the base bow

You must use the **basic bow**, not an upgraded one. If you are holding an upgraded bow, go to its altar and swap it back for the Wrath of the Ancients.

Then go to the teleporter room and shoot a **charged shot** at the 6 blue glowing balls on the teleporter. If done correctly, the balls turn orange / gold.

### steps
1. Make sure you are holding the **Wrath of the Ancients**.
2. Go to the teleporter room.
3. Fire a charged arrow at the 6 blue glowing teleporter balls.
4. Confirm the teleporter lights change to orange / gold.

## step 2 - find and shoot 4 electrified objects

After charging the teleporter, you will hear a Margwa roar. This means an object somewhere on the map has become electrified. You must shoot it with an **upgraded bow**.

After a short delay, another roar will happen and another item will electrify. You must shoot **4 objects total** out of the 8 possible locations.

Possible electrified item locations:
- car wheel near Double Tap
- phone near Quick Revive
- box in the room above Double Tap
- globe near Samantha's bedroom
- phone on the wall near the power lever
- clock in the living quarters near the clock tower
- clock in the church above the dragon
- radio in the church on the other side from Speed Cola

Important note:
- Up to around step 10, if you advance the round, you may need to do this spark step again to recharge the teleporter for time travel.

## step 3 - first trip to the past

Once the teleporter is charged again, the pad glows purple. All players must stand on it to travel back in time.

Inside the past, you must do 3 things before you are automatically sent back:
- pick up the **blue soul canister**
- pick up the **2 fuses**
- remember the **3-symbol password** Dr. Groph enters to lock the safe

The possible symbols are:
- arrow
- circle
- card
- lightning

### steps
1. Wait until the teleporter pad glows purple.
2. All players stand on the teleporter.
3. Travel to the past.
4. Pick up the blue soul canister on the left.
5. Pick up the 2 fuses on the box to the right.
6. Memorize the 3-symbol password shown on the screen.

## step 4 - insert fuse and set the Death Ray to Protect

Go to the roof of the castle. Insert one of the fuses you picked up into the central machine. Then go to the other side of the machine and set it to **Protect**.

### steps
1. Go to the roof.
2. Insert one fuse into the central machine.
3. Move to the other side.
4. Set the machine to **Protect**.

Related images:
- images/main_ee/14fusesinserted.jpg
- images/main_ee/15protectonmachine.jpg

## step 5 - enter the safe code

Hellhounds should now begin spawning, and a console near the clock tower should turn on. If it does not, make sure you have used the Death Ray at least once this game.

Enter the code you saw in the past from top to bottom.

### steps
1. Go to the console near the clock tower.
2. Enter the remembered 3-symbol code from top to bottom.
3. If you fail, go back in time and get the code again.

Related image: images/main_ee/16-put-the-code-on-the-computer.jpg

## step 6 - open the safe and place the fuses in the Tesla coils

Go back to the teleporter room. The safe should now be open. Take:
- the **2 fuses**
- the **card**

Then go back to the roof and place the 2 fuses into the 2 Tesla coils. After that, set the Death Ray back to **Destroy**.

### steps
1. Return underground to the teleporter room.
2. Open the safe and take the 2 fuses and the card.
3. Go back to the roof.
4. Insert the 2 fuses into the Tesla coils.
5. Set the Death Ray back to **Destroy**.

Related images:
- images/main_ee/17vaultopen.jpg

## step 7 - complete the two Simon Says consoles

Now you must do 2 memory games:
- one near the clock tower
- one on the landing pad

When you activate a console, 4 symbols are shown first:
- arrow
- circle
- card
- lightning

Then one symbol appears on the main screen, and you must quickly interact with the matching panel.

Important notes:
- unlimited hellhounds spawn during these memory games
- if you fail the second console, you must restart from the first one
- after each successful memory game, the Tesla coil becomes white

### steps
1. Start the first memory game near the clock tower.
2. Complete it correctly.
3. Go to the second console on the landing pad.
4. Complete it correctly.
5. Confirm both Tesla coils are glowing white.

## step 8 - crash the rocket and collect the Vrill Generator

Go back to the roof and set the Death Ray to **Protect** again. Then interact with the green button behind the machine. Stay away from the second courtyard.

The Tesla coils will hit the rocket, causing it to crash in the courtyard. Then you can pick up the **Vrill Generator** near the crash.

### steps
1. Go back to the roof.
2. Set the Death Ray to **Protect**.
3. Press the green button behind the machine.
4. Stay away from the courtyard.
5. After the rocket crashes, pick up the Vrill Generator.

Related images:

## step 9 - second trip to the past and collect the ornamented stone

Go back to the teleporter and use it to travel to the past again. If the teleporter is no longer purple, you must recharge it by repeating the spark step.

Once in the past, interact with the book on your right. This opens a crate near the exit. From that crate, pick up the **ornamented stone**.

### steps
1. Recharge the teleporter if needed by doing the spark step again.
2. Travel to the past.
3. Interact with the book on the right.
4. Open the crate near the exit.
5. Pick up the ornamented stone.

Related images:
- images/main_ee/24booktointeract.jpg

## step 10 - start the keeper soul step

Go underground, to the grave near where you picked up the Wrath of the Ancients, and insert the **golden rod**. This spawns a ghost keeper that moves to 4 locations on the map.

At each location, you must stand inside the circle and kill zombies with the **correct bow**. The required bow is indicated by the color on your screen. When kills are correct, glowing balls leave the zombies.

Important notes:
- infinite hellhounds spawn during this whole phase
- in 2-player games, 2 bows can cover the 4 spots, but each bow may be needed twice
- the Lightning Bow is especially strong here
- do not forget to place the ornamented stone in the slot near Double Tap

### steps
1. Insert the golden rod in the grave.
2. Follow the ghost keeper.
3. At each stop, stand in the circle.
4. Kill zombies with the correct bow.
5. Repeat for all 4 keeper stops.
6. Place the ornamented stone in the slot near Double Tap.

## step 11 - place the blue canister near the pyramid

After finishing the keeper soul step, follow the ghost underground and place the **blue glowing canister** you picked up earlier near the pyramid.

### steps
1. Return underground.
2. Follow the ghost.
3. Place the blue canister near the pyramid.

Related image: images/main_ee/28bluecanisteronpyramid.jpg

## step 12 - boss fight

Before starting the boss fight, make sure you are ready:
- bow
- strong weapon
- Juggernog / Quick Revive
- fresh shield
- ammo
- Gobblegums if desired

To enter the boss room, all players must place their **Ragnarok DG-4** on the glowing blue tiles from the anti-gravity room.

Inside the boss arena:
- skeleton zombies spawn
- the boss attacks and sometimes teleports to the center
- when he charges the deadly electric attack, a glowing ball appears in the center hole
- you must place a Ragnarok DG-4 there
- this weakens the boss and makes the chest symbol glow
- shoot the chest symbol to damage him

After enough damage, the boss disappears and Panzers spawn. Kill them, then repeat the boss damage cycle until he dies in a white flash.

### steps
1. Place all Ragnaroks on the glowing blue tiles.
2. Enter the boss room.
3. Avoid zombies and boss attacks.
4. When the boss charges the electric attack, place a Ragnarok on the glowing center ball.
5. Shoot the boss chest when it becomes vulnerable.
6. Kill the Panzers when they spawn.
7. Repeat until the boss dies.

Important notes:
- you can survive the electric attack by hiding behind a pillar
- many players prefer spamming normal arrows instead of charged ones on the boss

Related images:

## step 13 - final step with the Summoning Key

After the boss dies, place the **Summoning Key** from Shadows of Evil in the pyramid, then pick it back up.

Then go up the stairs near the clock tower and place the key in the consoles where you entered the code earlier. This triggers the ending.

### steps
1. Place the Summoning Key in the pyramid.
2. Pick it back up.
3. Go to the console area near the clock tower.
4. Insert / use the key there.
5. Trigger the ending cutscene.

## full progression summary

1. Use the base bow to charge the teleporter.
2. Find and shoot 4 electrified spark objects with an upgraded bow.
3. Travel to the past and collect the blue canister, 2 fuses, and the safe code.
4. Insert one fuse into the central machine and set it to Protect.
5. Enter the safe code at the clock tower console.
6. Open the safe, collect the 2 fuses and card, and place the fuses in the Tesla coils.
7. Set the Death Ray to Destroy.
8. Complete the two Simon Says consoles.
9. Set the Death Ray to Protect again and crash the rocket.
10. Collect the Vrill Generator.
11. Travel to the past again and get the ornamented stone.
12. Insert the golden rod in the grave and complete the keeper soul step.
13. Place the ornamented stone near Double Tap.
14. Place the blue canister near the pyramid.
15. Enter and complete the boss fight.
16. Place the Summoning Key in the pyramid, take it back, and use it at the console to finish the easter egg.
