# music 2 - gramophones

## overview

A second secret music easter egg can be triggered by interacting with 3 gramophones. This plays a part of a requiem by Anton Reicha.

## gramophone locations

- by the stairs between the pyramid room and the grave room
- beside Speed Cola in the church
- on a shelf in Samantha's room, on your left when coming from the first dragon

## steps

1. Go to the first gramophone by the stairs between the pyramid and grave room and interact with it.
2. Go to the gramophone beside Speed Cola in the church and interact with it.
3. Go to Samantha's room and interact with the gramophone on the shelf.
4. After all 3 are activated, the music begins to play.
