# Samantha's Sorrow

## Side easter egg - Samantha's Sorrow

Just like other Zombies Chronicles maps, there is a new secret easter egg, involving samantha dolls and giving a max ammo.

To begin with, head to the courtyard outside the theater. You must stay near the blue metal door. You will eventually hear 3 codes composed by 3 series of knockings on the door. Once the sequence is done, you will need to knife the door back, with the sequence you just heard.

Related image: images/source-guide/bd0aa47f7a50312e2b300f185128b5ed18dd33bf-ea6cd07632.webp

To "enter" the code, if for exemple it is '1 1 5', you will need to knife the door once, then wait like a second, knife it again, wait a sec, then knife it 5 times.

A sound should let you know when it's done.

The multiple codes possible are:

- 1-1-5
- 1-2-1
- 1-4-3
- 2-4-1
- 3-2-1
- 5-1-2
- 5-3-4
- 6-2-4
- 6-6-6
- 9-3-5

I advice you gobblegums or a friend taking the zombies away from you because it can be difficult to do this with noise around you.

Once the 3 codes are entered, go to the main room in the theater. There is a samantha doll in front of a projector, lying on the ground on the right of the stage.

Related image: images/source-guide/2e9e1740cbce926464ea31e254d2a99e5823bfee-d5092a3646.webp

Once activated, 5 dolls will appear within the theater. There are more possible locations than actually appearing dolls. If you don't shoot the doll fast enough, samantha will laugh and you will have to interact again with the first doll.
