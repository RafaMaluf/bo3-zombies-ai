# speed trials

## overview

If you complete the Gorod Krovi main easter egg, you receive all 9 perks.

If you complete it in **under 1 hour and 30 minutes**, you keep those perks even after going down and getting revived.

In solo, you still lose Quick Revive, but you can rebuy a perk from the Wunderfizz and it will always give Quick Revive first.

## requirement

- complete the main easter egg in **less than 1h30**

## result

- keep the 9 perks after being revived
- in solo, rebuying from Wunderfizz restores Quick Revive first

## practical note

This is not a separate gameplay quest with a special activation step. It is a reward tied directly to your easter egg completion time.
