# skull of nan sapwe

## overview

The **Skull of Nan Sapwe** is the specialist weapon on Zetsubou No Shima. Its basic attack destroys zombies, while its alternate use can control / stop enemies. It is also an important prerequisite for other systems on the map.

To obtain it, you must complete **4 skull cleansing rituals**. Each bloody skull has a symbol on it and must be placed on the matching mini altar somewhere on the map. After cleansing all 4 skulls and returning them to the main altar, a hidden cave opens and you must complete one final Keeper ritual to claim the Skull of Nan Sapwe.

## main altar and ritual setup

Right after spawn, if you go to the right and open the 2 doors, you reach the main ritual courtyard. This area contains:
- the **main altar**
- 4 ritual pillars
- the 4 bloody skulls

Each skull has a symbol on it. The guide notes them as having:
- 2 arrows
- 5 arrows
- 3 arrows
- 6 arrows

You must match each skull to the correct mini altar.

## how each ritual works

When you place the correct skull on the correct mini altar:
- green zombies spawn
- they move toward the skull
- they do **not** attack you directly
- your job is to stop them from destroying the ritual

If you succeed:
- the skull is cleaned and becomes white
- pick it up and return it to the main altar

If you fail:
- wait one round
- retrieve the skull from the main altar
- try again

Important notes:
- the **second ritual** spawns spiders
- the **third ritual** also spawns a swamp monster

The altar symbols change from game to game, but the **locations** stay the same.

The 4 mini altar locations are:

## cleansing all 4 skulls

### steps
1. Go to the main altar courtyard.
2. Identify the symbol on a bloody skull.
3. Find the matching mini altar.
4. Place the skull there.
5. Defend the altar from the green zombies.
6. Once the skull is cleansed, pick it back up.
7. Return it to the main altar.
8. Repeat this process for all 4 skulls.

## final cave ritual

After all 4 cleaned skulls are placed back on the main altar, a cave opens downstairs.

Go inside and interact with the skull. This locks you underground and spawns Keepers. Kill enough of them to complete the ritual. When the fight ends, you can claim the Skull of Nan Sapwe.

### steps
1. Cleanse all 4 ritual skulls.
2. Return all 4 cleaned skulls to the main altar.
3. Go into the newly opened cave below.
4. Interact with the skull inside.
5. Survive the Keeper fight.
6. After enough Keepers are killed, pick up the Skull of Nan Sapwe.

## full progression summary

1. Reach the main ritual altar area.
2. Take one bloody skull from the main altar.
3. Match its symbol to the correct mini altar.
4. Place it there and defend the ritual.
5. Return the cleaned skull to the main altar.
6. Repeat for all 4 skulls.
7. After all 4 are returned, enter the opened cave below.
8. Interact with the skull inside.
9. Kill the Keepers until the ritual is complete.
10. Pick up the Skull of Nan Sapwe.
