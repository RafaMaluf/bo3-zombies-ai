# pap

## overview

Pack-a-Punch is unlocked by completing the 4 character rituals, collecting the 4 Gateworms, then going to the underground Sacred Place / Rift, placing the 4 Gateworms on the altar to rebuild the Pack-a-Punch area, and starting the final ritual there. When that ritual ends, Pack-a-Punch opens. After the 2nd and 4th completed district rituals, a Margwa spawns.

The 4 ritual items are:
- Nero's Fountain Pen
- Jackie's Police Badge
- Jessica's Hairpiece / Toupee / Wig
- Floyd's Championship Belt

## get the summoning key + Nero setup

Normally, players use a single Beast to do everything in spawn: break the Summoning Key crate and set up Nero's ritual access in the same transformation. That is the standard efficient route.

### Steps
1. Start in spawn and use the Beast fountain.
2. In Beast Mode, break the crate on the truck to release the Summoning Key.  
   Related image: images/pap/summoning_key_crate.jpg
3. Shock the power box on the crane / central area to open Nero's item access.  
   Related image: images/pap/neros_feather_pen.jpg
4. Grapple up to the upper spawn roof path.  
   Related image: images/pap/grapple_nero_ritual_room.jpg
5. Follow the upper path.
6. Shock the switch that drops the stairs to Nero's ritual room.  
   Related image: images/pap/neros_ritual_room_stairs.jpg
7. Return to human form.
8. Pick up the Summoning Key.
9. Pick up Nero's Fountain Pen.
10. Go up the now-open ritual stairs.
11. Place the pen on the altar.
12. Start the ritual.
13. Survive until it ends.
14. Pick up the Gateworm.  
   Related image: images/pap/neros_worm.jpg

### Important note
- This is usually all done in one Beast if routed correctly.
- A common tip from the Steam guide is to train the Corrupted Keepers rather than killing them, since killing them only makes them respawn and can make the room messier.

## Jackie's ritual / Canals

This ritual can also be fully set up in one Beast if routed efficiently. The key detail is that you can break the badge crate first and shock the Beast-only-wall switch later; the order does not matter as long as both are done before you come back in human form.

### Optimized steps
1. Open Canals.
2. Use the Beast fountain in Canals.
3. On the first descent toward the canal, break the badge crate first.  
   Related image: images/pap/jackies_badge_crate.jpg
4. You can also break the portal door to the underground, since it is already in the way.
5. Continue deeper into the canal, all the way to the end.
6. Pass through the Beast-only wall.  
   Related image: images/pap/jackies_badge_shockwall.jpg
7. Shock the switch behind it to open the grate / access.
8. Continue the route and set up the ritual room access in the same Beast.
9. Grapple to the top of the cabaret roof.  
   Related image: images/pap/grapple_jackie_ritual_room.jpg
10. Go down until you reach the switch that opens / powers the ritual stairs.  
    Related image: images/pap/jackie_ritual_room_stairs.jpg
11. Shock that switch.
12. Return to human form.
13. Pick up Jackie's Police Badge.
14. Go into the ritual room.
15. Place the badge on the altar.
16. Start the ritual.
17. Survive.
18. Pick up the Gateworm.

### Important notes
- The badge setup is not just “break the box”; you also need the shock switch behind the Beast-only wall.
- The efficient route is to do everything in one Beast, then come back in human form and collect the badge / do the ritual.
- If this is your 2nd completed ritual, a Margwa will spawn right after it.

## Jessica's ritual / Footlight

This ritual can be done in one Beast only if the required doors are already open. If the route is not opened correctly, you cannot just grapple straight to the tower and finish it cleanly in one transformation.

### Steps
1. Open Footlight properly so the route is available.
2. Use the Beast fountain.
3. Go from below through the efficient path.
4. Grapple and shock the stairway.  
   Related image: images/pap/grapple_perk_area_jessica.jpg  
   Related image: images/pap/shock_stairs_perk_area_jessica.jpg
5. Go to the edge and jump, then break Jessica's item crate.  
   Related image: images/pap/jessica_wig_crate.jpg
6. Go down and go through the last door of Footlight.
7. Grapple to the top, turn around, and shock the switch.  
   Related image: images/pap/grapple_jessica_ritual_room.jpg
8. Return to human form.
9. Pick up Jessica's Hairpiece / Wig.
10. Enter the ritual room.
11. Place the item on the altar.
12. Start the ritual.
13. Survive.
14. Pick up the Gateworm.

### Important note
- If the needed doors are not already open, this is not realistically a one-Beast setup.

## Floyd's ritual / Waterfront

This ritual can also be done in one Beast if you are quick enough.

### Steps
1. Open Waterfront.
2. Use the Beast fountain.
3. Grapple up to the upper route and break the crate that contains Floyd's Championship Belt.  
   Related image: images/pap/floyd_grapple_and_crate.jpg
4. Jump to the last part of the Waterfront and go to the gym entry.
5. Break the gym / ritual room door.  
   Related image: images/pap/break_door_floyd_ritual_room.jpg
6. Return to human form.
7. Pick up the Championship Belt.
8. Go into the ritual room.
9. Place the belt on the altar.
10. Start the ritual.
11. Survive.
12. Pick up the Gateworm.

## final ritual to unlock Pack-a-Punch

After collecting all 4 Gateworms:

### Steps
1. Go to the underground Sacred Place / Rift.
2. Place all 4 Gateworms on the altar.  
   Related image: images/pap/place_worm_pap_area.jpg
3. This rebuilds / restores the Pack-a-Punch ritual area.
4. Start the final ritual.
5. Survive until it ends.
6. Pack-a-Punch opens.  
   Related image: images/pap/pap_open.jpg
