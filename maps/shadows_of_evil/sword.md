# sword

## base sword

To access the sword room, you must read 3 symbols and then shock the matching 3 symbols on the underground wall in Beast Mode. If you hit a wrong symbol, the wall resets and you must wait until the next round to try again.

### Steps
1. Read the signs. The easiest way to find all three symbols is to take the train from Waterfront to Footlight; this route shows two symbols on the right side of the train. Then, take the train from Footlight to Canals, looking at the right side again for the third symbol. Just check the `(?)` markers on the map and you will know where to look.  
   Related image: images/sword/map_signs.jpg
2. Go underground to the sword symbol wall.
3. Enter Beast Mode.
4. Shock the 3 matching symbols on the wall.  
   Related image: images/sword/wall_with_symbols_underground.jpg
5. If correct, the wall opens.
6. Enter the egg room.
7. Pick up the egg.
8. Find the 4 Eldritch statues hidden in crates around the map.  
   Related image: images/sword/statue_underground.jpg  
   Related image: images/sword/statue_canals.jpg  
   Related image: images/sword/statue_footlight.jpg  
   Related image: images/sword/statue_waterfront.jpg
9. Break each statue crate in Beast Mode.
10. Place the egg on a statue.
11. Kill 10 to 12 zombies near the statue until the soul requirement is complete.
12. Pick the egg back up.
13. Repeat this for all 4 statues.
14. Return underground.
15. Place the charged egg back in the sword room.
16. Take the Apothicon Sword.  
   Related image: images/sword/sword_in_hands.jpg

## upgraded sword / Reborn Keeper Sword

After getting the base sword, each player upgrades only their own sword. The red circle steps can only be done one per round. During these steps, the Margwas spawned for your ritual focus only on you and ignore the other players, which is a very important co-op detail.

### Steps
1. Take your sword to your character ritual site.
2. Interact with the floating spirit.
3. Receive the Arch-Ovum.  
   Related image: images/sword/arch_ovum.jpg
4. Take it to one of the red circles and interact with it.  
   Related image: images/sword/red_circle.jpg
5. There is one red circle in front of each ritual area. The spawn-side one is in Junction, near where you pick up the pen.
6. A Margwa spawns.
7. Kill the Margwa.
8. End the round.
9. Repeat this for the other 3 circles, one per round.
10. Once all 4 are done, return to your character ritual site.
11. Trade in the sword.
12. Receive the Reborn Keeper Sword.  
    Related image: images/sword/recieve_sword_up.jpg
