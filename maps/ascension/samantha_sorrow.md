# Samantha's Sorrow

## Side easter egg - Samantha's sorrow

Like all other Zombies Chronicles maps, you can get a free max ammo and listen to a secret song. To do so, you will firstly need to take 3 dolls with a Gersh Device.

- The first one is bellow the Pack-a-Punch room, on a pipe
  Related image: images/source-guide/9a3863fa4a140b7795d1f031130865e2e3f5e330-301b9b2765.webp
- On top of the building, on your left when you are coming from Widows Wine to the Spawn.
  Related image: images/source-guide/f38563978b3c1d5edb91c60c5390fec0514cf9b6-7a62faaa24.webp
- On pipes, right above Stamin-up
  Related image: images/source-guide/7795d935c7caa1e31d3bfde5b0093bf5d52adc88-94badbba31.webp

Once done, head to the Launching pad area . In a corner, near a mystery box location, you will find a doll near a small fire, in the grass.

Related image: images/source-guide/6736be83bc62366d4b495473f7c101371b9225b8-b3b7c68ff0.webp

Use action on it , spawning 5 other dolls on the map, which you need to fire at. The dolls need to be shot in the following order.

- On top of a pile of debris, while leaving the building with the power switch
  Related image: images/source-guide/a20774c45b3186a193b9bc8d1e69e95a90bce1f6-10c98c0a9f.webp
- On a barrel, on your right when coming from Widow's wine area to the spawn. Near a Wunderfizz machine.
  Related image: images/source-guide/ff59876715738b48d5b1bc5e408768a41a94bc15-34eede46e3.webp
- Descending from the second pipe bridge, in front of Stamin-up
  Related image: images/source-guide/bd0099101d9e9ab4de7ec0eb0af2db8e62fc3683-2f9b9ba57e.webp
- In front of a crashed Lander, in the area with a Moon Lander location, and the TV used for the main quest.
  Related image: images/source-guide/d9e8a02d8eb0079606f45dbb88a753e7515289a7-5f193534d7.webp
- On top of the previous crashed Lander.
  Related image: images/source-guide/d3f2acaf88e3ba7f5f8497907399f0bc75bd16b4-59f782c34a.webp

Once done, head back to where you interacted with the doll, giving you the Max ammo and playing the secret Song.
