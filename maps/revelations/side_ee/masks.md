# masks

## overview

Revelations has several secret masks. They are collected through specific side challenges and can be equipped in the **Kino Der Toten theater** on the mannequins.

Each mask gives different bonuses. Some are simple, others are grindier.

This file covers:
- Dire Wolf mask
- Viking helmet
- Al's hat
- Keeper skull
- Helmet of the King
- Margwa head
- Fury head
- Apothicon mask

## dire wolf mask

The Dire Wolf mask is done in **Der Eisendrache**.

### steps
1. Go to Der Eisendrache.
2. Activate anti-gravity.
3. Throw a grenade into the hole used for the Wolf Bow skeleton step.
4. A skull falls out.
5. During anti-gravity, kill zombies around that skull.
6. Wait for the confirmation sound.
7. Go equip the mask in Kino.

### reward
- purple Stamin-Up effect
- increased stamina

## viking helmet

The Viking helmet is also called the **Helmet of Siegfried**.

### step 1 - enter 935
In the Der Eisendrache ritual area, there is a clock above the pyramid room. Shoot the clock to enter **935**:
- IX
- III
- V

A bell should ring.

### step 2 - fill the 4 jars
Now you must headshot **6 zombies near each of 4 jars**.

The jars are located:
- Der Eisendrache near the Primis statues
- Kino Der Toten in the projection room
- Verruckt in the kitchen
- Origins in a room before generator 3

### result
Once done, the helmet becomes available in Kino.

### reward
- purple Juggernog effect
- lets you take 1 extra hit

## al's hat

Al's Hat is found in an isolated cell in **Mob of the Dead** near a door leading toward the Origins trenches.

### effect
- no actual gameplay bonus
- mostly used to remove another mask you were wearing

## keeper skull head

This mask needs the **Keeper Protector**.

### steps
1. Summon the Keeper Protector.
2. Let him kill around **30 zombies**.
3. Wait for the confirmation sound.
4. Then either:
   - if you already unlocked the Wolf mask, the Keeper mask becomes available
   - otherwise, kill around **7 enslaved keepers** with corruption turrets

### reward
- purple Juggernog (+1 hit)
- 50% less damage from Enslaved Keepers
- 33% more damage to Enslaved Keepers
- Keeper Protector lasts 30 seconds longer

## helmet of the king

This mask is focused on boss resistance.

### steps
You need to:
1. Kill around **30 zombies with traps**
2. Destroy a **Panzersoldat helmet**
3. Destroy a **Panzersoldat power core** (yellow chest core)

### reward
- purple Stamin-Up
- 50% less boss damage taken
- 33% more damage to bosses
- no damage from electrified / burning zombies

## margwa head

This one is straightforward.

### steps
1. Kill each type of Margwa.
2. For each one, kill it by shooting all 3 heads properly.
3. Do this for the 3 Margwa variants.

### reward
- Margwa resistance / damage utility mask

## fury head

### steps
1. Get around **15 Fury headshots**.

### reward
- 33% less damage from Fury melee attacks
- 50% more damage to Furies
- purple Juggernog (+1 hit)

## apothicon mask

This is the strongest and grindiest mask.

You must kill enemies inside the **Apothicon stomach**, while:
- standing in the green bile in the middle
- and while the green mist is active

The mist stays for a few minutes, disappears, then comes back later.

### required kills
- 5 parasites
- 5 spiders
- 5 corrupted keepers
- 10 furies
- 50 zombies
- 3 margwas

### reward
- purple Stamin-Up
- purple Juggernog
- 50% more damage to all enemies
- 33% less damage taken from all enemies
