# civil protector

The Civil Protector is activated by collecting 3 blue fuses and inserting them into the fuse box in the Rift. Each fuse has 3 possible spawns, and they are always in the section between the perk area and the Tram / Train Station of each district.

## general structure

### Canals fuse
This fuse has 3 possible spawns in the section next to the train.

## steps

1. Open the relevant district train-side room / section.
2. Find all 3 fuses.
3. Go to the Rift.
4. Insert the 3 fuses into the fuse box.  
   Related image: images/civil-protector/undergroun.jpg
5. You can now call the Civil Protector from the summon panels.
