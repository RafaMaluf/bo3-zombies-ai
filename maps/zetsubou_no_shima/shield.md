# shield

## overview

The Shield on Zetsubou No Shima is built from 3 parts. After collecting all 3, you can craft it at a workbench in either **Lab A** or **Lab B**.

The 3 parts are found in:
- **Lab A lower area**
- **Lab B tree area**
- **AA gun balcony / bunker upper area**

## part 1 - Lab A lower area piece

The first shield part is found **under Lab A**, attached around the pillars / lower structure.

### possible locations
- beside the **Kuda**
- near the neighboring windows
- another nearby pillar position in the same lower Lab A area

The third shield part is found on the **AA gun balcony**, which is the first door on your right when entering the bunker upper area.

### possible locations

## crafting the shield

After collecting all 3 parts, go to a crafting table and build the Shield.

### crafting table locations
- **Lab A**
- **Lab B**

### steps
1. Collect the Lab A lower area piece.
2. Collect the Lab B tree area piece.
3. Collect the AA gun balcony piece.
4. Go to a workbench in Lab A or Lab B.
5. Build the Shield.

## practical note

The Shield is one of the most useful support items on the map. It is important not only for survival, but also for later systems such as:
- electrifying the shield after all trials are completed
- Masamune setup
- spider transformation
- some perk-discount interactions mentioned in side easter eggs
