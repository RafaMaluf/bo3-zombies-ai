# golden bucket

## overview

The Golden Bucket is a side easter egg upgrade on Zetsubou No Shima. Once obtained, the bucket no longer empties after watering plants, making the plant system much easier to manage.

To unlock it, you must:
- hatch one of every major plant type
- use the Skull of Nan Sapwe on the poster near the purple water
- use the Skull again under Lab B to reveal the special growing spot
- place your bucket there
- feed the spawned carnivorous plants with zombies
- collect the Golden Bucket from the final plant

## requirements

Before starting, you should have:
- a normal bucket
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- access to the plant system
- already grown one of each major plant type

## step 1 - reveal the special growing spot

Go under **Lab B** and use the Skull of Nan Sapwe on the roots blocking the growing spot.

### steps
1. Go under Lab B.
2. Find the roots blocking the special growing spot.
3. Use the Skull of Nan Sapwe to mesmerize / clear them.
4. Reveal the growing spot.

Related image: images/side_ees/goldenbuckerrootstomesmerize.jpg

## step 2 - place the bucket

Once the special spot is open, place your bucket there.

### steps
1. Stand at the newly revealed growing spot.
2. Interact with it while holding the bucket.
3. Place the bucket into the spot.

## step 3 - feed the plants

After placing the bucket, 3 growing spots appear. Plant seeds there. Carnivorous plants will grow by themselves and must be fed with zombies.

### steps
1. Plant seeds in the new growing spots.
2. Let the carnivorous plants grow.
3. Feed them zombies.
4. Continue until the final special plant appears where the bucket was placed.

## step 4 - collect the golden bucket

Once the final plant is ready, interact with it to obtain the **Golden Bucket**.

### result
- your bucket will no longer empty after watering plants
