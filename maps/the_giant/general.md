# general

The Giant is a remake of Der Riese and represents the simpler, more classic side of Black Ops 3 Zombies when compared to Shadows of Evil. The map has a much more straightforward structure, with a central facility layout, power activation, teleporter linking, and Pack-a-Punch access forming the core progression. Because of that, The Giant is usually treated as an easy map to learn, but it still contains a few important easter eggs and side features.

Unlike maps with many layered mechanics, The Giant is centered around fast setup, efficient movement, and survival. The main systems you care about are power, the 3 teleporters, Pack-a-Punch, and the Fly Trap / Paradoxical Prologue easter egg. Most of the map’s extra content is built around those systems rather than around completely separate mechanics.

Another important point is that the Wonderwaffe DG-2 is present on The Giant as a wonder weapon and can be obtained from the Mystery Box.

## Key map systems

- Power is required to fully activate the map and begin using the main systems.
- The 3 teleporters must be linked to the mainframe to unlock Pack-a-Punch.
- Pack-a-Punch is one of the core progression goals and is tied directly to the teleporter system.
- The main easter egg is a short quest based around the classic Fly Trap step and leads to the Annihilator reward.
- The Wonderwaffe DG-2 is available on the map and can be pulled from the Mystery Box.
- The map includes a musical easter egg and a few smaller side easter eggs.
- One important side easter egg allows you to reveal an extra perk by using Monkey Bombs and the giant robot event.
- Compared to other BO3 maps, The Giant is much more compact and less mechanic-heavy, making it useful both for casual play and for testing general systems in the app.
