# music 2

## overview

Revelations has a special **music radio** in Nacht Der Untoten.

It can play different Zombies songs.

To make the radio appear, you must press 4 buttons quickly.

Important note:
- the more players there are, the shorter the allowed time window

## button locations

- to the right of the Der Eisendrache portal when facing it
- in a corner near the Tesla coil interface
- in the corner upstairs near the Dragon Shield
- to the left of the LCAR-9

## steps

1. Go to Nacht Der Untoten.
2. Press the 4 buttons in a short time window.
3. The radio spawns beside the box.
4. Shoot the radio to start a song.
5. Knife the radio to stop the song.
