# shield

## overview

The Rocket Shield on Der Eisendrache is built from 3 parts. Once all 3 parts are collected, you can craft it at a workbench.

The 3 parts are:
- a double pipe piece from the first courtyard
- a metal plate with a griffin from the second courtyard / church side
- a frame piece from the underground anti-gravity room

There are 2 crafting tables:
- underground, near the pyramid
- above Double Tap

## part 1 - double pipe item

The first part is a double pipe item found around the **first courtyard**.

### possible locations
- on the crates, right after the entrance, on your right
- near the stairs, on the left after the entrance
- next to the fence that opens when you activate power

## part 2 - griffin plate

The second part is a metal plate with a griffin figure on it. It is located in the **second courtyard**, in front of the church.

### possible locations
- by the stairs coming from the clock tower
- near the entrance of the church
- in the clock tower, near a dead zombie

## part 3 - underground frame

The third part is a frame piece found in the **underground / pyramid room**. You must wait for the **anti-gravity** to activate, because this part spawns in elevated positions.

### possible locations
- on the underground decoration near the crafting table
- above the teleporter door
- above the Pack-a-Punch teleporter pad

## crafting the Rocket Shield

After collecting all 3 parts, go to a crafting table and build the Rocket Shield.

### crafting table locations
- underground, near the pyramid
- above Double Tap

### steps
1. Collect the double pipe item from the first courtyard.
2. Collect the griffin plate from the second courtyard / church side.
3. Wait for anti-gravity and collect the underground frame.
4. Go to a crafting table.
5. Build the Rocket Shield.

## practical note

The underground frame is usually the most annoying part because it depends on anti-gravity. The other 2 parts are simple location checks in their respective courtyards.
