# music 3 - music box

## overview

A third secret music easter egg can be triggered by interacting with a music box in Samantha's room. This plays a modified version of **Samantha's Lullaby**.

## steps

1. Go to Samantha's room.
2. Find the music box on the table next to her bed.
3. Interact with it.
4. The music starts playing.
