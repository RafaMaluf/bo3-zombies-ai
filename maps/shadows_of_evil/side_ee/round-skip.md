# round skip / start on round 5, 10, or 15

At the beginning of the match, shoot the Shadow Man above the map in spawn. This lets you force the round to jump to round 5, 10, or 15 while giving points immediately. This is useful for experienced players, but risky because you skip the low-round setup window.
