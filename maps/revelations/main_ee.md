# main ee

## overview

The Revelations main easter egg can be completed with **1 to 4 players**.

Average time:
- around **2 to 2 and a half hours**

The quest is long, but most of it is a sequence of object hunts, reel placements, rune steps, and one final boss sequence.

Before starting, it is highly recommended that you already have:
- **Pack-a-Punch unlocked**
- **Keeper Protector available**
- **upgraded Apothicon Servant**
- **Lil' Arnies**
- strong weapons and Gobblegums

## step 1 - shoot the graves in the death order

In spawn, there are 4 graves tied to the main characters.

The order is the order in which the characters died:
- **Richtofen**
- **Dempsey**
- **Takeo**
- **Nikolai**

That corresponds to:
- **2 - 3 - 1 - 4**

If done correctly:
- a sound plays
- red flames appear on the graves

### steps
1. Go to spawn.
2. Find the 4 graves.
3. Shoot them in the correct order: **2 - 3 - 1 - 4**
4. Listen for the confirmation sound.

## step 2 - keeper protector ritual for the first reel

For this step, you need:
- **Keeper Protector altar built**
- **5000 points**
- a decent weapon and/or shield

You must find the correct bumper pad that has the sharp triangular rock.

Then:
- summon the Keeper Protector
- bring him to the **opposite landing pad**
- ride over so you are on the side where the rock is
- move away from the pad so the Keeper follows you
- lead him close to the rock
- if positioned correctly, he starts the ritual

During this ritual, enemies will try to kill the Keeper. Protect him until he finishes.

When done, he drops the **first audio reel**.

Then place that reel on the reader in **Nacht Der Untoten**, beside Juggernog.

### steps
1. Find the correct bumper pad with the triangular stone.
2. Summon the Keeper Protector.
3. Bring him to the opposite landing pad.
4. Ride to the correct side.
5. Lead him toward the stone.
6. Protect him while he performs the ritual.
7. Pick up the first audio reel.
8. Place it on the audio reader in Nacht.

## step 3 - lil arnies holes inside the apothicon

For this step, you need **Lil' Arnies**.

You must throw Arnies into **9 holes** inside the Apothicon **without leaving the beast**.

Important note:
- if you leave the Apothicon, the whole step resets

Every set of 3 completed holes spawns **3 Margwas**.
Kill them before moving on to the next set.

After all 9 holes and 9 Margwas are done, pick up the **second audio reel**.

Then place it in **Kino Der Toten** on the reader near the main desk.

### steps
1. Enter the Apothicon.
2. Throw Arnies into the 9 holes without leaving.
3. After each group of 3 holes, kill the spawned Margwas.
4. After all 9 are done, pick up the second reel.
5. Go to Kino.
6. Place the reel in the reader.

## step 4 - collect the 6 bones and create the third reel

For this step, you need the **upgraded Apothicon Servant**.

Each bone starts as a brick / object that you shoot down with a **Pack-a-Punched weapon**. Then a floating bone appears, and you must pull it in using the upgraded Apothicon Servant shot.

The 6 bone locations are:

- Nacht Der Untoten, above the gateway leading to Der Eisendrache
- near the waterfall around Verruckt
- near the giant robot footstep in Origins
- above Stamin-Up in Shangri-La
- in the broken church in spawn
- part of the wall tied to the free perk route

After all 6 are collected, the bones appear on the floor in **Nacht Der Untoten**, between the reel player and Juggernog.

Shoot them with the upgraded Apothicon Servant:
- first they become a body
- shoot again and they become the **third audio reel**

Then place that reel in **Origins**, on the table at the excavation site.

### steps
1. Go to each bone location.
2. Shoot the object down with a Pack-a-Punched weapon.
3. Use the upgraded Apothicon Servant to pull in the flying bone.
4. After collecting all 6, go to Nacht.
5. Shoot the bone pile with the upgraded Servant.
6. Shoot the body again to get the third reel.
7. Take it to Origins and place it on the reel player.

## step 5 - shoot s.o.p.h.i.a with the corruption turrets

After the previous reel step, **S.O.P.H.I.A** should appear above Nacht Der Untoten.

You must use the **4 corruption turrets** to shoot her, but not with a direct beam. You have to deflect the shot using the blue crystal near each portal.

When done correctly, the turret kicks you out automatically.

After all required shots are completed, go back to Nacht and interact with S.O.P.H.I.A. She then materializes and heads to the Kino teleporter.

### steps
1. Go to the corruption turrets.
2. Use each turret.
3. Aim at the blue crystal near the portal to deflect the beam toward S.O.P.H.I.A.
4. Repeat until the step is done.
5. Return to Nacht.
6. Interact with S.O.P.H.I.A.

## step 6 - samantha's room and the kronorium

Once S.O.P.H.I.A is waiting by the Kino teleporter, all players should stand in the teleporter.

You get sent to **Samantha's room**.

Take the **Kronorium** from the bed, then wait to be teleported away.

After returning, place the Kronorium on the desk in **Kino Der Toten**.

### steps
1. Go to the Kino teleporter.
2. Have all players stand inside.
3. In Samantha's room, pick up the Kronorium.
4. Wait to return.
5. Place the Kronorium on the Kino desk.

## step 7 - eggs, worms, and invisible runes

Now you must find **4 eggs** around the map.

Important notes:
- only **one egg** is on the map at a time
- after finding one, place it into one of the Apothicon holes
- kill zombies nearby to hatch it into a worm
- pick up the worm
- then place the worm at the invisible rune in the corresponding area

Egg locations can be in:
- spawn
- Origins
- Mob of the Dead
- Shangri-La
- Der Eisendrache
- Kino
- Verruckt

Once the egg is placed in the Apothicon:
- kill zombies nearby
- souls go into the egg
- it hatches into a worm

Then take the worm to one of these 4 zones:
- spawn
- Der Eisendrache
- Verruckt
- Mob of the Dead

You must search for an invisible rune.
You know you are close because the beep gets faster.

When found:
- interact
- place the worm
- it turns into a rune
- pick it back up

Repeat until all 4 are done.

### steps
1. Find one egg.
2. Bring it to the Apothicon.
3. Place it in the correct hole.
4. Kill zombies until it hatches.
5. Pick up the worm.
6. Go to the correct area.
7. Find the invisible rune by following the beeping.
8. Place the worm and pick up the finished rune.
9. Repeat for all 4 worms.

## step 8 - boss room rune order and summoning key

Go to the projection room in **Kino Der Toten** and stand on the circle. You get teleported to the boss room.

There is a base with the Kronorium on it. Interact with it to reveal the order of the 4 runes.

You must memorize the order, then go to the opposite side of the room where a floating purple symbol appears and changes repeatedly.

Each time the correct symbol appears, interact with it.

After this, 4 survival phases begin. You must survive each one, killing Margwas and avoiding hazards.

Eventually, you can pick up the **Summoning Key**.

### steps
1. Go to the Kino projection room.
2. Stand on the circle and teleport.
3. Interact with the Kronorium pedestal.
4. Memorize the 4-rune order.
5. Go to the floating purple symbol area.
6. Interact only when the correct symbol appears.
7. Survive the following boss phases.
8. Pick up the Summoning Key.

## step 9 - throw the summoning key at the 7 hidden objects

Now you must throw the Summoning Key at 7 out-of-map objects.

The 7 targets are:
- radio on the chandelier in Kino
- clock above Der Eisendrache
- convergence stone in a palm tree in Shangri-La
- grave around the excavation site in Origins
- poster in a cell in Mob of the Dead
- MG-42 on the fountain around Verruckt
- red barrel near a truck in Nacht Der Untoten

Important note:
- if you miss, the key falls near where you were standing
- if the key glitches when using a portal, the player who lost it should **go down / down himself**
- the key will reappear next to that player

### steps
1. Take the Summoning Key.
2. Go to each target area.
3. Throw the key at the 7 hidden objects.
4. Pick it back up if needed after each throw.
5. If it disappears due to a portal glitch, down the player who lost it so it respawns next to them.

## step 10 - final shadowman fight

Prepare fully before starting:
- weapons
- perks
- Gobblegums
- ammo

Go to the teleporter in Kino to return to the boss room.

Place the Summoning Key on the altar / pedestal, then kill zombies to charge it.

Once charged:
- throw the key at **S.O.P.H.I.A**
- this breaks the Shadowman shield
- shoot the Shadowman until he is pulled into the Apothicon mouth
- interact with the Kronorium to finish the easter egg

### steps
1. Return to the boss room via Kino teleporter.
2. Place the Summoning Key on the altar.
3. Kill zombies to charge it.
4. Throw the key at S.O.P.H.I.A.
5. Shoot the Shadowman until he is trapped in the Apothicon mouth.
6. Interact with the Kronorium to finish.

## full progression summary

1. Shoot the 4 graves in the death order.
2. Do the Keeper Protector ritual and get the first reel.
3. Complete the Lil' Arnies hole step in the Apothicon and get the second reel.
4. Collect all 6 bones and create the third reel.
5. Shoot S.O.P.H.I.A with the corruption turrets.
6. Go to Samantha's room and retrieve the Kronorium.
7. Find the 4 eggs, hatch them, and convert the worms into runes.
8. Enter the boss room and complete the rune order phase.
9. Pick up the Summoning Key.
10. Throw the key at the 7 hidden map objects.
11. Return for the final Shadowman fight.
12. Trap him in the Apothicon mouth and finish the easter egg.
