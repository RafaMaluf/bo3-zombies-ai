# Samantha hide and seek

## Side easter egg - Samantha Hide and Seek

There are 8 gongs on the map and you will have to knife 4 of them. To know which gongs are the right ones, you will have to randomly knife them, the sounds will slightly change depending on the fact it's the right gong or not. To know where are the gongs, check the main easter egg part 2, step 9.

Once done, no sounds will be notified to you. Head now underground, after the tunnel, down the water slide. There will be a wooden wall with 5 pans on it.

You will need to shoot 2 times the first pan, then one time the fifth pan. A samantha sound should play.

Related image: images/source-guide/4a2422eb6571a432f85000757e9a939dfe03d441-c5048b910c.webp

A samantha doll will be laying on the ground near the minecart entrance. Interact with it will begin the hide and seek.

Related image: images/source-guide/28bf9cf7c703d7e425f221b68e569acb197f061e-53fde04ddf.webp

- The first will be down the minecart entrance, behind the grid
  Related image: images/source-guide/dd21751530ab5154f4af53d4ec2c9af32aa558f0-ff3d6d5366.webp
- The second one will be laying down a lantern on the minecart track. You can see it from the power room
  Related image: images/source-guide/7b21f8d156fffec1f54c1d2c7511cdbef465aee5-3cc4cda65d.webp
- The third one will be laying down from the top of the tunnel in the obscurity
  Related image: images/source-guide/ab6c92ae382088fd2c2a6851ac83a2d58c2a39f1-f4f49ce8e1.webp
- The fourth doll will be up the minecart exit, visible from the waterfall pool
  Related image: images/source-guide/7d002c809ff1a56b1d15c71213ec1ab899ed21f9-53f86c2e29.webp
- The last doll will be on top of the rock in the waterfall pool
  Related image: images/source-guide/079131bc23d72822da85e29b2dc88f0f49fee6fb-002f918479.webp

Then, simply head back where you activated the hide and seek, to get your max ammo.
