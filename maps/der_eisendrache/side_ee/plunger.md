# plunger

## overview

A hidden melee weapon, the Plunger, can be obtained on Der Eisendrache. It can also temporarily become a flaming one-hit Panzer weapon after you kill a Panzer.

To get the Plunger, you must first destroy the Fire Bow quest wall in the Clock Tower, then use the Ragnarok DG-4 and the clock step to open the time-travel Plunger pickup.

## step 1 - prepare the clock step

Before doing the clock step, make sure the Fire Bow quest wall in the Clock Tower has already been destroyed.

Then place the Ragnarok DG-4 in the required location.

Related image: images/side_ees/9plungerragnarockclock.jpg

## step 2 - activate the clock

Go downstairs and interact with the small lever next to the door that leads to a Wundersphere landing point. This starts the clock ticking.

You must stop the clock at **9:35**. If done correctly, it will stop on **9:36**.

### practical tip
In co-op this is easier. In solo, many players stop it around 9:00 to 9:15, restart it, then count the remaining seconds carefully.

Related images:
- images/side_ees/10plungerswitch.jpg
- images/side_ees/11plungerclockat935.jpg

## step 3 - check for the energy ball

After the clock is done correctly, go downstairs to the pyramid room. A glowing energy ball should now be above the pyramid.

Related image: images/side_ees/12plungerenergyball.jpg

## step 4 - wall-run into the past

From the stairs going down into the pyramid room, wall-run on the symbol to your right. If the setup was done correctly, you will eventually be teleported back in time.

Related image: images/side_ees/13plungerwheretorun.jpg

## step 5 - pick up the Plunger

Once in the past, pick up the Plunger from the table to the left of the teleporter.

Related image: images/side_ees/14plungerinpast.jpg

## flaming plunger

The Plunger can temporarily upgrade into a flaming version.

### steps
1. Obtain the Plunger.
2. Kill a Panzer by any method.
3. Your Plunger becomes flaming for about 4 minutes.
4. During that time, it one-shots Panzers.

## full progression summary

1. Destroy the Fire Bow quest wall in the Clock Tower.
2. Place the Ragnarok DG-4 in the required spot.
3. Interact with the lever near the Wundersphere landing point.
4. Stop the clock at 9:35 / 9:36.
5. Go to the pyramid room and confirm the energy ball appears.
6. Wall-run on the correct symbol near the stairs.
7. Get sent to the past.
8. Pick up the Plunger from the table.
9. Kill a Panzer later to temporarily turn it into the flaming Plunger.
