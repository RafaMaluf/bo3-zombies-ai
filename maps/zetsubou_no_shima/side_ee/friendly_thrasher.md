# friendly thrasher

## overview

You can create a friendly Thrasher / transformed zombie on Zetsubou No Shima that helps attack enemies.

To do this, you need:
- the **Masamune**
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- to have already used the Skull on the poster and walls during the related hidden wall setup

## steps

1. Go to the green tanks room.
2. Return to the wall that was previously vanished / revealed with the Skull.
3. Shoot the roots there with the **Masamune**.
4. This reveals a spore.
5. Wait until a normal zombie is near the spore.
6. Shoot the spore.
7. The zombie transforms into a friendly Thrasher with blue eyes.

### result
- the friendly Thrasher attacks enemies for you
- it only lasts for a limited time
