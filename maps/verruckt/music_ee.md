# Lullaby for a Dead Man

## Side easter egg - Secret song

You can listen to Lullaby for a Dead Man on the map.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDtDKuOGE40

To do so, head to the bathroom next to the power room. Simply interact three times in front of the toilet on the left.
