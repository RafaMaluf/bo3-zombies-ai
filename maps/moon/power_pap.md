# Power, Area 51 teleporter and Pack-a-Punch

## Preparation - Activating the Power - Accessing Pack-a-Punch

At the beginning of your game, you will find yourself in the middle of Area 51, Groom Lake, Earth. The Pack-a-Punch is right behind you, it is directly available if you get enough points.

Related image: images/source-guide/6cbcb03c97830dbe36c43a278ff7a00545f4e9e5-fc29298246.webp

Your life is going to be very difficulty in the No Man's Land of Earth so, wait a couple of seconds after the alarm sounds, then head to the teleporter . Everybody needs to stand on it in order to get to the Moon.

Related image: images/source-guide/027d6d6bed7b82029a84e009e9bc2595b4d8c529-a5ab1c1776.webp

Don't forget to wear the space suit.

Related image: images/source-guide/6150c01bc43de0c2af291b5d692197e2057ca210-ee412a5441.webp

Proceed onto the base , after 4 doors you should arrive in a cavern with the MPD. (The pyramid thing) The power lever is located in front of it, on the opposite side.

Related image: images/source-guide/c70e1df8bf57bed9e1642fafad9cd95b6f9a1ec0-766ab79caa.webp

Now that the power is on, you need to open doors again to reach the outside of the base . A circular hole is located above the MPD's cavern. This is a teleporter you can access only from above. Once all players are on it, you will be sent back to Earth, in front of the Pack-a-Punch.

Related image: images/source-guide/de2a38473b29c5194f57ab835a6feaece8b1ef3d-243c8bee63.webp

Note that it is possible with the hacking tool to close temporarly the doors of the PaP.
