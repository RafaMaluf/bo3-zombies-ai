# Margwa head / mask

After 6 Margwas have been killed in the match, 6 Margwa Hearts will spawn on signs and billboards around the map. All 6 hearts must be shot while riding the Tram.

## Recommended route
- Waterfront to Footlight
- Footlight to Canals

You have a maximum of 2 Tram rides to hit all 6 hearts. If you take more than 2 rides, all hearts reset and you have to try again.

A small Margwa growl plays each time a heart is hit correctly.

## Margwa Heart locations
1. Leaving Waterfront, left side: above the M8A7 wallbuy, on the blue “Morg City Gas & Electric” sign.
2. Leaving Waterfront, left side: on the “Watermelon” poster on The Connoy building.
3. Junction area, left side if going Waterfront to Footlight: on the top-left side of the “Devil O Donuts” sign.
4. Leaving Footlight, right side: on the “Coffee” neon sign, below the “Hotel” sign.
5. Leaving Footlight, right side: on the “H” of the white Theater sign.
6. Leaving Canals, left side: on the “Y” of the Ruby Rabbit sign.

Once all 6 hearts are shot correctly, a Margwa death audio cue will play.

## Finding the Margwa Head
The Margwa Head itself is invisible and appears only as an intermittently shining white light with a pickup icon. Its location is randomized each game.

### Possible pickup locations
- Ruby Rabbit, top floor balcony area
- Nero’s Landing, underneath the staircase on the wooden table
- Boxing Gym, near the lockers in the back center of the building
- Black Lace Burlesque, on the left side of the stage near an overturned chair

## Reward
The Margwa Head reduces damage from Margwa slam attacks.
