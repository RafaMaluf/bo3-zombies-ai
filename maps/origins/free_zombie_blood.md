# Free Zombie Blood

## Side easter egg - Free Zombie Blood

Generally for the main easter egg quest, or simply in the game, a zombie blood is always usefull.

You can simply generate one on top of the excavation site by extinguishing 3 burning wooden cart in the center of the map, with the ice staff.

- Near the MP40 wallbuy, coming from the workshop bunker
  Related image: images/source-guide/addbd6dee30533da7d61a0a869a8989227bf9b39-33170657c6.webp
- Around the left path leading to generator 5
  Related image: images/source-guide/a342631ac5a48e05274b38f355844f8b37a701df-c5cbca35f7.webp
- Near an Odin's footstep, between the site and generator 4
  Related image: images/source-guide/bad8b888aacecfce85f6980a91df4acb6bc1eb87-4ebafe6fff.webp

If you were quick enough, a Zombie blood will spawn in a corner on the excavation site. If not, it means the fire lighted up again, just try again faster.
