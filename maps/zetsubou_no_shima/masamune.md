    # masamune

## overview

The **Masamune** is the upgraded form of the KT-4. To build it, you need:

- **Spider Tooth**
- **Rainbow Water plant**
- **Element 115 flask**
- the **KT-4**
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- the **Shield**

Important note:
- the rainbow water system has already been covered in `buckets_water.md`
- the 4 mesmerize wall locations shown here are **not really Masamune steps by themselves**
- only the **last mesmerize location** is directly tied to the Masamune plant step
- the other mesmerize wall spots are useful because the guide mixes them into the same general setup flow, but they are more broadly connected to the map / easter egg progression

## part 1 - spider tooth

To get the Spider Tooth, go to the blue water source area. Behind it there is a large spider web. Shoot that web with the **KT-4**.

This starts a mini-boss fight against the giant spider.

### steps
1. Go to the blue water area.
2. Find the big spider web behind it.
3. Shoot the web with the KT-4.
4. Enter the boss arena.
5. Fight the giant spider.
6. Aim for the mouth when it glows yellow.
7. After killing her, go through the opened path behind her.
8. Pick up one of the spider's teeth.

### practical note
This is much easier with:
- Pack-a-Punched weapons
- perks
- solid ammo
- a stable setup

## part 2 - rainbow water plant

For this part, you need:
- an **empty bucket**
- **rainbow water**
- a **seed**
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**

Rainbow water collection itself was already explained in `buckets_water.md`.

### mesmerize wall locations

Using the Skull of Nan Sapwe in hypnosis / mesmerize mode reveals hidden wall sections. The guide mixes several of these into the same setup chain.

The 4 shown wall spots are:

Important note:
- the **last** mesmerize location is the one that matters directly for the Masamune plant step
- that last wall reveals the underwater grow spot used for the rainbow water plant

### actual plant step
1. Collect rainbow water in an empty bucket.
2. Go to the underwater grow spot revealed by the correct mesmerize wall.
3. Plant a seed there.
4. Water it across 3 rounds using rainbow water.
5. Wait for the plant to fully grow.
6. Pick up the item from the plant.

## part 3 - Element 115 flask

For this step, you need an **electrified shield**.

To electrify the shield:
- **all players** must complete **all 3 of their trials**
- once that is done, lightning starts striking near the main altar area
- when lightning falls, quickly pull out the shield and let it get electrified

Important note:
- if not everyone has completed their 3 trials, this step will not work

### steps
1. Make sure all players have completed all 3 trials.
2. Equip the shield.
3. Wait near the main altar until lightning strikes.
4. Let the shield get electrified.
5. Go to Lab B.
6. Hit the correct panel with the electrified shield.
7. Enter the opened cage.
8. In solo, the cage moves by itself.
9. In co-op, another player may need to activate the panel.
10. Once the cage descends, pick up the Element 115 flask underground.

## crafting the Masamune

After collecting:
- Spider Tooth
- rainbow water plant item
- Element 115 flask

go back to the crafting room where the KT-4 is assembled. There is another table there for the upgrade.

### steps
1. Build the KT-4 first.
2. Collect the Spider Tooth.
3. Collect the rainbow water plant item.
4. Collect the Element 115 flask.
5. Return to the upgrade table.
6. Craft the **Masamune**.

Related image:
- images/masamune/14masamuneinhands.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Build the KT-4.
2. Shoot the giant spider web with the KT-4.
3. Kill the giant spider and collect the Spider Tooth.
4. Collect rainbow water.
5. Reveal and use the correct underwater plant grow spot.
6. Grow the required plant with rainbow water.
7. Pick up the plant item.
8. Complete all trials for all players.
9. Electrify the shield with lightning near the altar.
10. Hit the Lab B panel with the electrified shield.
11. Enter the cage and collect the Element 115 flask.
12. Return to the upgrade table and craft the Masamune.
