# G-Strike

## Equipment - G-Strike

To get the G-strike equipement, which act like the dragon strike from Gorod krovi, you will have to obtain and clean a stone tile. It is located in the Tank station near generator 2. On a table stands 4 stones tiles with a fist on it. Pick one up.

Related image: images/source-guide/1ffcd38464e031ef6a1534c41b2b2d5550e442a5-5fbe481c51.webp

Go now to the church, on the first floor there is a bassin with water where you need to place your stone.

Related image: images/source-guide/67ba78c8a4805713b10212697a0eb36e62f8fbc8-13058da704.webp

You will be asked to do melee kills on zombies, either with knife, upgraded staff, shield, or One inch punch. (See part bellow)

Related image: images/source-guide/6791408f7925d63c0f6f4558905743116b5e25c4-e2b9d69a44.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/a4273e97a8f1f34e1b2e51657b1815a14595b64c-9860589f0b.webp

Once placed back, melee kill zombies again until it's done. You will see a glowing G-strike equipment hovering above the table.

Related image: images/source-guide/981fa35c454845ec513b91e286196045c24e1e7e-7b3a0ab689.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/d4720dded4cc42b6cca10ac912dddd6fbe6e8d50-3230a7bb58.webp

Doing this grants the G-Strike only for this player. If this player exchange the g-strike for monkey bombs, g-strike will be available from the mystery box.
