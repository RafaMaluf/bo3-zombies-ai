# panzer helmet

## overview

You can unlock the Panzer Soldat Helmet on Der Eisendrache. This reduces the damage you take from Panzers.

To get it, you must kill 3 Panzers with hidden metal claws located on the ceiling in 3 different parts of the map. You shoot the claw to make it drop, then shoot it again when a Panzer is in front of it so the claw launches forward and kills the Panzer.

## claw locations

- on the wooden balcony near the first dragon
- in the armor corridor, in the right corner
- in the stone tunnel beside Quick Revive

## steps

1. Find one of the hidden claws on the ceiling.
2. Shoot it once so it drops down.
3. Wait until a Panzer moves in front of it.
4. Shoot the claw again so it launches forward and kills the Panzer.
5. Repeat this with all 3 claw locations.
6. After the 3 Panzer claw kills, the helmet is unlocked.
