# buckets + water

## overview

Buckets and water are core systems on Zetsubou No Shima. You need a bucket to:
- fill the Lab A and Lab B generators
- water seeds
- work with several special plant outcomes
- collect rainbow water for specific advanced steps

A bucket can hold **3 charges of water**, which means you can:
- water 3 seeds in the same round
- or keep enough water to use the same seed over multiple rounds

## bucket locations

Buckets can be found throughout the map. There are many total spawn points, so this file focuses on the system rather than listing every exact location.

The main bucket areas are:
- spawn
- Lab A
- Lab B
- main bunker

## how watering works

By killing zombies, you can get **seeds**. These can be planted in growing spots around the map.

Once planted, you can water a seed **once per round**.

Important notes:
- if a seed is **not watered for a round**, it still progresses on its own
- watering a seed with different colors affects what kind of plant it becomes
- later, the KT-4 / Masamune can also improve plant outcomes, but that belongs more to the plant system itself

## rainbow water

Rainbow water is a special water type used for advanced plant progression, especially for specific quest-related plant outcomes.

To collect rainbow water:
1. go to the sewer tunnel near where the KT-4 is crafted
2. pay **500 points** to use the underwater tube / sewer route
3. while moving through the tunnel, interact near the **115 meteor**
4. you must have an **empty bucket**
5. if done correctly, your bucket is filled with rainbow water

The easiest method is usually to spam the interact button while moving through the tunnel.

## practical note

Buckets and water are one of the main reasons Zetsubou No Shima feels more complex than other maps. If you understand:
- where to get a bucket
- where each color water source is
- and how rainbow water is collected

then the rest of the map becomes much easier to organize.
