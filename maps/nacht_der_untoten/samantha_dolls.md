# Samantha dolls

## Side easter egg - Samantha's dolls

Zombies chronicles meta, you can get a free Max ammo as well as listening to Samantha's Lullaby.

In order to begin, you will need to activate 4 buttons around the map. They are :

- On side of a pillar, on the left of Mule Kick
  Related image: images/source-guide/33ed050c8a592dfee09dd59887fa7d3039bfbef8-95f7c61f72.webp
- On the roof, above the main stairs with "You must ascend from darkness" inscription.
  Related image: images/source-guide/737c66e52d9c82660912843f005dd8ee18d3246a-0b14e3399d.webp
- Under a shelf, facing the stairs near the Mystery box spot.
  Related image: images/source-guide/b68c399a482e1f6f9fcb5e161461b32e05794353-0bb587d72e.webp
- On a wall, on the far right, when coming from the main stairs.
  Related image: images/source-guide/f96609551d0fd3e2e5e9254ec7da3431e9332488-a0c7c60693.webp

Head back to the main room, a doll of Samantha should be laying on the ground, in front of the RK5.

Related image: images/source-guide/a7c8dd752918b182d4771682e0f8d0f0b4b1c8d4-93436d0590.webp

Interacting with it will make spawn 8 other dolls around the map which you need to shoot down .

- On an electrical pole, facing the cabinet holding the Locus
  Related image: images/source-guide/968587410596679171ed62254ef869fd92b77d72-69b8ffb330.webp
- On the ground, outside the map, on the opposite side of the building, not facing the moon. Visible from a barricade on the spawn.
  Related image: images/source-guide/41060454dd22bef8723e0348125fbe589e70529f-34c697a00e.webp
- On a truck, near a window revealing you the Moon, in the spawn.
  Related image: images/source-guide/2b8aa8c7b82f133b1e7986cbc6a5c119d7640d0c-0fb57e0d26.webp
- On a fence, outside the map, visible from the furthest window from your spawn, in the first room.
  Related image: images/source-guide/61e797cb484a275e6b548899f48958cf0279f368-1c5d72a093.webp
- On a Tank's cannon, spotted from the window near Mule Kick
  Related image: images/source-guide/ac8e49024da4bf18558cfb05a3381037094dcb70-a5a2539556.webp
- Near a power generator, on the first window on the right, after the main stairs.
  Related image: images/source-guide/e1392c8f44a13d65b1a6cb8b214db660a83c7b45-f8a640b2b2.webp
- On a hole in the roof, above a shelf in the main room.
  Related image: images/source-guide/be694169187eb19a74f47d8b96e07c916cff2e3e-dd114f7ff2.webp
- On a hole in the roof, near the door between main and smaller stairs, in the first floor.
  Related image: images/source-guide/d36093d1e93aec42e83aa03a01ce50eb9aeca8a4-68ac01be67.webp

Once done, a sound should be heard. Head back where you found the previous doll in front of the RK5 and press action. There goes your reward !
