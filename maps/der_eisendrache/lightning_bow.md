# lightning bow

## overview

The Lightning Bow upgrade turns the Wrath of the Ancients into **Kreema'ahm la Ahmahm**. This is usually considered the strongest bow on Der Eisendrache because of its powerful charged shots and area control.

The upgrade flow is:
1. shoot the weathercock to get the broken arrow
2. light the 3 bonfires / wood piles with charged arrows
3. activate the 5 blue wall symbols during anti-gravity
4. charge the 3 electric jars with zombie kills
5. electrify your arrows with the jars and shoot the 3 lit bonfires
6. interact with the blue cloud near the clock tower
7. place the rebuilt arrow on the altar in the pyramid room
8. charge it with zombie souls and claim the upgraded bow

## step 1 - start the quest

To get the broken arrow, shoot the weathercock on top of the castle.

### steps
1. Obtain the base bow, **Wrath of the Ancients**.
2. Go where you can see the weathercock on top of the castle.
3. Shoot the weathercock with the bow.
4. Pick up the broken arrow that drops.

## step 2 - light the 3 bonfires

You must ignite 3 wood piles / bonfires with charged shots from the bow.

### bonfire locations
- one in the landscape of the town below, on a hill
- one on the castle rampart, outside the map, visible from outside the clock tower on your right
- one on a hill above you when you are at the landing pad, on your right

### steps
1. Fully charge the bow.
2. Shoot the first bonfire until it lights up.
3. Repeat for the second bonfire.
4. Repeat for the third bonfire.

## step 3 - activate the 5 blue wall symbols

There are 5 blue symbols on the cavern walls in the underground / pyramid room area.

You must wait for anti-gravity to activate, then wall-run across all 5 symbols without touching the ground. If done correctly, they remain blue after gravity returns to normal.

### steps
1. Go to the underground / pyramid room.
2. Wait for anti-gravity to activate.
3. Wall-run across all 5 blue symbols in one sequence without touching the ground.
4. Confirm they remain blue after anti-gravity ends.

## step 4 - charge the 3 electric jars

You now need to kill 6 zombies next to each of the 3 electric jars.

### jar locations
- one in the clock tower
- one in the room above Double Tap
- one in the tunnel that leads from rocket to the landing pad

### steps
1. Go to the first jar.
2. Kill 6 zombies near it.
3. Repeat for the second jar.
4. Repeat for the third jar.

## step 5 - electrify your arrows and shoot the bonfires again

After the jars are charged, you must electrify your arrows and use them on the 3 bonfires you previously lit.

To electrify an arrow:
- aim with your bow at an electrified jar

Important notes:
- you cannot use the same jar twice
- if you stop aiming, the arrow is no longer electrified

### steps
1. Aim at one charged electric jar to electrify your arrow.
2. Keep aiming and shoot one of the lit bonfires.
3. Use a second charged jar to electrify another arrow.
4. Shoot the second lit bonfire.
5. Use the third charged jar.
6. Shoot the third lit bonfire.

Related image:
- images/lightning_bow/12woodshedlitupandelectrified.jpg

## step 6 - interact with the blue cloud

After all previous steps are completed, the weathercock on top of the castle becomes electrified and starts moving.

Go to the bottom of that tower and interact with the blue cloud.

### steps
1. Return to the clock tower / weathercock area.
2. Find the blue cloud at the bottom of the tower.
3. Interact with it.
4. Pick up the rebuilt arrow.

Related images:
- images/lightning_bow/13cloudedtointeract.jpg
- images/lightning_bow/14arrowrebuilt.jpg

## step 7 - place the arrow on the altar

Take the rebuilt arrow to the corresponding altar in the pyramid room.

### steps
1. Go back to the altar room underground.
2. Place the rebuilt arrow on the Lightning Bow altar.

## step 8 - charge the altar and claim the bow

Kill zombies near the altar until it is fully charged. Then interact with the arrow after the animation finishes.

### steps
1. Kill zombies near the altar.
2. Wait until the altar is fully charged.
3. Interact with the arrow.
4. Wait for the short animation to finish.
5. Pick up **Kreema'ahm la Ahmahm**.

## full progression summary

1. Shoot the weathercock to get the broken arrow.
2. Light the 3 bonfires with charged arrows.
3. Activate the 5 blue symbols during anti-gravity.
4. Kill 6 zombies near each of the 3 electric jars.
5. Electrify your arrows with the jars and shoot the 3 lit bonfires.
6. Interact with the blue cloud near the clock tower.
7. Pick up the rebuilt arrow.
8. Place it on the Lightning Bow altar in the pyramid room.
9. Charge the altar with zombie souls.
10. Interact and claim the upgraded Lightning Bow.
