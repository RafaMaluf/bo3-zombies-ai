# Kino der Toten achievement

## Achievement

I've seen some things...

Difficulty: Easy

Simply use the teleporter multiple times. You will need to visit Samantha's room, Samantha's corrupted room, the surgery room and "Five" Pentagon's office. Once teleporting sequence is done, you will need to relink it.
