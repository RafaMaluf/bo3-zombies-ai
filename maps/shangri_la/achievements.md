# Shangri-La achievements

## Achievements

Time Travel Will Tell:

Difficulty: Moderate to Hard

Complete the main easter egg quest. See the guide above. :3

Small Consolation:

DIfficulty: Easy

You must acquire the Wonder Weapon of the map in the mystery box (31-79 JGb215) and use it to shrink a regular zombie, a Napalm zombie, a Shrieker zombie and a zombie monkey. Both napalm and shrieker zombies will normally spawn in some rounds. The monkeys will come for you when a bonus is on the ground.
