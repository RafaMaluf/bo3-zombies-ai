# fire bow

## overview

The Fire Bow upgrade turns the Wrath of the Ancients into **Kreeaho'ahm nal Ahmhogaroc**. This upgrade is one of the more annoying ones because it mixes multiple different mechanics:
- starting the quest from the wall in the Clock Tower
- waiting for a rocket test and shooting the glowing fireball
- hitting 3 red circles while using the Wunderspheres
- charging those circles by killing zombies while standing inside them
- using the clock to identify the correct fireplace
- moving the blazing rock from the active circle to the correct fireplace
- collecting the reforged arrow
- charging it at the fire altar in the pyramid room

## step 1 - start the quest

To begin the Fire Bow quest, go to the **top of the Clock Tower** and use a **charged shot** on the marked wall. This breaks the wall and releases the quest item for the Fire Bow path.

### steps
1. Obtain the base bow, **Wrath of the Ancients**.
2. Go to the top of the Clock Tower.
3. Shoot a **charged arrow** at the marked wall.
4. Interact with the revealed Fire Bow quest item.

## step 2 - rocket test site fireball

After choosing the Fire Bow, go to the **rocket test site**  and wait for a rocket test to begin. When the countdown starts, stay inside the safe teleporter room. As soon as the test ends and the doors open, run outside and quickly shoot the glowing fireball on the mountainside / above the concrete structure. If you miss it, you must wait for the next rocket test. 

### steps
1. Go to the landing pad / rocket test site.
2. Wait for a rocket test countdown.
3. Stay safe inside until the test ends.
4. As soon as the doors reopen, run outside.
5. Look to the right and shoot the glowing fireball with the bow.
6. If done correctly, the fireball flies back toward the castle and a giant sun appears in the sky.

## step 3 - shoot the 3 red circles while in the air

The next step is to hit **3 red circles with charged arrows while using the Wunderspheres**. They are intended to be shot while you are airborne. The guide’s recommended routing is:
- courtyard Wundersphere for the Death Ray circle
- room above Double Tap for the near-Clock-Tower circle
- Death Ray / landing pad Wundersphere for the remaining one

### circle locations
- near the **Death Ray**, in front of the Wundersphere
- near the **Wunderfizz / Clock Tower side**
- in the **upward alley near Double Tap**, by the car

### steps
1. Use a Wundersphere.
2. While airborne, shoot the glowing red circle with a **charged** arrow.
3. Repeat until all 3 circles have been hit.

## step 4 - charge the circles with zombie kills

After all 3 circles have been activated, you must **stand inside each circle** and kill zombies. The kills themselves do not have to happen inside the circle, but **you** must be standing in the circle when they die. When the step fills correctly, a beam rises toward the sun and you hear a sound cue. Do this for all 3 circles. 

### steps
1. Go to one activated circle.
2. Stand inside it.
3. Kill zombies while staying inside the circle.
4. Wait for the sound cue / beam to the sun.
5. Repeat for the other 2 circles.

## step 5 - use the clock

Now go to the **Clock Tower** and interact with the clock. One symbol will be highlighted. That symbol tells you which fireplace is the correct destination for the next step. 

Related image:
- images/fire_bow/clock_with_symbol.jpg

## step 6 - move the blazing rock to the correct fireplace

After using the clock, **one circle will glow**. From that active circle, shoot a charged arrow at the ground to create a **blazing rock**, similar to a vulcan. Then move that rock toward the correct fireplace by:
1. spawning it from the active circle
2. standing on the rock and shooting again to move it (your screen will get a red glow to show you are close enough)
3. repeating until it reaches the correct fireplace

Once the blazing rock reaches the correct fireplace, interact with the fire to pick up the arrow. 

### fireplace destinations from the Steam guide
- **circle near Double Tap** → **fireplace near Samantha’s room**
- **circle on top of the castle** → **church fireplace**
- **circle near the Clock Tower** → **fireplace upstairs near the first dragon / Wunderfizz** 

### optional symbol reference
From the extra guide you found, the symbol mapping is:

- **Templar cross** → fireplace by Samantha’s Room → corresponding ring by **KRM-262 / Double Tap**
- **Arrow right with two triangles underneath** → fireplace under the Dragon Head statue in the **Control Room** → corresponding ring by the **Death Ray / Wundersphere**
- **Two triangles right, arrow right, two triangles right** → fireplace in the **Lower Courtyard upstairs across from the Mystery Box / first dragon area** → corresponding ring outside the **Clock Tower**

## step 7 - interact with the sun

After picking up the arrow from the fireplace, go back to the **top of the castle** and interact with the sun, which is now at the same height as you. It rises and explodes, leaving behind the **reforged arrow** to pick up. 

### steps
1. Return to the upper castle / sun position.
2. Interact with the sun.
3. Pick up the reforged arrow left behind.

Related images:
- images/fire_bow/sun_near_trap.jpg
- images/fire_bow/interact_sun.jpg

## step 8 - place the arrow in the pyramid room

Take the reforged arrow back to the **fire altar** in the pyramid room and place it there. Then kill zombies **very close** to the altar until the charge is complete. After that, interact with the arrow and wait for the animation to finish. Then the upgraded bow becomes available. 

### steps
1. Go to the pyramid room.
2. Place the arrow on the Fire altar.
3. Kill zombies very close to it until you hear the completion sound.
4. Interact with the arrow and wait for the animation.
5. Pick up the upgraded Fire Bow.

## full progression summary

1. Go to the top of the Clock Tower.
2. Shoot the marked wall with a charged arrow and start the quest.
3. Go to the rocket test site and wait for a rocket test.
4. After the test ends, shoot the glowing fireball outside.
5. Use the Wunderspheres to hit the 3 red circles while airborne.
6. Stand in each circle and kill zombies to charge them.
7. Use the clock to identify the correct fireplace.
8. Spawn and move the blazing rock from the active circle to that fireplace.
9. Interact with the fireplace to collect the arrow.
10. Return to the top of the castle and interact with the sun.
11. Pick up the reforged arrow.
12. Place it on the Fire altar in the pyramid room and charge it with nearby zombie kills.
13. Interact with the altar and pick up **Kreeaho'ahm nal Ahmhogaroc**.
