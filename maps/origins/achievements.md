# Origins achievements

## Achievements

Little Lost Girl

Difficulty: Moderate to Hard

Complete the main Little Lost Girl quest to free Samantha. Use main_ee.md for its full ordered procedure; this achievement is the main quest, not a separate random-luck task.

Overachiever

Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Complete all four reward-chest challenges in one match: activate all six generators, earn 115 zombie headshots, spend 30,000 points, and fill all four soul chests. Check the challenge icons and claim rewards at the chest in spawn or behind the church. In co-op, filling the soul chests is shared; each player must satisfy their own other counters. Generator-templar and Insta-Kill headshots may not count toward the headshot challenge, even if the scoreboard increases.
