# Wind Staff and Boreas' Fury

## Preparation - Wind staff

Same song than before, you'll need 3 parts, a crystal and a yellow record.

The 3 parts are quite easily obtained, they are located in the head of the three giant robots. Thor, is moving in a line crossing generator 1 / 2 / 3, Odin moving from generator 5 to 4, and Freya accessible only via the church, near generator 6

Related image: images/source-guide/a2b1a73f9206d8ed1a2a2cc75adb3418d2c6d4c7-02d33b550e.webp

To access the robot, you must look bellow their feet. One of the foot will be lighted by multiple lights. When shooting those lights, it will "open" the foot and you will have to let the giant smash you beneath his opened foot. Don't worry if you opened the door, you will survive and be propulsed to his head before being evacuated after a short time. The spots where the giant robots walk are symbolized by big rectangular holes in the ground.

## Preparation - Borea's Fury (Upgraded wind staff)

With the wind staff in hand, head to the crazy place. Right above the teleporter, is a stone disk separated into 4 circles.

Related image: images/source-guide/8716649851069f7c2346536b8101ec8b1c1335a6-199277fbfb.webp

Following this picture (Thanks to www.Kronorium.com) , you must arrange the circles in a certain order. Shoot them with your staff until the symbols on the left are in the highlighted line.

Related image: images/source-guide/a34ec79b7939d9c5b1b2781d01f1e53e0a7b9355-d6616f66d7.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/6de897b35bd86c9a50a040d172eb48e3e955a594-60a04958ae.webp

Once done, head back to the main area of the map. 3 chimney will be smoking, you will have to shoot them with your wind staff in a way that the smoke heads towards the excavation site.

Basically just make sure the chimney is between you and the excavation site, then shoot it.

They are located:

- Near generator 4, between Juggernog and Wind portal's entrance
  Related image: images/source-guide/ebe4d341faa5f39970f81b7ee1156e0f83b2802e-0daf39edae.webp
- In the No Man's Land area near the church, on the opposite way from where the tank moves
  Related image: images/source-guide/7ed75edcc067a5a79f27f5079ce6d0423cd0d6c9-402f9243e1.webp
- Just on the left of Stamin-up near generator 5
  Related image: images/source-guide/f536838713c7d061cf1abbe529cda0fde5db1ef0-a1aa21df03.webp

Make sure the smoke is redirected towards the excavation site, and not in a slightly altered direction. A sound should play.

Proceed now to the "Preparation - Final upgrade stage for all staffs" part.
