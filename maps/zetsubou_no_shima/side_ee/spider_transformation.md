# spider transformation

## overview

On Zetsubou No Shima, you can temporarily transform into a spider.

To do this, you need:
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- the **KT-4**
- all **3 trials completed**
- an **electrified shield**

This side easter egg is a bit buggy, but when done correctly it lets you transform into an almost invulnerable spider for about 1 minute.

## step 1 - prepare a red glowing spider

During a spider round, use the Skull of Nan Sapwe in **Hypnosis mode**. One spider should begin glowing red.

### steps
1. Wait for a spider round.
2. Use the Skull of Nan Sapwe in hypnosis mode.
3. Identify the spider that begins glowing red.
4. Keep that spider alive.
5. Kill the other spiders.

Related image: images/side_ees/spiderglowingred.jpg

## step 2 - take the spider to all water sources

You must bring the red glowing spider to each water source and let it drink.

### steps
1. Lead the red spider to one water source.
2. Let it drink.
3. Repeat for the other water sources.

## step 3 - use the Lab A cage

Now lower the cage in **Lab A** and bring the spider into it. Then raise it again, hit the panel with the electrified shield, and lower it once more.

### steps
1. Bring the red spider to Lab A.
2. Lower the cage.
3. Trap the spider inside.
4. Raise the cage again.
5. Hit the panel with the electrified shield.
6. Lower the cage again.

Related images:
- images/side_ees/spiderundergroundoncage.jpg
- images/side_ees/spiderupagaingettingvenom.jpg

## step 4 - complete the special spider round

A new round should begin. End it. Then a special spider round begins where the spiders can only be killed by:
- the **KT-4**
- the **electrified shield**
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**

After that, raise the cage again and take the venom.

## step 5 - transform into a spider

Once the setup is complete, you can transform into a spider for a short time.

### result
- lasts around **1 minute**
- the form is very strong
- it is somewhat bugged and can clip / kill you if enemies pin you into bad geometry
