# music 1

## overview

The first secret song on Zetsubou No Shima is triggered by interacting with 3 puppets around the map.

This plays **Dead Flowers**.

## puppet locations

- beside the perk machine near the **blue water** source
- on a table on the **upper floor**
- on a table near the **KT-4 crafting underground** area

## steps

1. Go to the first puppet near the blue water / perk area and interact with it.
2. Go to the second puppet on the upper floor and interact with it.
3. Go to the third puppet near the KT-4 crafting underground area and interact with it.
4. After all 3 are activated, the song starts playing.
