# pap

## overview

At the start of Revelations, Pack-a-Punch has been swallowed by an Apothicon. To unlock it, you must activate the 4 ritual altars around the map, open the portal routes, go to Nacht Der Untoten, and enter the Apothicon.

Once inside, Pack-a-Punch is directly in front of you. To free it, you must shoot the 3 glowing parts holding it in place.

## step 1 - activate the 4 ritual altars

There are 4 ritual altars around the map. For each one:
- pay **500 points**
- stay in the area until the activation finishes
- the spawned enemies disappear at the end
- you get your points back
- a portal to Nacht Der Untoten is opened

### portal / altar locations
- spawn area
- Der Eisendrache area
- Mob of the Dead area
- Verruckt area

## step 2 - go to Nacht Der Untoten

After all 4 ritual altars are completed, go to **Nacht Der Untoten**.

Go upstairs and watch the console. When it is flashing, interact with it.

The guide notes this is when the Apothicon is in front of you.

### steps
1. Complete all 4 ritual altar activations.
2. Go to Nacht Der Untoten.
3. Go upstairs.
4. Wait for the console to flash.
5. Interact with it.

Related image: images/pap/consoleyoushouldinteract.jpg

## step 3 - jump into the Apothicon

After using the console, the door on your right opens.

Jump into the mouth of the Apothicon.

### steps
1. Use the flashing console in Nacht.
2. Go through the opened door.
3. Jump into the Apothicon's mouth.

Related image: images/pap/apothicanmouth.jpg

## step 4 - free Pack-a-Punch

Inside the Apothicon, Pack-a-Punch is right in front of you.

To release it, shoot the 3 glowing parts / glowing balls on the side of it.

### steps
1. Enter the Apothicon.
2. Stand in front of Pack-a-Punch.
3. Shoot the 3 glowing objects attached around it.
4. Once they are destroyed, Pack-a-Punch becomes available.

Related images:
- images/pap/shoothisintestine.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Activate the 4 ritual altars.
2. Open the 4 main routes / portals.
3. Go to Nacht Der Untoten.
4. Use the flashing console upstairs.
5. Enter the Apothicon.
6. Shoot the 3 glowing parts holding Pack-a-Punch.
7. Use Pack-a-Punch.
