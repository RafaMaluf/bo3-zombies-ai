# skeletons

## overview

On Der Eisendrache, you can replace the normal zombies with skeletons like the ones seen during the boss fight. This is purely cosmetic / fun, but it changes their appearance and sounds.

To activate it, you must shoot 3 hidden skulls with the **Wrath of the Ancients**. These skulls can only be seen while using the Gobblegum **In Plain Sight**.

## skull locations

- right next to Mule Kick
- in front of the central stained glass in the church
- on the wall next to Double Tap

## steps

1. Use **In Plain Sight** so the hidden skulls become visible.
2. Go to the skull near Mule Kick and shoot it with the Wrath of the Ancients.
3. Go to the skull in front of the central stained glass in the church and shoot it.
4. Go to the skull on the wall next to Double Tap and shoot it.
5. After all 3 are hit, zombies will start spawning as skeletons.

## how to disable skeleton mode

If you want zombies to go back to normal:
1. Go to the church.
2. Interact with the 3 skulls on the chair in front of the dragon.
3. The skeleton mode will turn off.

If you want skeletons again, repeat the original hidden-skull step.
