# free perk

## overview

There is a free perk easter egg in **Der Eisendrache** on Revelations.

You must use:
- anti-gravity
- the corruption generator
- a parkour route outside the map path

At the end, you collect a perk bottle that gives a random perk to every player.

## steps

1. Go to the Der Eisendrache area.
2. Activate anti-gravity.
3. Activate the corruption generator.
4. Look above the portal and locate the perk bottle.
5. Use wall-runs and the parkour route outside the normal area to reach it.
6. Pick up the perk bottle.

## result

- every player gets a random perk

Important note:
- falling into the void here is safe; you respawn back into the map
