# swamp monster

## overview

There is a small visual easter egg on Zetsubou No Shima involving a distant monster outside the playable area.

It has no gameplay reward, but it is a neat background detail.

## steps

1. Reach around **round 50**.
2. Listen for a distant feral roar.
3. Go near the Lab B side where the sightline opens outward.
4. Look far outside the playable area.
5. You may see a giant shadowy creature moving between the trees.
