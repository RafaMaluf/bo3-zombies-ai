# Ascension main easter egg

## Main easter egg quest

The goal of your quest in Ascension is to free Dr.Gersh out from the Casimir Mechanism. After completing it, you will receive a shy Death Machine for about 90 seconds. Yay. *.*

Requirements :

- 4 Players
- Gersh Devices ( Black hole special grenade found in mystery box)
- Highly Effective Weapons ( PaP-ed Thunder gun, Ray gun or / and Ray gun mark 2)
- Matrioshka dolls
- Have the power on

First step: Now that the facility is juiced up, you will have to throw a gersh device on a light on top of a generator , located outside of the map, on your right when facing the stairs leading to Widow's Wine.

Related image: images/source-guide/e25af236718d899765f9603bc65b4926f571275c-d0185f0369.webp

Second step: Now that the generator is gone, head to the area of the Moon lander near Stamin-up. Upon arriving, turn to your left, there should be a flickering tv screen under the stairs. Interact with it , it should now show you the Illuminati symbol.

Related image: images/source-guide/4c5742be8e6b919ded7a58ddd7708a0f897649f7-3bd77c3e48.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/e6817436cde5d33e019ac2dc282307b726a28709-6bd7f1ab1f.webp

Third Step: Now, you must wait a special round to occur. In this wave, zombie monkeys will attack you. They will also attack your perk machines, stealing your perks if you don't protect the soda machines.

You must activate 4 buttons scattered around the map at the same time. Each player must press them simultaneously.

They are spotted:

- On the wall, left side to Widow's Wine

- On the wall, right side to Speed Cola
  Related image: images/source-guide/267644161a13d32fc9b5274400f83891b4fa9367-f2f8d52142.webp
- On the wall, left side to Stamin-up
  Related image: images/source-guide/54a3bc5369e10576893dbe94696d840191b4cef5-43e8eb47b0.webp
- On the wall, facing Juggernog, above the centrifuge room
  Related image: images/source-guide/b6e06efa3dabfdf2e497c8c579b58b20ec7a9644-35d3b66fc0.webp

Note that the buttons will appear on the wall only in the special round, and after completing previous steps.

Fourth Step: Head back to the Pack-a-Punch, on the opposite wall there is a clock with a pressure plate forming a circle in front of it. All players must stand on the plate without leaving for 2 consecutive minutes. Be sure to keep a crawler and draw him far enough. Once the needle has done one full tour of the clock, an explosion should intervene.

Related image: images/source-guide/f75b392b29bead52eadfb9ed9f2b6b612558f774-6bdd076026.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/38d24ea7b5bdd505ccec5603d9bdbb3af7dfe3e0-197264fb55.webp

Fifth step: In this step you will need to write the word "LUNA" by taking the letters, acting as bonuses. You will have to call the Moon Lander in the spawn landing location, near Quick Revive. One player shall stand on it, while another player will call the Moon lander to the location around Stamin-up.

On the travel, a giant floating "L" should be in your path.

Related image: images/source-guide/ceb20e3537799cf309a8a968f980b64eef5bd2b7-f1246b2081.webp

Stand still on the platform, another player shall call it back to the spawn to let you pick up the "U".

Related image: images/source-guide/8dc21591ed31dfcd73af39301952bff0de247b4f-402281f078.webp

Again, another player shall call the platform at the landing pad near Speed Cola.

Related image: images/source-guide/5ba15789078c24148236c01307ee41cc528e50f4-5470508aca.webp

Finally, someone else shall call the Moon lander at the landing pad near Stamin-up while you pick the "A" up.

Related image: images/source-guide/a6d63bad528fa7d4a2cbe5ef3b266ac811f180eb-666bea7c13.webp

Sixth step: (Finale): Between Stamin-up and its appropriate landing pad, a light should have appeared. A player needs to throw a Gersh Device on the light , then together shoot it with the Matryoshka Dolls and PaP-ed Thundergun and Raygun.

Related image: images/source-guide/21a4d64ee1cf165322e1fcbf9068b38ad269ac4c-74eea739d6.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/897716e22b7fb38583c913d182cbb525590910b1-329163fcad.webp

Eventually, the light will raise in the sky and you will receive your reward, completing the Easter Egg. Noice !
