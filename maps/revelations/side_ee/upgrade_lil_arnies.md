# upgrade lil arnies

## overview

You can upgrade the Lil' Arnies in Revelations.

The first requirement is collective:
- the lobby must kill **150 zombies total with Lil' Arnies**

After that, there is a short placement step.

## steps

1. Have the team collectively get 150 zombie kills with Lil' Arnies.
2. Go to the spawn area.
3. Throw a Lil' Arnie into the window above the church.
4. If done correctly:
   - you get all your Arnies back
   - every player who has Lil' Arnies receives the upgraded version
