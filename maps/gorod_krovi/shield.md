# shield

## overview

The zombie shield on Gorod Krovi is the **Guard of Fafnir**. It is a special shield because:
- it protects you from normal rear attacks like other BO3 shields
- it can fire **green flame blasts**
- while holding it, it protects you from **dragon fire**

It can later be upgraded into **Tiamat's Maw**, which is stronger, more durable, and fires **red flame** instead of green.

This file covers:
- how to build the base shield
- how to upgrade it

## part 1 - first shield piece

The first part is located between the **Department Store** and the **Operations Bunker**.

### possible locations
- on a chair beside **Juggernog**
- on the door of a truck, down the stairs after Juggernog
- on beds in the Operations Bunker, just left of the entrance

## crafting the shield

Once all 3 parts are collected, build the shield at the crafting table in the **Operations Bunker underground**.

### steps
1. Collect the first part between Department Store and Operations Bunker.
2. Collect the second part between Operations Bunker and Infirmary.
3. Collect the third part around the Armory.
4. Go to the crafting bench underground in the Operations Bunker.
5. Build the **Guard of Fafnir**.

Related image: images/shield/shieldready.jpg

## upgrade - tiamat's maw

The upgraded version of the shield is called **Tiamat's Maw**.

Benefits:
- more durability
- stronger fire blast
- red fire instead of green

## upgrade step 1 - get 50 shield kills

To begin the upgrade, you must get **50 kills** using:
- shield fire blast
- shield bash

A sound plays when this part is complete.

### steps
1. Use the shield flame attack and melee bash to kill zombies.
2. Reach 50 total shield kills.
3. Wait for the confirmation sound.

Related image: images/shield/upgradefireblast.jpg

## upgrade step 2 - survive dragon fire with the shield

Next, survive dragon fire for long enough while holding the shield.

### steps
1. Wait for a dragon to burn an area.
2. Equip and hold the shield.
3. Stay in the dragon fire while protected by the shield.
4. Wait for the confirmation sound.

Important note:
- the guide says if the upgrade is not working later, repeating the first two steps can fix it

## upgrade step 3 - shoot the 3 purple symbols

Now you must hit **3 purple symbols** with the fire blast from the shield.

These symbols are in the 3 areas where you can call dragons.

### symbol 1 - dragon command
Stand on the platform and look at the building on the left.

Related image: images/shield/upgradesymbol.jpg

### symbol 2 - supply depot
Look toward the roof area in Supply Depot.

Related image: images/shield/upgradesymbol2.jpg

### symbol 3 - tank factory
The symbol is on the giant robot shoulder near Tank Factory.

Related image: images/shield/upgradesymbol3.jpg

### steps
1. Go to each of the 3 dragon travel areas.
2. Find the purple symbol.
3. Shoot it with the shield fire blast.
4. Confirm the symbol disappears.
5. Repeat for all 3.

## upgrade step 4 - place the shield in the dead dragon

After the previous steps, go back to **spawn**, near the graves. There is a dead dragon there.

You should be able to place the shield into the dragon's belly. After a short wait, retrieve it.

### steps
1. Return to Belinski Square / spawn.
2. Find the dead dragon near the graves.
3. Place the shield into the dragon.
4. Wait a few seconds.
5. Take it back.

Related image: images/shield/upgradegetinsidethedragon.jpg

## important note

Once the upgrade is completed:
- every future shield taken from the crafting table is also upgraded

If you cannot place the shield into the dragon at the end:
- repeat the first 2 upgrade steps again

## full progression summary

### base shield
1. Collect the 3 shield parts.
2. Go to the Operations Bunker crafting bench.
3. Build the Guard of Fafnir.

### upgraded shield
1. Get 50 kills with shield bash / shield fire.
2. Survive dragon fire while holding the shield.
3. Shoot the 3 purple symbols with the shield fire.
4. Go to spawn.
5. Place the shield into the dead dragon.
6. Retrieve **Tiamat's Maw**.
