# wolf bow

## overview

The Wolf Bow upgrade turns the Wrath of the Ancients into **Kreeholo lu Kreemasaleet**.

This upgrade follows this order:
1. interact with the 4 paintings in the correct order
2. collect the broken arrow / quest item underground
3. shoot the flag at the rocket area and pick up the glowing skull
4. place the skull on the headless wolf skeleton
5. follow the spectral wolf through its stops
6. kill zombies near each pile of bones and pick up the revealed piece
7. shoot the wolf-symbol hole while wall-running during anti-gravity
8. stand on the platform, interact with the hole, and pick up the arrow
9. place the arrow on the corresponding altar in the pyramid room
10. charge it with zombie souls
11. claim the upgraded bow

## step 1 - interact with the 4 paintings in the correct order

To begin the Wolf Bow quest, you must interact with the 4 paintings in the correct order.

When you interact with the correct painting:
- a green symbol remains
- your character speaks

If you interact with the wrong painting:
- the green symbol disappears

Important note:
- if you get the second painting wrong, you must interact with the first painting again before trying the second one again

### correct order of the paintings

1. **The king on his throne with a wolf beside him**
2. **The king on his horse with his armor on**
3. **The castle burning with a dark creature in the sky**
4. **The skeleton of the king**

### painting locations

- past Samantha's room, near the 1500 door
- on a balcony in the church
- in the clock tower near the stairs
- in the room beneath the clock tower, near Mule Kick

## step 2 - collect the broken arrow / quest item underground

Once the painting order is completed correctly, go back underground. The wall immediately on the left after the stairs will be broken, and the quest item will have dropped there.

### steps
1. Go back underground.
2. Check the broken wall on the left after the stairs.
3. Pick up the Wolf Bow quest item.

Related image: images/wolf_bow/arrow_start_quest.jpg

## step 3 - shoot the flag at the rocket area

Head to the landing pad / rocket area and look behind you. There is a flag above the entrance on the mountain. You must use a charged shot on the base of that flag.

### steps
1. Go to the landing pad / rocket area.
2. Look behind you toward the mountain.
3. Find the flag above the entrance.
4. Charge your bow shot.
5. Shoot the base of the flag.
6. Pick up the glowing skull once it lands.

Related image: 
- images/wolf_bow/flag_from_rocket.jpg
- images/wolf_bow/wolf_skull.jpg

## step 4 - place the skull on the headless wolf skeleton

Take the glowing skull back to the altars room and place it on the headless wolf skeleton near the Spirit altar.

### steps
1. Return to the altars / pyramid room.
2. Go to the headless wolf skeleton near the Spirit altar.
3. Place the skull on it.

Related image: images/wolf_bow/bones_to_interact_pyramids.jpg

## step 5 - follow the spectral wolf

A spectral wolf will now spawn. You must follow him until he stops.

At each stop:
- he reveals a small pile of bones
- you must kill zombies around that pile

After enough kills:
- the wolf scratches the pile
- a quest piece is revealed
- you must pick it up

Then the wolf moves to the next stop.

### steps
1. Follow the spectral wolf.
2. When he stops, find the revealed pile of bones.
3. Kill zombies around it.
4. Wait for the wolf to scratch the pile.
5. Pick up the revealed piece.
6. Repeat for the next piles.

Related images: images/wolf_bow/wolf_spirit_you_follow.jpg

## step 6 - wall hole / anti-gravity step

After the wolf finishes its path, it stands against a wall in the basement.

Above the wolf there is a hole with a wolf symbol above it. This symbol only appears while you are wall-running during anti-gravity. You must shoot it while it is lit up.

If done correctly:
- a platform rises
- you stand on the platform
- interact with the hole
- interact again to grab the arrow

### steps
1. Go to the wolf's final stop in the basement.
2. Wait for anti-gravity to activate.
3. Wall-run so the wolf symbol above the hole appears.
4. Shoot the lit wolf-symbol hole.
5. Wait for the platform to rise.
6. Stand on the platform.
7. Interact with the hole.
8. Interact again to collect the arrow.

## step 7 - place the arrow in the pyramid room

Now that the arrow is in your inventory, return to the pyramid room and place it on the Wolf Bow altar.

### steps
1. Go back to the pyramid room.
2. Place the arrow on the corresponding altar.

Related images: images/wolf_bow/arrow_in_pyramids.jpg

## step 8 - charge the altar

Kill zombies close to the altar until the charging is complete.

### steps
1. Stay near the altar.
2. Kill zombies close to it.
3. Wait until you hear the completion sound.
4. Confirm that no more souls enter the altar.

Related image: images/wolf_bow/arrow_in_pyramids.jpg

## step 9 - claim the upgraded bow

When the altar is fully charged, interact with the arrow. This triggers the animation. After the animation is over, pick up the upgraded bow.

### steps
1. Interact with the arrow on the altar.
2. Wait for the animation to finish.
3. Pick up **Kreeholo lu Kreemasaleet**.

## full progression summary

1. Interact with the 4 paintings in the correct order.
2. Go underground and collect the broken arrow / quest item.
3. Go to the landing pad and charge-shoot the base of the flag.
4. Pick up the glowing skull.
5. Return to the altars room and place the skull on the headless wolf skeleton.
6. Follow the spectral wolf.
7. At each stop, kill zombies around the pile of bones and pick up the revealed piece.
8. At the final wall, shoot the lit wolf-symbol hole while wall-running during anti-gravity.
9. Stand on the raised platform and interact twice to collect the arrow.
10. Return to the pyramid room and place the arrow on the Wolf altar.
11. Kill zombies close to it until fully charged.
12. Interact with the arrow and pick up the upgraded Wolf Bow.
