# main ee

## overview

The Zetsubou No Shima main easter egg is mechanically simpler than some other Black Ops 3 maps. The hard part is not the quest itself, but the amount of setup required before you can start it cleanly.

Before starting, you should already have:
- **Masamune**
- **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- **Power on**
- **Gas Mask**
- **Shield**
- **Bucket**
- **Anywhere But Here!** Gobblegum

Important note:
This guide assumes you already used the **Skull of Nan Sapwe mesmerize spots** during the earlier setup, especially the ones tied into the Masamune path. The easter egg itself is short compared to the total preparation time.

The goal of the quest is to collect **3 gears**, repair the elevator near the bunker entrance, and defeat the final boss.

## step 1 - grow the shell plant and shoot down the plane

You should start preparing this step as early as possible.

You need to grow a plant that gives the **shell** used for the anti-air cannon. To do that:
- plant a seed
- water it 3 times with **blue water**
- fertilize it with the **KT-4 / Masamune** after each watering

This can result in either:
- a bonus plant
- a cloning plant

What you want is the **bonus plant** that contains the shell.

### steps
1. Plant multiple seeds around the map.
2. Water them over 3 rounds with **blue water**.
3. Fertilize them with the KT-4 / Masamune after each watering.
4. Keep doing this until one gives you the **shell**.

Once you have the shell:
5. Go to the bunker roof / AA cannon area.
6. Place the shell into the cannon.
7. Wait until the large plane is in sight.
8. Fire the cannon and shoot the plane down.
9. Go collect the **first gear** from where the plane part falls.

### practical note
The more plants you run at the same time, the faster this step becomes.

## step 2 - get the second gear from the zipline platform

To get the second gear, you must use the Lab A zipline and drop onto the otherwise unreachable platform.

Before doing this, you must have an **electrified shield**.

### solo method
1. Electrify your shield.
2. Go to the Lab A zipline console.
3. Hit the console with the electrified shield.
4. Use the zipline.
5. While directly above the platform, use melee to drop straight down.
6. Pick up the **second gear**.

### co-op method
1. Player A takes the zipline.
2. Player B waits with the electrified shield.
3. When Player A is directly above the platform, Player B hits the zipline console.
4. Player A drops onto the platform.
5. Pick up the **second gear**.

Important note:
There is basically no inertia here. The drop is straight down, so timing matters a lot.

## step 3 - get the third gear with Anywhere But Here

The third gear is the easiest one. You need to use **Anywhere But Here!**

When you use it, it teleports you to a normally unreachable room above Lab A. The **third gear** is on the floor there, near the large eggs.

### steps
1. Equip **Anywhere But Here!**
2. Use it.
3. Check the hidden room above Lab A.
4. Pick up the **third gear**.

## step 4 - repair the elevator

Once you have all 3 gears, go to the elevator room near the bunker entrance and place them in.

### steps
1. Go to the elevator room.
2. Insert all 3 gears.
3. Prepare fully before activating the elevator, because from here the quest goes directly into the final sequence.

Related image: images/main_ee/11gearsbehindelevator.jpg

## step 5 - enter the boss area

Before activating the elevator, make sure you have:
- Pack-a-Punched weapons
- perks
- Masamune
- Gobblegums
- Gas Mask

Then use the elevator.

Once below:
- destroy the roots with the Masamune
- pass through the final door
- begin the boss fight

### steps
1. Enter the elevator with your team.
2. Ride it down.
3. Shoot the blocking roots with the Masamune.
4. Move through the opened path.

Related image: images/main_ee/12destroytrees.jpg

## step 6 - boss fight

The boss is a corrupted swamp / tree version of Takeo. He has **4 roots**, each with glowing spores on them.

Your job is to:
- shoot the weak spore points with the **Masamune**
- survive while the weak points are hidden
- repeat until all 4 roots are destroyed

During the fight, you will also deal with:
- zombies
- Thrashers
- spiders

The Masamune is very strong here, and the fight gives frequent Max Ammos, so keep using it.

### steps
1. Enter the boss arena.
2. Look for the exposed glowing weak spots on the roots.
3. Shoot them with the Masamune.
4. When the weak spots are hidden, survive the enemy pressure.
5. Repeat until all 4 roots are destroyed.
6. Finish the fight and trigger the ending cutscene.

Related image: images/main_ee/13wheretoshoottheboss.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Grow blue-water fertilized plants until you get the shell.
2. Load the shell into the AA cannon.
3. Shoot down the plane and collect the first gear.
4. Electrify the shield.
5. Use the Lab A zipline method to collect the second gear.
6. Use Anywhere But Here! to reach the hidden room and collect the third gear.
7. Insert all 3 gears into the elevator.
8. Enter the lower area.
9. Destroy the blocking roots with the Masamune.
10. Fight the boss by shooting the glowing weak points on the roots.
11. Survive until all 4 roots are destroyed.
12. Finish the easter egg.

## practical note

This is one of the easiest BO3 main easter eggs once setup is complete. The real time sink is everything before this:
- Masamune
- trials
- electrified shield
- shell plant
- equipment setup
