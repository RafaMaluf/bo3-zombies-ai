# dubstep monkeys

## overview

It is possible to upgrade the Monkey Bombs on Gorod Krovi into **Dubstep Monkeys**.

Each player must do this independently.

There is also an easier alternate method using **Crate Power**.

## step 1 - pick up the flowerpot

Each player sees only one valid flowerpot.

### possible flowerpot locations
- Dragon Command, on the first floor, on the desk beside an Ace of Spades card and a Mystery Box spot
- Supply Depot, on a desk just after the crashed train wagons
- Supply Depot, on desks near a bomb location and a Dead Ended bottle
- Department Store, on a table beside the LCAR 9

### steps
1. Go to your visible flowerpot.
2. Pick it up.

## step 2 - light and pick up the candle

Now you must ignite a candle with the **Fafnir Shield** and pick it up.

Each player only sees one correct candle.

### possible candle locations
- on a shelf in Department Store
- in Supply Depot, beneath the desk where one flowerpot can appear
- on a metal table in Tank Factory
- on a bed beside the Infirmary stairs

### steps
1. Find your visible candle.
2. Light it with the shield fire.
3. Pick it up.

## step 3 - get monkey bomb kills

You now need roughly 40 to 50 zombie kills using Monkey Bombs.

### steps
1. Throw Monkey Bombs into zombie groups.
2. Keep using them until enough kills are reached.
3. A flashy blue reward drop appears.
4. Pick it up.

Related images:
- images/side_ees/upgradedmonkeybombs.jpg
- images/side_ees/upgradedmonkeybombpickflashydrop.jpg

## step 4 - activate the tomb

Go back to spawn and throw a Monkey Bomb onto your tomb.

Look at the green torch and press interact when prompted.

### steps
1. Return to your grave near spawn.
2. Throw a Monkey Bomb onto the tomb.
3. Watch for the prompt.
4. Press interact.
5. The Monkey Bombs become upgraded.

## alternate easy method

There is a shortcut:
- use the **Crate Power** Gobblegum
- get Monkey Bombs from the Mystery Box

This can give you the upgraded version more easily.

## result

After the upgrade:
- your Monkey Bombs become **Dubstep Monkeys**
