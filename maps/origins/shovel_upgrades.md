# Shovel, Golden Shovel and Golden Helmet

## Equipment - Shovel, Golden Shovel, Golden Helmet and red Dig ups

You can find shovels in Origins in various interior rooms. Some examples are the bunker at the beginning or at the entrance of the Wind tunnel.

Related image: images/source-guide/cdf77f3699876c9e87a6af6174e03853e64bfa21-e967256ebc.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/9d5deddaa19aa6679a5f5f563a69a7d70d7e9ba9-71917dcf80.webp

With the shovel in your pocket, you can use it to dig small pile of skulls. It will give you weapons, zombies, grenades, or even ice staff part.

They are located all around the map and are commonly visible.

Related image: images/source-guide/a0f3c31ded2a5ad1497a5f5757d6ee6d966105be-29f670247e.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/14673afe8cb3fc31c2cb32f924a53acd0ff4d475-461b5d1fa3.webp

For the ice staff parts, you will dig one in Thor's patrol, one in Odin's and another one in Freya's. (Basically starting part of the map, middle area, and then church area.)

Related image: images/source-guide/771215fa697454bbe506e88bd182ee0127f62bd3-dc31ca2a0b.webp

However note that you must wait the weather to be snowy to dig staff parts.

Related image: images/source-guide/024af267c2a9e63e0a7729e9dcd7bc3b7fe5ace2-c4eb6b84c1.webp

Eventually, by digging up 30 dirt piles, your shovel will turn into a golden shovel, giving you overall best outcome from the piles. Keep digging with your golden shovel and you will eventually find a golden helmet. This rare equipment will protect you against the giant robots feet. Moreover you will look pimp as hell.

Related image: images/source-guide/76b768351d3796a9eea1a4218d1c7c68a9c5bf0d-b207e45b02.webp

Another cool thing is that in zombie blood, you will see (if you've got the golden shovel) red glowing dirt piles. By digging them you might find empty perk bottle, acting like Unquenchable gobblegum.

They are located at tens of fixed locations, not respawning when you used one.
