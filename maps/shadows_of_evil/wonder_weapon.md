# wonder weapon

The wonder weapon is the Apothicon Servant. It is built from:
- Margwa Heart
- Margwa Tentacle
- Xenomatter

## Margwa Heart

Dropped by a Margwa.

Related image: images/ww/margwa_heart.jpg

## Margwa Tentacle

Comes from the purple pods. Purple is the only pod stage that can provide the Margwa Tentacle.

Related image: images/ww/margwa_tentacle.jpg

## Xenomatter

Dropped by Parasites or Insanity Elementals. Many players call the Insanity Elementals “meatballs”.

Related image: images/ww/xenomatter.jpg

## build

Take all 3 parts to a crafting table and build the Apothicon Servant.
