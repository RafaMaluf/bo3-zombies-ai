# wrath of the ancients

## overview

Before you can upgrade any of the 4 elemental bows on Der Eisendrache, you must first obtain the base bow, the **Wrath of the Ancients**. To do that, you need to feed all 3 dragon heads around the map. Once all 3 dragons are satisfied, the wall holding the bow opens and you can claim it.

Each dragon must fully consume **8 zombies**, for a total of **24 zombies**. A dragon only accepts souls after it fully wakes up. While feeding a dragon, keep the zombies close to it when killing them, or the souls will not count. You have to kill each zombie and wait for the dragon to consume it. The zombie body will levitate to the dragon's mouth and after fully consuming it you can kill another one.

## dragon head 1

The first dragon head is located in the **courtyard / lower area near Double Tap**.

### steps
1. Open the map until you reach the first dragon.
2. Kill a zombie directly under or very close to the dragon so it starts feeding.
3. Continue killing zombies near it until it has eaten 8 souls.
4. When finished, the dragon will turn back to stone and destroy itself.

Related image: images/base_bow/first_dragon.jpg

## dragon head 2

The second dragon head is located in the **church / upper area**.

### steps
1. Reach the church side of the map.
2. Wake the dragon by killing a zombie near it.
3. Feed it 8 zombies total.
4. Once complete, it turns back to stone.

Related image: images/base_bow/second_dragon.jpg

## dragon head 3

The third dragon head is located in the **Undercroft / pyramid room**.

### steps
1. Reach the pyramid room.
2. Kill a zombie close enough to the dragon to begin feeding it.
3. Continue until it has consumed 8 zombies.
4. After the final soul, it returns to stone, meaning it is complete.

Related image: images/base_bow/third_dragon.jpg

## important feeding notes

- Each dragon requires **8 zombies**
- The zombie must die close enough to the dragon
- If the dragon is still moving and eating, it is not finished yet
- If the dragon has turned back to stone, that dragon is done

## obtaining the bow

After all 3 dragons are fully fed, the Knight's Tomb area unlocks the base bow. (Between church and pyramids)

### steps
1. Feed all 3 dragon heads completely.
2. Go to the room where the bow is held in the wall.
3. Interact with the bow to take it.
4. You now have the **Wrath of the Ancients** and can begin any of the 4 elemental bow upgrade quests.

Related image: images/base_bow/base_bow.jpg

## practical note

A very common early-game route on Der Eisendrache is:
1. turn on power
2. feed the 3 dragons as rounds go by
3. open Pack-a-Punch progression
4. take the Wrath of the Ancients
5. immediately start the bow upgrade you want

This is one of the most important setup steps on the map, because all upgraded bows depend on this base weapon first.
