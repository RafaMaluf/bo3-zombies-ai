# power + pap + teleporter

## overview

Der Eisendrache has a structured early setup built around 4 connected systems:
- power
- Pack-a-Punch
- the first teleporter trip to the rocket test site
- the Wunderspheres / landing pads for later movement

Power is activated with a normal switch. Pack-a-Punch starts the game broken and must be rebuilt by interacting with its split pieces in 3 different locations. After it is rebuilt, it no longer stays fixed in one place and instead rotates between those same 3 locations.

The rocket test site is first accessed through the teleporter. After that, movement becomes much easier once the jump pads / Wunderspheres are active.

## power

Power on Der Eisendrache is turned on with a standard switch.

### steps
1. Open the map toward the power room.
2. Reach the switch.
3. Interact with it to turn the power on.

Related image: images/pap_power/power_switch.jpg

## pack-a-punch overview

At the start of the match, Pack-a-Punch is broken. You do not unlock it by pressing one button or linking teleporters like on The Giant. Instead, you rebuild it by interacting with its separated pieces in 3 locations.

Those 3 locations are:
- the Undercroft / pyramid room
- the rocket test site
- the upper castle / bastion side

After all 3 interactions are done, Pack-a-Punch is rebuilt. From then on, it rotates between those 3 locations.

The third Pack-a-Punch rebuild point is on the upper castle / bastion side of the map.

Even if you already know the other 2 locations, do not forget this third interaction, because Pack-a-Punch only becomes fully rebuilt after all 3 points have been activated.

## how to rebuild Pack-a-Punch

### steps
1. Turn on the power.
2. Reach the Undercroft / pyramid room and interact with the broken Pack-a-Punch piece there.
3. Reach the upper castle / bastion Pack-a-Punch point and interact with it.
4. Use the teleporter to access the rocket test site and interact with the Pack-a-Punch piece there. (The order of interactions does not matter. You can change it)
5. After all 3 interactions are completed, Pack-a-Punch is rebuilt.
6. From then on, the machine rotates between those 3 locations.

### steps
1. Turn on the power.
2. Progress through the castle until you can use the teleporter route.
3. Take the teleporter to the rocket test site for the first time.
4. While there, interact with the rocket-side Pack-a-Punch piece if you have not done so already.

## rocket launch sequence

The rocket platform is not always safe. At certain times, a rocket test / launch sequence can happen.

When that happens:
- get off the exposed platform area
- move into the safe room
- wait until the launch sequence ends before going back out


## Wunderspheres / landing pads / jump pads

After progressing further, you can also use the Wunderspheres / landing pads for fast travel. These are often just called jump pads or jumpers.

Important note:
- the jump pads must be activated before they can be used

The pad routes are:

- Upper Courtyard → Lower Courtyard
- Lower Courtyard → Upper Courtyard
- Bastion → Rocket Platform
- Rocket Platform → Tram Station

Related images:
- images/pap_power/jumppad_lander.jpeg
- images/pap_power/jumper.jpeg

### usage notes
- these become a major movement shortcut once active
- the Bastion → Rocket Platform route is especially important for returning to the rocket area quickly
- the Rocket Platform → Tram Station route helps you get back from the rocket side efficiently
- the Upper / Lower Courtyard pads make normal castle movement much faster

## full early progression summary

### standard opening route
1. Activate Spawns landing pad
2. Open the map toward power.
3. Turn on the power.
4. Reach the Undercroft / pyramid room.
5. Start rebuilding Pack-a-Punch by interacting with its separated pieces.
6. Use the teleporter to reach the rocket test site for the first time.
7. Interact with the rocket-site Pack-a-Punch point.
8. Activate the landing pad
9. Interact with the upper castle / bastion Pack-a-Punch point.
10. After all 3 interactions are complete, use Pack-a-Punch as it rotates between those locations.
