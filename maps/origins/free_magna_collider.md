# Free Magna Collider

## Side easter egg - Free MagnaCollider

You can, with the help of the Maxis drone, obtain a free Pack-a-Punched MG08/15

To get it, you will have to move the drone near 4 locations where stands circular targets. The drone will pick them up.

You will eventually have to recharge the drone.

The targets are spotted:

- On a broken floor of the church, accessible above the arch from where the tank exits.
  Related image: images/source-guide/9193358a5737c7e17ef52b480bd62e6007f53ce6-2dfc61cdbe.webp
- On a platform, above the excavation site, on the site facing generator 5
  Related image: images/source-guide/6fa6244acda8f754fb09bfc692cade2c91845171-6f23e23ad3.webp
- On a wooden plank near generator 5
  Related image: images/source-guide/bb54d4e0984b88df9a134a033c7bfd08b10204b5-e086792808.webp
- Inside a crumbled burning alley located right after the spawn, heading to generator 2
  Related image: images/source-guide/4f73fc36ec29c379d7d6bd2e159b43a9626d91ae-14f9653e02.webp

Once done, the drone will drop a PaP-ed machine gun in front of the Pack-a-Punch machine.
