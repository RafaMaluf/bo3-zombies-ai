# main ee + annihilator

## overview

The main easter egg on The Giant is the Fly Trap / Paradoxical Prologue. It is a short quest that rewards you with the Annihilator specialist weapon.

This easter egg is much simpler than the quests on the larger BO3 maps. You only need:
- any Pack-a-Punched weapon

After that, you shoot the hidden panel, then shoot 3 teddy bears, and finally pick up the Annihilator from the furnace.

## requirements

Before starting the easter egg, make sure you have:
- Pack-a-Punched any weapon

## step 1 - shoot the hidden panel

Go to the Pack-a-Punch area and stand near the machine. From there, shoot the hidden panel with any Pack-a-Punched weapon.

The easiest way to understand where to stand and where to aim is by following the marked map.

### Steps
1. Unlock Pack-a-Punch.
2. Pack-a-Punch any weapon.
3. Go between the pap area and teleporter A.
4. Stand in the marked position.
5. Shoot the hidden panel marked with the yellow X using your Pack-a-Punched weapon.

## step 2 - shoot the 3 teddy bears

After shooting the hidden panel, you will hear a voice line and the quest will continue. Now you need to find and shoot the 3 teddy bears around the map. Use the map to find the locations

### Steps
1. Find the first teddy bear and shoot it.  
   Related image: images/ee/second-shot.jpg
2. Find the second teddy bear and shoot it.  
   Related image: images/ee/third-shot.jpg
3. Find the third teddy bear and shoot it.  
   Related image: images/ee/fourth-shot.jpg

Important note:
- after the panel is activated, just shoot all 3 bears to complete the quest

## step 3 - collect the Annihilator

Once all 3 teddy bears have been shot, go to the Furnace Room and collect the Annihilator.

### Steps
1. Go to the Furnace Room.
2. Pick up the Annihilator from the furnace.

### Related image
- images/ee/annihilator_in_furnace.jpg

## Annihilator

The Annihilator is the reward for completing the main easter egg on The Giant. It acts as a specialist weapon.

### Important notes
- it has limited ammo
- you need kills to recharge it
- Max Ammo does not refill it
- it is a strong reward, but it is still a limited-use weapon, not a permanent infinite-ammo solution
