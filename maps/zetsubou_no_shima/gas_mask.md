# gas mask

## overview

The Gas Mask is a very useful support item on Zetsubou No Shima. It does 2 main things:

- protects you from toxic spore gas so you stop coughing
- lets you stay underwater longer before taking damage
- if you dont use it you wont get to the boss fight. you die before

After collecting all 3 parts, you can craft it at a workbench in **Lab A** or **Lab B**.

## part 1 - cable

The first part is a **cable** found in the crash site area.

### possible locations
- on a rock beside the Mystery Box spot
- right outside the plane, on one side
- right outside the plane, on the other side

## crafting the gas mask

After collecting all 3 parts, go to a crafting table and build the Gas Mask.

### crafting table locations
- **Lab A**
- **Lab B**

### steps
1. Collect the cable from the crash site area.
2. Collect the mask from the green water area.
3. Collect the filter from the outdoor room near power.
4. Go to a workbench in Lab A or Lab B.
5. Build the Gas Mask.

## practical note

The Gas Mask is one of the most useful utility items on the map because Zetsubou constantly forces you to deal with:
- spores
- toxic gas
- underwater sections

It is especially useful for:
- safer movement through spore-heavy areas
- longer underwater routing
- overall easter egg and setup consistency
