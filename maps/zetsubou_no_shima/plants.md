# plants

## overview

Plants are one of the biggest systems on Zetsubou No Shima. By planting seeds and watering them over multiple rounds, you can grow different plant types with very different uses.

In general:
- zombies can drop **seeds**
- you plant the seed in a growing spot
- you can water it **once per round**
- your bucket has **3 charges**
- if a seed is **not watered for a round**, it still progresses by itself
- using the **KT-4 / Masamune** on a plant before its final growth can fertilize it and improve some outcomes

Important note:
- **Rainbow Water** should generally not be treated as a normal plant-growth color. It acts like a random water color, so it is not reliable for controlled plant outcomes. It is better reserved for specific advanced uses like the Masamune setup.

## basic no-water plant

If you plant a seed and do **not** water it, it becomes a basic orange / yellow pod on the following round.

### possible contents
- 1 explosive grenade
- zombie
- MR6
- Sheiva
- Locus
- XM-53
- Blood Money blue (+500 to single player)
- Blood Money green (+500 to all players)

## blue water plant

Using **blue water** grows the loot plant path.

### growth odds
- water once → next round small growth → following round **1 in 3** chance to become the blue-water plant
- water twice over two rounds → **2 in 3** chance
- water three times over three rounds → **guaranteed**

### standard blue-water plant rewards
- Razorback
- HG-40
- Man-O-War
- Gorgon
- Haymaker
- Brecci
- MX Garand
- Nuke
- Carpenter
- Double Points
- Insta-Kill
- Fire Sale
- Death Machine
- Blood Money blue
- Blood Money green

Related image: images/plants/bluewaterplant.jpg

## fertilized blue water plant

If you fertilize the blue-water plant with the **KT-4** before its final growth, it has a 50/50 chance to produce the upgraded yellow-spotted version.

### possible rewards
- Bowie Knife
- Marshal 16
- Ray Gun
- Monkey Bombs
- Shell for the main easter egg step
- Death Machine
- Double Points
- Max Ammo
- Blood Money
- Mimic Plant
- Extra perk slot bottle

Related image: images/plants/fertilizedbluewaterplant.jpg

## green water plant

Using **green water** grows the claw plant.

### growth odds
- water once → next round small growth → following round **1 in 3** chance
- water twice over two rounds → **2 in 3** chance
- water three times over three rounds → **guaranteed**

### ability
- stabs and holds a zombie or spider in place
- confirmed duration around **10 minutes in solo**
- does **not** affect Thrashers

Related image: images/plants/greenwaterplant.jpg

## fertilized green water plant

If you fertilize the green-water plant with the KT-4 before final growth, it has a 50/50 chance to become the upgraded version.

### ability
- stabs and holds a zombie or spider much longer
- confirmed duration around **60 minutes in solo**
- does **not** affect Thrashers

Related image: images/plants/fertilizedgreenwaterplant.jpg

## purple water plant

Using **purple water** grows the zombie-eating plant.

### growth odds
- water once → next round small growth → following round **1 in 3** chance
- water twice over two rounds → **2 in 3** chance
- water three times over three rounds → **guaranteed**

### ability
- attracts zombies similarly to a Monkey Bomb
- can also attract Thrashers
- slowly kills nearby zombies
- instant-kills Thrashers
- can also kill spiders if they get close enough

Notes:
- the guide notes this plant may sometimes glitch and die shortly after spawning

Related image: images/plants/purplewaterplant.jpg

## fertilized purple water plant

If you fertilize the purple-water plant before final growth, it has a 50/50 chance to become the upgraded version.

### ability
- stronger version of the zombie-eating plant
- attracts more enemies than the normal version
- still instant-kills Thrashers
- can still kill spiders that get too close

Related image: images/plants/fertilizedpurplewaterplant.jpg

## fruit plant

To grow a fruit plant, you must use **1 of each water color**:
- blue
- green
- purple

The order does **not** matter.

### growth process
- plant seed
- water once with the first color
- next round water with the second color
- next round water with the third color
- after final growth, there is a **50/50 chance** to become a fruit plant

### normal fruit plant ability
- 50% chance to gain a random perk
- 50% chance to vomit / fail

Important note:
- there is no perk limit from the plant reward itself

Related image: images/plants/fruitplant.jpg

## fertilized fruit plant

If you fertilize the fruit plant before final growth with the KT-4, it has a 50/50 chance to become the upgraded version.

### upgraded fruit plant ability
- 100% chance to give a random perk
- no chance to fail with the vomit outcome

Related image: images/plants/fertilizedfruitplant.jpg

## mimic plant / clone plant

The Mimic Plant can be created in 2 ways.

### method 1
Through the fertilized blue-water upgraded plant path.

### method 2
No-water fertilized method:
- plant seed
- fertilize with KT-4
- next round fertilize again
- next round fertilize again
- small chance to become a Mimic Plant

### ability
When activated, it saves your current:
- perks
- weapons
- setup state

If you later die without Quick Revive saving you first, you respawn inside the Mimic Plant with that stored setup.

Important notes:
- if you create a second Mimic Plant while one is already active, the new one can replace the old one
- in solo, if you gain a Quick Revive after activating the Mimic Plant, that can still count against your normal solo Quick Revive uses

Related image: images/plants/cloneplant.jpg

## mixing colors

If you mix only **2 colors** during the watering process, the result becomes weighted but not guaranteed.

Example:
- watering once with green
- watering twice with blue

This would give:
- **1 in 3** chance for the green plant path
- **2 in 3** chance for the blue plant path

Important note:
- fertilizing can still affect the final upgraded result when applicable

## practical summary

### no water
- basic orange / yellow pod

### 3x blue
- loot plant

### 3x fertilized blue
- upgraded loot plant with high-value rewards

### 3x green
- claw plant that traps zombies / spiders

### 3x fertilized green
- longer-lasting claw plant

### 3x purple
- zombie-eating plant

### 3x fertilized purple
- stronger zombie-eating plant

### 1 blue + 1 green + 1 purple
- fruit plant chance

### mixed 2-color watering
- weighted random result based on counts used

### repeated fertilization / special route
- possible Mimic Plant
