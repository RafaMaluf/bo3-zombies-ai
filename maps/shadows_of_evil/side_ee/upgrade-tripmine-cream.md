# upgraded trip mines - cream cakes

Buy Trip Mines near the left side of the boxing gym. To turn them into cream cakes, place Trip Mines near the 4 Devo Donut food carts and let a zombie explode near each one. After each successful cart, you hear an angelic audio cue.
