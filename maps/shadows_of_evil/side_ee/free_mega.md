# free random Mega Gobblegum

## Requirements
- open the Rift
- physically pick up Widow's Wine from its machine location in the underground

## Steps
1. Throw Widow's Wine grenades into all 4 lion mouths above Canals.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lion_widows.jpg
2. A white Gobblegum appears from one of the lion mouths.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lion_gum.jpg
3. Place it on the plate in the candy shop window near Stamin-Up.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/plate_gum.jpg
4. Progress a few rounds.
5. Pick it up and receive a random Mega Gobblegum not currently equipped.
