# trials

## overview

The **Trials of the Ancients** are a challenge system on Zetsubou No Shima. There are **16 possible trials** in total, but each player only gets **3 random trials per game**.

Each pillar shows 3 challenge slots:
- **bottom** = easy
- **middle** = medium
- **top** = hard

Your own trial symbols appear **yellow**, while other players' trial symbols appear **blue**.

Completing the 3 trials gives 3 rewards:

- **Bottom trial reward:** Max Ammo
- **Middle trial reward:** random weapon reward, usually **Dingo**, **SVG-100**, or **205 Brecci**
- **Top trial reward:** Empty Perk Bottle

Important note:
- if you do not like the middle weapon reward, you can let it drop back into the altar and reopen it until you get the one you want
- the result is random, and the same weapon can appear multiple times in a row

## electric shield requirement

After completing **all 3 trials** and collecting **all 3 rewards**, lightning will occasionally strike near the ritual area.

If you hold out your shield when the lightning strikes, you can **electrify the shield**.

Important co-op note:
- in co-op, **all players** must complete their own 3 trials and collect their rewards before anyone can get the electric shield

The electric shield is required for:
- part of the **Masamune** setup
- **Spider Transformation**
- the **Assault with Battery** achievement / trophy
- the **Seeds of Doubt** achievement / trophy progression

## easy trials

### use a spore to regain air underwater
Destroy a spore while underwater and move into the cloud it creates. Instead of coughing, you regain air.

### destroy 5 spores
Destroy any 5 spores on the map. Shooting or melee works.

### destroy 5 webs with explosives
Destroy 5 webs using explosives. Frag grenades work especially well because they stick to the web. Ray Gun shots also count.

Common web locations:
- GobbleGum machines
- Perk machines
- pathways blocked by spider webs

Related image: images/trials/web.jpg

### water 5 plants
Water plants 5 times total.

### water the same plant 3 times
Water one plant across 3 rounds until it has been watered 3 times.

## medium trials

### kill 20 zombies underwater
Kill 20 zombies in underwater sections of the map.

Useful places:
- power room area
- underwater cavern near Mule Kick

### kill 15 zombies attracted to a plant
Grow a **purple-water zombie-eating plant**, then kill the zombies while they are being attracted to it, before the plant kills them itself.

### kill 10 spore-infected zombies with headshots
Lead zombies through a spore cloud so they become infected, then kill them with headshots.

Important note:
- Insta-Kill headshots also count

### obtain a power-up from a plant
Grow a **blue-water reward plant** and collect any power-up from it.

### kill 20 zombies with the fan trap
Use the fan trap and get 20 kills with it.

Related image: images/trials/fantrap.jpg

## hard trials

### kill 8 spiders with spores
During a spider round, lead spiders into spore clouds so the cloud kills them.

### shoot the heads off 5 mutated zombies
Kill 5 Thrashers by destroying their heads instead of only their weak spores.

### eat a fruit grown by a plant
Grow a **fruit plant** using one of each water color:
- blue
- green
- purple

Then eat the fruit.

Important note:
- do **not** fertilize this one with the KT-4 if your goal is specifically this trial, because you are more likely to get a cloning / respawn-style plant instead of the normal fruit outcome

### use the plane / propeller trap to kill 30 zombies
Lead zombies into the plane / propeller trap and get 30 kills with it.

Related image: images/trials/planetrap.jpg

### kill 3 zombies while they are mutating
Kill a zombie while it is in the short transformation phase of becoming a Thrasher, before it gets dragged underground.

Practical note:
- this is one of the most annoying trials in the whole set
- using spores and being ready to instantly kill the zombie is the best method
- the bunker opening sequence guarantees at least one mutation chance early

## full list of possible trials

### medium
- Kill 20 zombies underwater

- Kill 8 spiders with spores
- Shoot the heads off 5 mutated zombies

## practical advice by challenge type

### easiest trials to clear fast
- destroy 5 spores
- water 5 plants
- water the same plant 3 times
- use a spore to regain air underwater

### trials that benefit from planning ahead
- obtain a power-up from a plant
- eat a fruit grown by a plant
- kill zombies attracted to a plant
- kill spiders with spores
- kill zombies while mutating

### trials tied to trap locations
- fan trap challenge
- plane / propeller trap challenge

## full progression summary

1. Go to the trial altar area.
2. Check your 3 assigned challenges.
3. Complete the easy, medium, and hard trials.
4. Collect the 3 rewards.
5. If playing co-op, make sure every player also completes all 3 and takes all rewards.
6. Hold your shield out near the ritual area when lightning strikes to electrify it.
