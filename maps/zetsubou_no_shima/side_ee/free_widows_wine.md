# free widows wine

## overview

You can get **Widow's Wine** for free after defeating the giant spider during the Masamune / spider tooth step.

This is tied directly to the giant spider boss encounter.

## steps

1. Start the giant spider fight during the Masamune setup.
2. Defeat the spider.
3. Follow the exit path behind the arena.
4. On the way out, enter the purple pool.
5. Collect the free **Widow's Wine** perk.

- the image for this reward is already used in the Masamune guide and should be referenced from there
