# One Inch Punch

## Equipment - One inch punch

The One Inch Punch is an upgrade to your melee attack. To obtain it, you must fill 4 chests located in 4 different giant robot footsteps.

You just need to kill zombies around it, however if a giant robot walks on the chest, you will need to restart the process. When a chest is done, it will disappear, saving your progress. You should be focusing on one chest at a time.

The chests are:

- In Odin's footstep near generator 5
  Related image: images/source-guide/7d41050b1adec05a757127d402a8fe03f5c4befe-0319835fe3.webp
- In Odin's footstep near the excavation site
  Related image: images/source-guide/a938743d80e58ba2a99cdd1baf23cb8d58961cc0-21a8431c11.webp
- In Odin's footstep near generator 4
  Related image: images/source-guide/a047e0b7085c9770810a5bb26aadf4b12c486b93-55ce057ac6.webp
- In Freya's footstep between the church and the excavation site
  Related image: images/source-guide/66a21efe565e9142818f7e1636fa4315fcc3ea76-8bece8929c.webp

Once all chests have been filled, a challenge has been completed, you only need to pick up your One Inch Punch on a reward chest, there is one on the spawn and another one near generator 6.

Note that it is possible to upgrade the fists with the main easter egg quest.
