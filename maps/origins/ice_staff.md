# Ice Staff and Ull's Arrow

## Preparation - Ice staff

To craft the Ice staff you will need a blue disk, as well as 3 parts and a crysyal.

The disk is located in the Tank station near generator 2.

It can be either:

- On the table, right in front ot the entrance
  Related image: images/source-guide/98ab60e3b5889450307f07a8bc3011651e6ef507-ebcf2c01da.webp
- On a shelf, at your left when entering the bunker, next to a mystery box location
  Related image: images/source-guide/bd1fa54879180c61dfff9756348fbec9b5efca0c-19b7771436.webp
- On a shelf, at your right after the way leading to the roof
  Related image: images/source-guide/998da8fcae1b72b06b6411ccc0df440065e61a1c-b13c4e6fa4.webp

Concerning the parts for the ice staff, they are acquired all the same way. You need to use the shovel equipment on the piles of dirt around the map but you must dig only when it is snowing.

Related image: images/source-guide/58365724d50de58360f61ae9075ad9215e235bf9-6e3ebefd69.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/3aa92a0248725e3beb75d42456ba73e9805d23cd-9d6c824ebf.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/a377dceb7eff8533fd655c1faf1edc487e2165e4-aefcd6b925.webp

Each part will drop on each part of the map. Basically one in Thor's area, one in Odin's area and one in Freya's area of the map.

For more infos about the shovel and the digging spots, see the part bellow.

To get the ice gem, you will now need to use the blue record with the gramophone, to summon the Ice portal. It is located in the underground tunnel near generator 6. Once in the Crazy place, get the ice gem from the blue altar.

Then, head back under the excavation site and craft the staff on it's altar.

## Preparation - Ull's Arrow (Upgraded ice staff)

Once the Ice staff is done, you will be able to do its upgrade quest.

Head to the crazy place. Around the Ice portal, you will notice two things.

First, there are tiles floating above the area with symbols made with triangles and lines.

Related image: images/source-guide/e166c922c61a2301aac3d05b015ff8ad071ba80b-6f8afa3d47.webp

Second, there is an ice pillar nearby, with a symbol on it, this one made with circular symbols.

Related image: images/source-guide/df5b0b38ec7fe72f960263c7cb593dcd140ec31a-ae289dd826.webp

By using the following picture (Thanks to www.kronorium.com) , you will need to shoot the tile above that is corresponding with the symbol on the pillar. Once a tile has been shot, another symbol will be highlighted.

Related image: images/source-guide/077a6d98128c1482d665b819a383b15f53d8343e-e7228ced26.webp

Once done, you will need to freeze three graves with your staff, then shoot them with bullets. They are located:

- In front of you, when going to the giant footstep where stands a soul chest, from generator 4
  Related image: images/source-guide/001cc4f16d32df7a5b898f38e6af688dc1363978-c559b14537.webp
- On a side of the hill, between the excavation site and the first grave
  Related image: images/source-guide/5cf3c208f36f290224cf292d1f6419510582c775-266a894783.webp
- Outside the map near the destroyed Giant robot, near generator 2
  Related image: images/source-guide/cf3a67a609eae7487159b4919904c37d721b63ce-6c1b0b06c2.webp

Now that this is done, you can proceed to the "Preparation - Final upgrade stage for all staffs" part
