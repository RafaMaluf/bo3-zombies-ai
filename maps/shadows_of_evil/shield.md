# shield

The Rocket Shield is built from 3 parts found in Canals, Waterfront and Footlight, plus a workbench. Each part has 3 possible spawn locations according to the buildables guide.

## part 1 - Canals

This part is in the upstairs perk / Four Lions area of Canals.

### Verified spawns
- Immediately on your right as you enter the Four Lions room from the stairs.
- Against the left railing of the connecting bridge / outside walkway.
- Directly across from the perk machine, to the right of the 1250 door.

## part 2 - Waterfront

This part is in the upstairs perk area / lounge path of Waterfront.

### Verified spawns
- At the top of the stairs as you come up toward the perk area.
- On the wall directly to the right of the perk machine if you are facing the perk.
- Inside the room with the statue, on the right-side wall, left of the 1250 door, on the floor by the debris.

## part 3 - Footlight

This part is in the upstairs perk area of Footlight.

### Verified spawns
- Straight out from the perk machine, against the railing.
- About 2 meters left of the perk machine, by the railing.
- Farther left of the perk machine, in the doorway by the stairs, on a chair.

## upgraded shield / Goddard Apparatus

To upgrade the shield, get repeated shield boost kills through groups of 10 or more zombies. The requirement is commonly described as 12 successful bursts through groups of 10 or more, with an audio confirmation when done correctly.
