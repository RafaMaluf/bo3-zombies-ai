# power + bunker + pap

## overview

On Zetsubou No Shima, power progression is more involved than on most maps. You do not just walk to a switch and turn the facility on. First, you must fill **2 generators with water**, then quickly enter the bunker before they run dry, survive the underwater section, and only then reach the actual power switch.

Pack-a-Punch is also a small setup quest. You must collect **3 parts**, then return to the Pack-a-Punch area and interact with the 3 control panels around the machine to drain the water and unlock access.

## power - fill the 2 generators

To begin the power setup, you need a **bucket with water**. The color of the water does not matter for this step.

There are 2 generators:
- one in **Lab A**
- one in **Lab B**

Once you fill one generator, you have limited time to fill the other before they run dry.

### steps
1. Get a bucket and fill it with water.
2. Go to one generator and fill it.
3. Quickly move to the second generator and fill it as well.
4. After both are active, head to the main bunker.

## bunker entry

Once both generators are filled, the bunker becomes accessible. You need to move there before the generators run dry.

When you enter, the bunker door opens at no cost and infected zombies will be inside. This also guarantees a swamp monster spawn.

### steps
1. After filling both generators, go to the main bunker.
2. Enter through the now-open bunker door.
3. Push deeper into the bunker toward the final power route.

Related image:
- images/power_bunker_pap/3bunkerdoor.jpg

## underwater bunker step

Inside the bunker,  open the door on the left and go as deep as possible. One vent is not working correctly. To continue, you must go underwater and clear the web. This closes the door and forces you to survive until it reopens.

After surviving that section, you come out of the water and the actual power switch is directly in front of you.

Important note:
- A later comment on the guide suggests this step is effectively tied to killing the enemies that spawn during the underwater section, not just waiting passively.

### steps
1. Progress through the bunker until you reach the underwater section.
2. Remove the web on the left.
3. Survive the encounter in the underwater area until the route reopens.
4. Exit the water.
5. Flip the power switch in front of you.

## pack-a-punch overview

To unlock Pack-a-Punch, you must collect **3 parts** and then use the 3 control panels around the machine.

Once the 3 parts are installed, the water around Pack-a-Punch is drained and the machine becomes accessible.

## pack-a-punch part 1 - underwater fan area

The first Pack-a-Punch part is dropped underwater after you clear the fan area from webs and get the zombies down. According to the guide, this part can be picked up even before activating power.

### steps
1. Reach the underwater fan area.
2. Clear the web / obstruction there.
3. Complete the underwater encounter.
4. Pick up the dropped Pack-a-Punch part.

Related image: images/power_bunker_pap/6pap1parstunderwatertoo.jpg

## pack-a-punch part 2 - cocoon drop

The second Pack-a-Punch part is randomly dropped by the cocoons in the underground room with the purple water source.

### steps
1. Go to the underground area with the purple water.
2. Melee / knife the cocoons.
3. Zombies may come out of them.
4. Keep checking until the Pack-a-Punch part drops.
5. Pick it up.

Related images:
- images/power_bunker_pap/7pap2partbodiestoknife.jpg
- images/power_bunker_pap/8part2dropped.jpg

## pack-a-punch part 3 - valve in the submerged caverns

The third part is a **valve** found in the submerged caverns where you can also find the flower used for the KT-4. The guide specifically notes that it **cannot be in the blue tunnel**.

### steps
1. Go into the submerged caverns.
2. Search the cavern routes for the valve.
3. Remember that it cannot be in the blue tunnel.
4. Pick up the valve once found.

Related image: images/power_bunker_pap/9papthirddrop.jpg

## unlocking pack-a-punch

After collecting all 3 parts, return to the Pack-a-Punch machine. There are 3 control panels around the fence. Interact with all of them to drain the water and open access to the machine.

### steps
1. Return to the Pack-a-Punch area with all 3 parts.
2. Install / use the 3 parts there.
3. Interact with each of the 3 control panels around the fence.
4. Drain the water.
5. Access Pack-a-Punch.

Related image: images/power_bunker_pap/10wheretoputparts.jpg

## full setup summary

1. Get a bucket and fill it with water.
2. Fill the generator in Lab A.
3. Fill the generator in Lab B before the first one runs dry.
4. Enter the bunker.
5. Clear the underwater web section and survive the encounter.
6. Flip the power switch.
7. Collect the first Pack-a-Punch part from the underwater fan area.
8. Collect the second Pack-a-Punch part from the cocoons in the purple-water underground room.
9. Collect the valve from the submerged caverns.
10. Return to Pack-a-Punch.
11. Interact with the 3 control panels to drain the water and unlock the machine.
