# general

Shadows of Evil is built around Beast Mode, rituals, the underground Rift / Sacred Place, and district-based progression instead of a normal power switch. The map is effectively split into Spawn / Easy Street, Junction, Canals, Footlight, Waterfront, and the underground Rift. The 4 rituals can be done in any order, ritual items can be picked up in any order, but only one ritual can be active at a time. After the 2nd and 4th completed district rituals, a Margwa spawns. The full main easter egg can be progressed with any player count until the last step, but the final completion requires 4 players.

## Key map systems

- Beast Mode is used to break crates, grapple, shock switches, and open ritual access.
- The Summoning Key is required for rituals.
- Pack-a-Punch is opened only after the 4 district rituals and the underground ritual.
- The sword can be started once you can access the symbol wall / egg room path.
- Civil Protector, shield, and Apothicon Servant are optional but extremely helpful for the easter egg.
