# valkyrie helmet

## overview

The Valkyrie Helmet is a wearable item on Gorod Krovi.

It gives:
- 30% more damage against Valkyrie Drones
- 50% less damage taken from Valkyries
- 50% less electricity damage taken

## unlock requirement

To unlock it, you must:
- destroy 6 Valkyrie cameras
- destroy all 3 arms / tentacles on Valkyries 6 times

A creepy sound plays when each requirement is completed.

## steps

1. Fight Valkyrie Drones.
2. Destroy the red camera light on 6 Valkyries.
3. Destroy all 3 arms / tentacles on 6 Valkyries.
4. After both conditions are complete, go to Department Store.
5. Find the mannequin with the German military jacket.
6. Pick up the Valkyrie Helmet.
