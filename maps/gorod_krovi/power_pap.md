# power + pap

## overview

Unlocking Pack-a-Punch on Gorod Krovi is a short setup chain built around:
- turning on **power**
- using the **code cylinder** system
- defending **Groph Pods**
- placing the **Network Circuits** in the bunker underground
- calling a **dragon** to the Hatchery

This is one of the first major routes you should learn on the map, because it also opens access to other important progression steps later.

## step 1 - turn on power

The power switch is located in **Dragon Command**.

Turning it on also activates **S.O.P.H.I.A.**, which is important if you plan to do the easter egg.

### steps
1. Open the map toward **Dragon Command**.
2. Reach the power switch.
3. Turn the power on.

Related image: images/power_pap/1powerswitch.jpg

## step 2 - collect code cylinders

After power is on, zombies can begin dropping **code cylinders**.

These cylinders come in 3 colors:
- **blue**
- **yellow**
- **red**

Each one belongs to a specific machine.

### steps
1. Kill zombies until a code cylinder drops.
2. Pick up the cylinder.
3. Take it to the correct machine based on its color.

Related image: images/power_pap/2cylinderdropped.jpg

## step 3 - use the correct machine

Each cylinder color has its own decryption machine:

- **Blue machine** = **Dragon Command**
- **Yellow machine** = **Supply Depot**
- **Red machine** = **Tank Factory**

When you insert the correct cylinder into its machine, it launches a **Groph Pod** somewhere on the map.

### machine locations

#### blue machine - Dragon Command
Related image: images/power_pap/3machinedragoncommand.jpg

#### yellow machine - Supply Depot
Related image: images/power_pap/4machinesupplydepot.jpg

#### red machine - Tank Factory
Related image: images/power_pap/5machinetankfactory.jpg

## step 4 - find the Groph Pod

Once a cylinder is inserted, a Groph Pod lands somewhere on the map. You can find it by looking for the **green beam in the sky**.

### steps
1. Look around the map for the green sky beam.
2. Go to the Groph Pod location.
3. Prepare to defend it.

Related image: images/power_pap/6grophrayinthesky.jpg

## step 5 - defend the Groph Pod

When you reach the Groph Pod, kill zombies around it until it opens.

After enough kills, it opens and gives you a **Network Circuit**.

### steps
1. Stand near the Groph Pod.
2. Kill zombies around it.
3. Keep defending until the pod opens.
4. Pick up the Network Circuit.

Related images:
- images/power_pap/7grophtoprotect.jpg
- images/power_pap/8pickuppiece.jpg

## step 6 - place the Network Circuits in the bunker

After collecting the required Network Circuits, go to the bunker underground and place them there.

This enables the dragon network for travel to the Hatchery.

### steps
1. Go underground into the bunker.
2. Find the circuit placement area.
3. Insert the Network Circuits.

Related image: images/power_pap/9wheretoplacebunker.jpg

## step 7 - call the dragon

After the dragon network is active, you can call a dragon from the dragon travel stations.

The 3 dragon stations are near the same areas as the cylinder machines:
- Dragon Command
- Supply Depot
- Tank Factory

Calling a dragon costs **500 points**.

### steps
1. Go to one of the dragon call stations.
2. Pay 500 points.
3. Ride the dragon to the Hatchery.

Related image: images/power_pap/10calldragon.jpg

## step 8 - access pack-a-punch

After the dragon ride cinematic, you arrive in the **Hatchery**, where Pack-a-Punch is located.

If you want to leave, go downstairs and jump into the sewers.

### result
- Pack-a-Punch is now accessible
- you can return later using the dragon network again

Related image: images/power_pap/11packapunch.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Turn on power in Dragon Command.
2. Kill zombies until a code cylinder drops.
3. Insert the cylinder into the correct machine.
4. Follow the green beam to the Groph Pod.
5. Defend the pod and collect the Network Circuit.
6. Repeat until you have enough circuits.
7. Place the circuits in the bunker underground.
8. Use a dragon station and pay 500 points.
9. Ride the dragon to the Hatchery.
10. Access Pack-a-Punch.
