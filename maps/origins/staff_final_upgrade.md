# Final staff upgrade stage

## Preparation - Final upgrade stage for all staffs

Once your dedicated quest is done, a ray of light should be emitted from the excavation site.

Related image: images/source-guide/5e7c07d6c64ef0a20db8ac73cfe2097358e887fe-e864d9e006.webp

Head back there. Above the 4 altars stands 4 stone circles, with a colored ball, depending of your element. You firstly need to use 4 levers in the underground, to rotate the stone circles.

Related image: images/source-guide/a93c679575aaae0be0ed6b13e5b1a7e58727d6ff-26550b8ace.webp

When moving them, you'll notice they have got 4 positions, linked with colors. (Red -> Fire, Blue -> Ice, Purple -> Lightning and Yellow -> Wind). You need to match all rings to the color of the staff you want to upgrade. When all rings are matched, shoot at the colored ball in the center with the staff.

Related image: images/source-guide/f13197969286c7ba01b465f00474acc1632341a7-52e0aa00ba.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/c01bbf8a90c84a3885504eca30d45bda9b257311-87b7af3a5e.webp

Finally, head to the crazy place and put your staff in the altar , above the orb you just teleported. You'll need to kill zombies to fill it . It will eventually be upgraded and you will be able to take it back.
