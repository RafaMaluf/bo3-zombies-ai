# Origins challenges

## Challenges

Two boxes (One at generator 1 the other at generator 6) displays you the progression of 4 challenges. Each one of them will grant you a reward you will need to pick up on one of the two chests.

- Perform 115 Headshots Easily done, will grant you a Pack-a-Punched rifle. Needs to be headshots on normal zombies, not electrical ones nor Panzersoldaten.
- Activate 6 generators This doesn't need you to activate THE 6 generators, but to succesfully activate 6 of them. Understand that if a players activates 5 generators and the second player activate the last, the first player will need to wait for a crusader squad to deactivate one of them. Will grant you a Max Ammo
- Spend 30.000 points Requires time. Will reward you with Double Tap perk
- Fill the 4 soul chests up Not really hard either, will grant you with the One Inch Punch melee upgrade.

Activating generator 1 will provide you a zombie blood bonus in the first chest. Activating generator 6 will provide you with a fire staff part.
