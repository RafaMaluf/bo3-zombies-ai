# Space monkey rounds

## Information - Special rounds

Ascension's special round is not the classical round like Kino or Eisendrache. Hellhounds will not come, instead zombie monkeys will be dropping on the map.

The particularity of those monkeys is that they will jump on the perk machines. Eventually, they will steal you this very perk that you will have to buy back.

The closer you are from the monkeys, the higher damages they will "deal" to the perk machine. Once the "health bar" of the machine is empty, every player owning the perk will loose it. You will know which machine is being attacked with the perk logo blinking in your "inventory".

Note that this special round will come only if the power is on and if at least one perk has been bought .

Depending on how much players you are, you will have to defend your most important perks. Mostly Juggernog, Speed Cola, ...

If no perk machine has been damaged, the last monkey will drop the usual max ammo bonus AND a free perk for everybody.
