# music ee

## overview

The music easter egg on The Giant plays Beauty of Annihilation. To activate it, you need to interact with 3 hidden musical objects around the map.

Using the map with the marked locations is the easiest way to understand where all 3 objects are.

## how to activate the song

### Steps
1. Interact with the first music object.  
   Related image: images/music/music_1.jpg
2. Interact with the second music object.  
   Related image: images/music/music_2.jpg
3. Interact with the third music object.  
   Related image: images/music/music_3.jpg

After interacting with all 3, Beauty of Annihilation will begin to play.

## location help

The easiest way to find all 3 objects is to use the marked map.
