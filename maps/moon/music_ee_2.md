# Nightmare secret song

## Side easter egg - Secret song 2

Actually this part will show you three different secret songs. They are 8-bit versions of other songs.

You simply have to interact three times with a console. Power must be turned on first.

- The first console will trigger the 8-bit version of Coming Home. It is located in the spawn room, near the wall facing tunnel 11.
  Related image: images/source-guide/e837a7c7df871c26bb11982ff19b5356ef69f556-7655dd3741.webp
- The second console will play an 8-bit version of Damned. It is located at the end of Tunnel 11, before the stairs leading to the power lever.
  Related image: images/source-guide/22cecf886cdd7cb94279b1eb2c0222eff754e70a-4c4e7ec4db.webp
- The third console will play an 8-bit version of Pareidolia. It is located in the lab's upper floor, next to Deadshot Daiquiri.
  Related image: images/source-guide/e69cdaf2878aa5424e5ec6a90ecd219b3c6078ec-2dc5cb0d4e.webp
