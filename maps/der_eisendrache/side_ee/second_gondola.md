# second gondola

## overview

The gondola panel in spawn normally gives a small reward when you use a fuse. There is also a hidden second gondola that gives much better supplies.

To unlock it, you must throw all your grenades into the starting gondola before it leaves. Then keep using fuses as normal. On the fifth fuse use, the second gondola should appear instead.

## steps

1. At the beginning of the match, throw all your grenades into the gondola before it leaves.
2. Continue playing and inserting fuses into the spawn panel as usual.
3. Keep track of your fuse uses.
4. On the fifth fuse, the second gondola should be summoned instead.
5. Use it to get the improved reward.

## important note

This side easter egg does not require a dedicated image in your current setup.
