# Power and pressure-plate Pack-a-Punch

## Preparation - Activating Power - Accessing Pack-a-Punch

First of all, you'll need to activate the power on the map.

To turn the power on you must activates power switches. Both of them are located underground in a large room with 2 wooden wheels. From the spawn room, just pick a side and open doors until you end up in this place.

Related image: images/source-guide/6ae2ffb81ad6ab18f9a00e71651a8977da33d338-665e52d7a3.webp

Now that the power is on, you should notice that there are 4 statues scattered around the map.

They are:

- In the middle of the spawning area

Related image: images/source-guide/3823a9a0a3438665aa9264650dd329435a5a847b-90115447a4.webp

- In the tunnel going into the underground cavern, second door on the right of the spawn, while facing PaP

Related image: images/source-guide/27eef05e672be47c6bbb2acebe90657e4f00bfb7-070c3cd1a1.webp

- On the hill right after the wooden bridge

Related image: images/source-guide/15f6ef7d85d984e3941a4fee95049c62911b6fab-42efd450aa.webp

- Between the two power levers

Related image: images/source-guide/1de89266e911930f7b588839e6c6119609f49c89-ed9a06b244.webp

Each plates will prevent a quarter of the statue to stop spinning. Once the statue is immobile, a countdown will begin and the Pack-a-Punch willl be accessible.

Related image: images/source-guide/dec6d3e3bf1b08cad9f810cd726530567cbdf1d9-77abc4b61a.webp

When the timer is finished, water will push player out of the PaP area and the staircase will disappear.
