# Nacht der Untoten achievement

## Achievement

I said we're CLOSED!

DIfficulty: Easy

For I said we're CLOSED!, prevent every zombie from entering the building throughout rounds 1, 2 and 3 in one match. Guard the five starting-room windows and repair their boards between waves. Kill outside the barriers; a zombie that has already climbed inside fails the requirement. Divide the windows in co-op. GobbleGums are optional.
