# Power, teleporter and Pack-a-Punch

## Activating the power - Getting to the Pack-a-Punch

The power isn't really a big deal to turn on there. Simply open doors until you reach the backstage. The main switch lies there.

Related image: images/source-guide/5df7093b2ffa4b7452163f885b0926688bbcb945-5010f1159c.webp

Once the power is on, the curtain will open and you will be able to acces the teleporter. Get in it, and turn it on.

Related image: images/source-guide/50421d12edbc662ecbc1bb1d3b3dcc9897060891-2a2430d206.webp

Now, head back to the spawning room, and press action on the platform to link it with the teleporter.

Related image: images/source-guide/7c7f103d2a16aea71b796d69ed0ac47d77280841-86965918cb.webp

Finally head back to the teleporter, you will be able to use it. It will then teleport you up on the projection room, where the Pack-a-Punch stands. After about a minute, you will be teleported either on spawn, or in a side room for a little time.
