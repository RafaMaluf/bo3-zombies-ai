# Staff crafting setup

## Preparation - Setting-up staff crafting

In Origins the wonder weapon is an Elemental staff and there are 4 of them. Fire, Ice, Lightning and Wind. One for each player. To get them however, hitting the mystery box won't make it. You will need 3 parts, a disk and a crystal to craft each staff. To upgraded it you will need to do a step in the Crazy Place, a second one in the real world. We'll explain that later on don't worry.

In order to start crafting the staffs, you will need two things.

Firstly, you'll need a black disk. It is located around the excavation site.

- On top of a small wall, near some crates, on the side facing the church
  Related image: images/source-guide/1281b5915528b01ee47e4a7ea6fa9d935703ab49-33781dc33e.webp
- On the left side of the Pack-a-Punch machine
  Related image: images/source-guide/79d5ccbf205c40e06261f1d53dc2351629efe1c1-9cad457e75.webp
- On the left of the wooden platform leading to the excavation site, right on your left coming from bunker between generators 2 and 3.
  Related image: images/source-guide/7e365c5d61d9fccbeed15aee6fdee3c2f1c5487a-47790961c6.webp

You will also need the gramophone, which will let you open portals, in order to get staff parts. It is located inside the excavation site.

Related image: images/source-guide/f1cf37070562406a0bec43fd9cf41c37a2e47768-0013bf52ac.webp

Use now the grammophone on the table to open the stairs leading to the underground altars.
