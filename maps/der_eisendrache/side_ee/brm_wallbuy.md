# brm wallbuy

## overview

The BRM can be unlocked as a wallbuy on Der Eisendrache for **1800 points**.

To unlock it, you must complete a short anti-gravity wall-run step in the underground pyramid room.

## steps

1. Go to the underground room where the anti-gravity device is located.
2. Wait for anti-gravity to activate.
3. Wall-run on every blue square on the walls.
4. You are allowed to fall and touch the ground during this step.
5. Once all the required blue squares have been activated, the BRM wallbuy becomes available in the same room, next to the crafting table.
