# siegfried gauntlet

## overview

The **Siegfried Gauntlet** is the specialist weapon on Gorod Krovi. It is a dragon mounted on your arm that works like a flamethrower, and it can also be sent out to attack zombies. While the dragon is away, you can still melee with the gauntlet.

To obtain it, you must complete a set of shared egg challenges tied to the **dragon egg**.

## challenge tracking

The Gauntlet progress is tied to your dedicated tomb near spawn.

Important note:
- these challenges are **shared for all players**
- you can check progress by looking at the tomb
- the egg icon also helps show progress as steps are completed

## step 1 - get the dragon egg

Go to the Pack-a-Punch area near the sewer exit and look upward. You should see a large green egg. Shoot it down, wait a moment, and then pick it up.

### steps
1. Reach the Hatchery / Pack-a-Punch area.
2. Look up near the sewer exit.
3. Shoot the green dragon egg down.
4. Wait a few seconds.
5. Pick it up.

Related images:
- images/gauntlet/2eggonroof.jpg

## step 2 - warm up the egg

You must place the egg on one of the dragon nests and heat it up. This can be done either by:
- waiting for a dragon to burn the area
- or using a **Dragon Strike** on the nest

The nests are located:
- near the **Infirmary**
- near the **Armory**
- in front of the **power building**
- near the **tombs in spawn**

### steps
1. Take the egg to one of the nests.
2. Place it there.
3. Let dragon fire hit the nest, or use Dragon Strike on it.
4. Once the egg is heated, leave it alone.

Important note:
- after the egg is warmed, you must complete a full round **away from the nest**
- if you stay too close, the dragon may burn the area again and interfere

Related image: images/gauntlet/3eggnesttowarmit.jpg

## step 3 - kill napalm zombies

Now you need to kill around **15 burning zombies**.

### steps
1. Go to an area that dragons can burn.
2. Wait for dragon fire to ignite zombies.
3. Kill the burning / napalm zombies.
4. Check your egg progress afterward.

## step 4 - kill multiple zombies with one bullet

For this step, you must get multi-kills with a single bullet several times.

Weapons like these help:
- **Drakon**
- **SVG**
- other strong penetrating weapons
- Insta-Kill can also help

### steps
1. Line zombies up.
2. Kill multiple zombies with one bullet.
3. Repeat until the egg progress increases.

## step 5 - melee kill zombies

Now you must get melee kills.

Valid methods include:
- normal knife
- Bowie Knife
- Dragon Shield melee

Around **15 melee kills** should complete the step.

### steps
1. Use melee weapons or shield bash.
2. Kill enough zombies with melee.
3. Check the egg progress.

## step 6 - incubate the egg

Return to the Pack-a-Punch area and place the egg into the incubator underground.

You will be locked in for a short lockdown. Kill zombies until the lockdown ends. After that, end the round, then come back and retrieve the egg.

### steps
1. Go back to the Hatchery.
2. Place the egg into the incubator.
3. Survive the lockdown.
4. End the round.
5. Return and pick up the incubated egg.

Related image: images/gauntlet/5incubateegg.jpg

## step 7 - claim the gauntlet

Once the egg is ready, go back to your grave in Belinski Square and collect the reward.

### steps
1. Return to your tomb near spawn.
2. Interact with it.
3. Claim the **Siegfried Gauntlet**.

Related image: images/gauntlet/7retrieve.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Go to the Hatchery and shoot down the dragon egg.
2. Pick up the egg.
3. Place it on a nest and heat it with dragon fire or Dragon Strike.
4. Leave it and let it cool for a full round.
5. Kill napalm zombies.
6. Kill multiple zombies with single bullets.
7. Get melee kills.
8. Put the egg in the incubator and survive the lockdown.
9. End the round and retrieve the egg.
10. Return to your grave in spawn and collect the Siegfried Gauntlet.
