# jumpscare

## overview

Zetsubou No Shima has a doppelganger jumpscare involving one of the crew characters.

The documented co-op method uses a sniper scope aimed at the FAR-RIGHT test tube in the green tank room, followed by looking around for the double. This guide does not establish a guaranteed solo trigger or a fixed waiting time.

## requirements

- a **sniper rifle**
- at least two players for the documented method

You can get one from:
- the Mystery Box
- the middle trial reward, if it gives one

## steps

1. Get a sniper rifle.
2. Go to the main bunker room with the green tanks.
3. Scope into the centre of the far-right tank.
4. Lower the scope and turn around to look for the double.
5. If done correctly, a hallucinated version of one of the crew may appear in the room.
6. Walk up to it to trigger the jumpscare.
