# Generators and Pack-a-Punch

## Preparation - Accessing Pack-a-Punch

In order to open PaP you will need to activate 6 generators scattered on the map. The activation sequence is similar to Revelation corruptor generators. Paying points will generate crusader zombies, endlessly coming to you until the generator is activated. You might kill them, but walking around is the best option.

Note that if no players are left in the area around the generator, it will slowly discharge. Once activated you are free to leave the are.

The generators are located:

- Generator 1 is in the first Bunker
  Related image: images/source-guide/4dce5e59cc801d9fb445e73b571861847f5103bd-f792372340.webp
- Generator 2 is outside the bunker when you open the door on the right from the spawn
  Related image: images/source-guide/a2270286159938be2a4b10ec709882a3ad583e64-4fa4d85fc7.webp
- Generator 3 is up the trenches when going on the left side from the spawn
  Related image: images/source-guide/7878ba2273756020877b4be463e4bc9c4f817522-7b51e28797.webp
- Generator 4 is on the far left when going to the excavation site, from the big bunker between generators 2 and 3
  Related image: images/source-guide/c3bd6994a01eea2338d750218b0c5110312f782d-7ec6622873.webp
- Generator 5 is on the right side when coming from the big bunker between generators 2 and 3
  Related image: images/source-guide/020ae52122d45550e38eb4ca9aa223814a2e14d1-a2baefc71f.webp
- Generator 6 is in the open space past the demolished Church
  Related image: images/source-guide/25eff6fe27c8fc4b4f0c27db43a6e501353be198-4ac12922e5.webp

Once done, the Pack-a-Punch machine on top of the Excavation site will be assembled and available.

Related image: images/source-guide/9b33d3c9d86479e1f9a433a8413f7e366a92a463-9b7870faa2.webp

After a certain period of time, a squad of crusader zombie thugs will appear to disable the generators. Prevent them to do so.
