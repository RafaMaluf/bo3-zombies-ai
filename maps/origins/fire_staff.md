# Fire Staff and Kagutsuchi's Blood

## Preparation - Fire Staff

The three parts composing the fire staff are awarded by doing various things.

- One of the part will be available on the reward chest once you activated Generator 6
  Related image: images/source-guide/06b371d78ba22d107e6a5f4437ccd638d0cf7cb1-0932307002.webp
- The second one will ask you to shoot down a particular plane. Look at the skies, you will eventually see an orange glowing plane . Shoot it, it will drop a part down to the ground between excavation site and generator 4
  Related image: images/source-guide/43aa2c54775f7cdef59e5da8fbfdcee95fe33756-8552a2d1ef.webp
  Related image: images/source-guide/83c78a8022d96b9830023f954b43e04b9edca728-0dc05f4843.webp
- The last part will be dropped by the first PanzerSoldat you kill . (Round 8)
  Related image: images/source-guide/d3fe538ff639f52fc187b8b265ebae05446063ce-5b4e97bfaf.webp

You will also need the red disk. It is located in various locations around the church

- On benches on your far left on first floor
  Related image: images/source-guide/9e4102461def32e7cb01b07ea0f5a3b577763195-f3f23a42fb.webp
- On boxes, right on your left when facing generator 6
  Related image: images/source-guide/24cbf78af08a581259e3617756aecd289a87833b-94b1ed4939.webp
- Behind the Tank bellow the church, you'll need to move the armored vehicle first.
  Related image: images/source-guide/665c236bc4d294a89a0069057c3c9c4d777ba8f9-5e4fae4749.webp

Once you've got the three parts, head to the Fire portal, in the bottom of the tunnel, on your left when exiting the spawn. Place the Gramophone on the table and enter the portal.

Related image: images/source-guide/5caa6567598512ca184b7a44a460b60937f523c5-8674e77b93.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/c21cea5dbf899f5317355f9130a04af270670e67-1da5859b59.webp

Once in the Crazy place, reach for Juggernog tonight the fire altar. You will be able to pick up a fire crystal.

Related image: images/source-guide/fb3a366307c781316a9cd74e0e64c0fc00c569e7-60a0dd2d23.webp

Head back under the excavation site and craft the staff on the alter of the same color.

Related image: images/source-guide/2967ea5d0660424c2d84cbc8de58de28c526fdfe-92fa6cd49f.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/92f84b60e5c966b8032b5b493bfbed40f66b55bd-7526c43edc.webp

## Preparation - Kagutsuchi's blood (Upgraded fire staff)

Once the fire staff is in your hands, head to the Crazy place.

In the fire-themed area of the Crazy place you will find metal grids with jars near it. You need to kill zombies on those grids with the staff until the jars are filled.

Related image: images/source-guide/23fff6272138aa12080202fde8fe7ff5c3bab4c6-bfeaaf8e44.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/49eb23228116317468a0c0b826cbc90947181457-c996a5a5f3.webp

Go to the church now, multiple symbols made of an addition of points are visible on the wall on the first floor, on the left side from the stairs. A group of those symbols will be glowing, following this picture (Thanks to www.Kronorium.com) it will give you numbers.

Related image: images/source-guide/913a1d2ed266676f2472bf9a525d92dfa4d839aa-26a7c0377c.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/8128e0af77fa2080bdeac8e7b66ba34ce61df291-bd62e9dd0e.webp

The numbers you'll get are linked to the torches in the tank's floor. You will need to shoot them with your staff following the previous code.

Related image: images/source-guide/742b72a498a8339fd5073072ef5e89368469ff45-5e1347ddff.webp

Your 2 steps are done, to complete the upgrading sequence, move to the "Preparation - Final upgrade stage for all staffs" part in the guide.
