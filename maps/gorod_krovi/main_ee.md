# main ee

## overview

The Gorod Krovi main easter egg is called **Love and War**. It is one of the more involved Black Ops 3 easter eggs, with several independent setup steps, escort-style objectives, a bomb step, the valve / cylinder puzzle, and a 2-phase boss fight.

Before starting, you should already have:

### required
- **Power on**
- **Siegfried Gauntlet**
- **Dragon Strike Controller**

### very useful
- **Upgraded Dragon Strike**
- **Guard of Fafnir / Tiamat's Maw**
- **Pack-a-Punched weapons**
- **Ray Gun Mark 3**
- Gobblegums like:
  - Undead Man Walking
  - Aftertaste
  - Phoenix Up
  - Danger Closest
  - Perkaholic

Important note:
- after the first step, the **generator** in the Hatchery stays active for **5 minutes**
- you can reactivate it **once per round**
- the first step and the second / third / fourth steps are partially independent, so some teams like to split labor early

## step 2 - activate the Hatchery generator

Go to the Pack-a-Punch area near the egg incubator. You must activate the generator there.

To do that, either:
- destroy a **Valkyrie Drone** close to it
- or bait a Valkyrie into shooting it

Then interact with the generator. A visible airflow should appear, showing it is active.

### steps
1. Go to the generator near the egg incubator in the Hatchery.
2. Bring a Valkyrie near it or kill one next to it.
3. Interact with the generator.
4. Confirm the airflow starts.

Important note:
- once active, it lasts **5 minutes**
- it can be reactivated **once per round**

Related image: images/main_ee/2generatorworking.jpg

## step 3 - solve the valve puzzle and collect the cylinder

This step is one of the most confusing parts of the easter egg if done manually.

There are **6 valves** on the map. Each valve can be set to **1, 2, or 3**.

The valve locations are:

- **Dragon Command** - upstairs, to the right
- **Armory** - beside the 500-point bridge and Armory sign
- **Supply Depot** - next to the Mystery Box spot under the stairs
- **Infirmary** - beside beds while descending
- **Tank Factory**
- **Department Store** - top floor

### what you are looking for
- one valve contains the **code cylinder**
- one valve has the **green light**, which is the start of the circuit

The goal is to set the valves so the air path connects from the **green-light valve** to the **cylinder valve**.

### steps
1. Check all 6 valve locations.
2. Identify:
   - where the **green light** is
   - where the **cylinder** is stuck
3. Set the other valves to the correct numbers for that combination.
4. Return to the final valve.
5. Pick up the cylinder.

## valve step - quick solution lookup

### very important valve rule

For every valve solution:
- set only the valves listed in the matching solution
- the codex / cylinder valve is the end point
- do not assign a number to the codex valve
- if an answer sets the codex valve to 1, 2, or 3, that answer is wrong

Tank Factory and Tank Station mean the same location.

Important note:
- In some tools and guides, **Tank Factory** is called **Tank Station**
- In this guide, they mean the same location

The valve step always works like this:
- find where the **green light** is
- find where the **codex / cylinder** is
- set only the valves listed in the matching solution below
- the **codex valve is the end point**
- **do not set the codex valve itself**

If the **green light** and the **codex** are in the same location, the setup is invalid:
- **you cannot begin and end at the same location**

## step 4 - enter KRONOS on S.O.P.H.I.A

Take the cylinder to S.O.P.H.I.A.

On the left side of S.O.P.H.I.A there are 6 small rotating letter cylinders. Shoot them until they spell:

**K R O N O S**

Then interact with S.O.P.H.I.A.

### steps
1. Insert the cylinder into S.O.P.H.I.A.
2. Shoot the rotating letter cylinders.
3. Spell **KRONOS**.
4. Interact with S.O.P.H.I.A.

Related image: images/main_ee/4kronosonsophia.jpg

## step 5 - place the trophies and start the challenges

Place the 6 trophies on the desk below the green screen in front of S.O.P.H.I.A.

This starts a sequence of random challenges. Use the same console to begin.

Important note:
- the challenges are timed
- zombies respawn endlessly during them
- good communication helps a lot in co-op

Related image: images/main_ee/5placetrophiesandstartchallenges.jpg

## challenge 1 - bomb step

You must defuse bombs in the correct order.

### important rule
- the screen first flashes a fast random-looking order
- then it shows the **real order more slowly**
- **only the second, slower order is correct**

### time limit
- **co-op:** 2 minutes
- **solo:** 3 minutes

### bomb locations
- Department Store - under the stairs
- between Infirmary and Operations Bunker - on a pillar
- Dragon Command - upper floor
- Supply Depot - next to the city map
- Tank Factory - near Wunderfizz and Gobblegum
- Armory - on the wall near Wunderfizz downstairs

### steps
1. Watch the screen above the trophies.
2. Ignore the first fast flashing sequence.
3. Memorize the second, slower order.
4. Run the route and defuse the bombs in that exact order.

## challenge 2 - gersh orb

A yellow orb, Gersh's soul, starts moving around the map.

Shoot it with a **Pack-a-Punched weapon** until it stops and speaks. Then it moves again. Repeat this until it reaches Dragon Command. After that, walk up to it.

### steps
1. Find the yellow orb.
2. Shoot it with a Pack-a-Punched weapon until it stops.
3. Wait for it to move again.
4. Repeat until it reaches Dragon Command.
5. Walk up to it there.

## challenge 3 - friendly mangler escort

A friendly Mangler with green eyes spawns in one area of the map.

You must escort / lure him to Dragon Command, specifically into the corner to the right of the trophies.

### practical note
You can speed him up by shooting off his arm, but be careful not to kill him.

### steps
1. Check the command screen for the highlighted area.
2. Find the friendly green-eyed Mangler there.
3. Lead him to Dragon Command.
4. Bring him to the correct corner near the trophies.

### steps

3. Escort it to Dragon Command.

## challenge 5 - groph pod part

A Groph Pod lands in one of the 3 dragon-travel areas. It is outside the playable area, but marked by a green sky beam.

Defend it until it opens, then use the **Siegfried Gauntlet dragon** to pick the part up and bring it back to S.O.P.H.I.A.

### steps
1. Find the Groph Pod by following the green beam.
2. Defend it until it opens.
3. Use the Gauntlet dragon to retrieve the part.
4. Return to S.O.P.H.I.A and give it to her.

## step 6 - download step in the Hatchery

This is always the final challenge in the S.O.P.H.I.A sequence.

Go back to the trophies control screen and pick up the **card**. Then go to the Pack-a-Punch area and place the card in the **red computer**.

This starts a lockdown with Manglers. Survive until the download completes, then take the card back.

### steps
1. Pick up the card at the trophies console.
2. Go to the Hatchery.
3. Insert the card into the red computer.
4. Survive the lockdown.
5. Pick the card back up after the download completes.

## step 7 - get the power core

Return to S.O.P.H.I.A and give her the card. After a short exchange, she gives you the **power core**.

Then go back to Belinski Square and send your **Siegfried Gauntlet dragon** to Nikolai 1.0 in the mech.

### steps
1. Return to S.O.P.H.I.A.
2. Give her the card.
3. Receive the power core.
4. Go back to spawn.
5. Send the Gauntlet dragon to Nikolai 1.0.

## step 8 - enter the boss fight

This is the final point of no return.

All players must stand on the grid where S.O.P.H.I.A was sitting. It opens, dropping the team into the boss arena.

Press the button in the center to start.

### steps
1. Make sure everyone is ready:
   - ammo
   - shield
   - perks
   - Gobblegums
   - weapons
2. Stand on S.O.P.H.I.A's platform.
3. Drop into the arena.
4. Press the center button.

## boss phase 1 - dragon

In the first phase, you fight the dragon while allied with Nikolai 1.0.

Zombies spawn endlessly while the dragon attacks with fire. Survive until Nikolai shoots the dragon and reveals a glowing weak spot.

The weak spots can appear on:
- wing
- belly
- neck

Shoot the glowing orange wound as hard as possible.

### practical note
The **Ray Gun Mark 3** is extremely strong here.

### fire survival note
When the dragon covers most of the arena in fire, the outer edges stay safe.

### steps
1. Survive zombie pressure and dragon attacks.
2. Wait for Nikolai to expose a weak point.
3. Shoot the glowing weak point.
4. Repeat until the dragon dies.

Related images:
- images/main_ee/13dragonredwheretoshoot.jpg
- images/main_ee/13dragondead.jpg

## boss phase 2 - nikolai 1.0 mech

After the dragon dies, you fight Nikolai 1.0 in his mech.

He attacks with:
- machine guns
- electric harpoon
- RAPS

To damage him, shoot the glowing yellow lights on the mech.

The important weak points include:
- shoulder lights
- belly / lower lights
- the final weak point under the pilot seat

### important warning
His chaingun can kill extremely fast.

### steps
1. Avoid Nikolai's attacks.
2. Shoot the glowing yellow lights.
3. Break each weak point in sequence.
4. Finish the final core / under-seat weak point.

## full progression summary

1. Collect the 6 trophies.
2. Activate the Hatchery generator.
3. Solve the valve step and collect the cylinder.
4. Enter **KRONOS** on S.O.P.H.I.A.
5. Place the trophies and begin the challenge sequence.
6. Complete:
   - bomb step
   - Gersh orb step
   - friendly Mangler escort
   - Valkyrie escort
   - Groph Pod retrieval
7. Do the Hatchery download step.
8. Return the card to S.O.P.H.I.A.
9. Take the power core.
10. Send the Gauntlet dragon to Nikolai 1.0 in spawn.
11. Enter the boss arena.
12. Kill the dragon.
13. Kill Nikolai 1.0.
