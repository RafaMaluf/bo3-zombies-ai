# Samantha dolls

## Secret easter egg - Samantha's dolls

You need to have activated the secret song before doing this.

Head to the bathrooms, you must use the flushes in a specific order. From right toilet, to left, "enter" 9 3 5.

Related image: images/source-guide/5aa25adeabd24a6de59ef589f1738a2ac8f105fa-cb8c7a34fb.webp

Once done, you must interact with a figurine of Samantha, which appeared besides a Wunderfizz machine, near the toilets.

Related image: images/source-guide/35a3546d809d1b46f4af595d701bca515ec3860c-e392e9702d.webp

Like the other maps, this will spawn a bunch of other dolls ( spared in 10 different locationsl) that you must shoot down. You will have a limited time to do it, if you are too slow, you will hear samantha laughing and you will have to reactivate the doll.

Also, the dolls will play a lullaby song, so you can spot them easily.
