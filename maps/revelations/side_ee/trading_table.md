# trading table

## overview

Revelations has a hidden **trading table** in Nacht Der Untoten.

This lets one player place a weapon on the table so another player can take it.

Completing this easter egg also unlocks the **M1927 wallbuy** in Der Eisendrache.

## step 1 - get the pink chalk

The pink chalk is in **Der Eisendrache** and requires anti-gravity.

### steps
1. Go to Der Eisendrache.
2. Activate anti-gravity.
3. Grab the pink chalk from the small platform location.

Related image:
- images/side_ees/tradingtablechalkloc.jpg

## step 2 - move the wall phrases in order

You now need to move phrases from one wall to another in sequence.

### move 1
Pick up:
- **"Wish Too Often And Your Wishing Well Will Run"** in Der Eisendrache

Place it:
- in Verruckt, in front of the power generator, where **"Ascend from Darkness"** is written

### move 2
Take:
- **"Ascend from Darkness"**

Place it:
- on the spiral staircase in Nacht Der Untoten

### move 3
Take:
- **"Salvation lies above"**

Place it:
- on the empty wall next to the back staircase in Nacht Der Untoten

### move 4
At the Mob of the Dead infirmary entrance, pick up:
- **"Knowledge Itself Is For The Taking"**

Place it:
- in Kino Der Toten, on the wall behind the teleporter where the lever used to be

You will receive:
- **"A Soul Alone Can Follow The Path"**

Take that and place it:
- back in Mob of the Dead where you picked up the previous line

When all of this is done, a sound plays and the table in Nacht glows.

## result

- the **trading table** in Nacht becomes active
- any player can place a weapon there for another player to take
- the **M1927 wallbuy** is unlocked in Der Eisendrache where the first phrase was picked up
