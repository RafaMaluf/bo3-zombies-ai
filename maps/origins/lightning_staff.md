# Lightning Staff and Kimat's Bite

## Preparation - Lightning staff

The lightning staff will require 3 parts, a purple disk and a purple gem.

The three parts are located on platforms and high-grounds you can access only by getting on the tank.

- The first high-ground is located in the first half of the tank's path, between the church and the tank station at Generator 2 . You need to jump on the wooden platform on your right then going forward, the part is in the giant's footstep
  Related image: images/source-guide/f67aa5dfa584d64e0712284601f7fb20e271313a-3dd57b7839.webp
  Related image: images/source-guide/7916898765bff4338ff1e148cf8ccec024ed604f-65d7a9cd52.webp
- The second place you need to reach is on the second part of the tank's travel, on your left when at the level of the excavation site, you need to jump on a wooden platform . The part is in the following cave
  Related image: images/source-guide/e88adc3f491856dbd63222fe270f37d6baf5a6ad-464e0327fe.webp
  Related image: images/source-guide/74906ef77669ed0083e3856b6ae6d164593c2b6a-2feab7266a.webp
- The last spot is just before entering the church again, you need to jump on the ground to your right
  Related image: images/source-guide/ea8ca02a9dac026e17915d46c76ab22ee68e9d18-acf026905e.webp
  Related image: images/source-guide/77fc50b232fd70a68835ced3168568d017580bc4-aa5bdac522.webp

With a bit of practice and Stamin-up, you could get the second part and climb back on the tank just in time to get the third part.

About the purple disk, it is located around generator 4.

- On a wooden chariot cart between generator 4 and excavation site

  Related image: images/source-guide/b971a05e40593ba5bbd3220bc3f0a522222eb00e-1a578f7097.webp
- On a metal machine on the right of the Wind portal, in the tunnel descending near generator 4
  Related image: images/source-guide/51a9a004810ed39c570c1bfa944c9368665417bb-d6c4a335fe.webp

For the purple gem, you must use the lightning portal which is located next to generator 5. Place the gramophone, enter the crazy place and pick up the crystal on the purple altar.

## Preparation - Kimat's bite (Upgraded lightning staff)

Now that you've got the lightning staff, head back to the Crazy place. Around the lightning area, you should see multiple purple triangles arranged like a piano keyboard.

Related image: images/source-guide/baf9e24ea0ce1feaa84f11ae3f9100c4793277ac-fa4dcad5ea.webp

Following this picture (Thanks to www.kronorium.com) , you will need to shoot at the triangles with the staff. Enter the first sequence (136), then wait a little bit and enter the second (357) and finally enter the last one (246).

Related image: images/source-guide/0812a1b3f78f00c3e7862de87a521f105ae212ac-69b0b07cfe.webp

Once done, head back to the battlefield. Several metallic pannels are scattered around the map. They have a small cursor you can interact with to change its position.

They must be pointed using the following order:

- In the tank station , near generator 2 needs to be set Down
  Related image: images/source-guide/de3f2930f13a0792c1ca8a0ca53a4f4e7069780e-f941e91b2f.webp
- In the spawn , down the stairs needs to be set Left
  Related image: images/source-guide/8d8010996081f01ec1b50c2dfcfb3fd4e369b04a-060c012e23.webp
- Near the wind tunnel's entrance at generator 4 needs to be set Up
  Related image: images/source-guide/6fe568d05c415c36e67cec16f52ef7f6f876ab0c-a69a2db15f.webp
- In the church, first floor needs to be set Up
  Related image: images/source-guide/a6ad3bb1c7039d6a589ab1b5123fc3ebd714263f-945b524a90.webp
- Under the church, behind the tank needs to be set Right
  Related image: images/source-guide/4934a7714fa14c2b5cec25e136c72a1326e96af3-887beccbed.webp
- Around generator 5's broken house, needs to be set Down
  Related image: images/source-guide/a56036c99c0c28642c04addcfc7b44aee2b6eeff-2dd1bc0707.webp
- On a wall of the Mound facing the church, needs to be set Up
  Related image: images/source-guide/83ceae84dbe8d590d4a235e651a61c4153c28efc-3eccd32a32.webp

A sound should play to hint you that every pannel stopped sparkling. You can proceed to the "Preparation - Final upgrade stage for all staffs" part.
