# harvest pods and fumigators

## fumigators

You need a Fumigator before you can open Harvest Pods.

Listed spawn examples:
- On a crate to the right of the Beast pedestal in the starting area.
- Between two windows to the left of "Nero's Landing." When the players spawn, it is behind them.
- On the tailgate of a truck to the right of spawn, next to Quick Revive.
- On a chair in the room between the Junction Beast pedestal and Stamin-Up.
- On a crate to the right, below a window, just through the entrance to Canals from Junction.

Related image: images/pods/fumigator.webp

## pod stages

In Shadows of Evil, unharvested pods swell as rounds pass. They change color as they advance:
- lowest stage: green-yellow
- intermediate stage: red
- highest stage: purple

The rewards get better with each stage, and purple is the only pod stage that can provide the Margwa Tentacle for the Apothicon Servant.
