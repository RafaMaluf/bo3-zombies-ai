# side ee

## overview

The Giant has a few small visual / story easter eggs that do not affect progression but are still worth knowing. These are mostly environmental references and hidden details rather than gameplay-changing rewards.

This file only covers the visual side easter eggs and excludes radios and ciphers.

## Icarus plane

The Icarus plane from Mob of the Dead can be seen on top of the roof near Teleporter C. This is a visual callback and story reference rather than a gameplay mechanic.

## giant robot head

Outside the map, you can see the giant robot head being built. This is another visual easter egg and a clear reference to Origins and the broader Zombies storyline.

### Related image
- images/side_ees/robot.jpg

## Maxis office

One of the windows outside the map lets you see into Maxis's office. This is a small environmental easter egg and another storyline reference hidden in the background of the map.

### Related image
- images/side_ees/maxis.jpg

## teddy bear shadow

A hidden teddy bear shadow can be seen as a small visual detail on the map. This is just a fun environmental easter egg with no gameplay reward.
