# time trials

## overview

Gorod Krovi has hidden time trials tied to round completion speed. If you reach certain rounds quickly enough, you unlock secret melee weapons.

These challenges are independent:
- failing an earlier one does not automatically block the later ones

The melee weapons are picked up in the **Operations Bunker** near the sign shown in the map.

Important note:
- the melee weapon replaces a weapon slot
- it does not replace your normal knife

## weapon 1 - wrench

### requirement
Reach round 6, meaning complete round 5, in **under 5 minutes**

### effect
- one-shots until round 10

## practical tips

The guide suggests:
- avoid opening too many doors early, especially for the first 2 time checks
- abuse helpful Gobblegums
- use wall weapons and fast setups
- Perkaholic and strong early-round Gobblegums help a lot

Completing the final benchmark also gives the **Time Attack** achievement.
