# Samantha's Sorrow

## Side easter egg - Samantha sorrow

To activate this Zombies Chronicles easter egg you must first of all have the 4 standard staffs crafted.

Head now under the excavation site. Use each staffs to shoot on each side of the lowest stone circle. You willl shoot them following this:

Related image: images/source-guide/c4d1f6ec972b37f10b7a541eb27011be1462b6e0-41bcad5c54.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/fd31ee34b33ae7e5adf921d60132faf62485c20b-d0358844bd.webp

Once done, head to the Crazy place, a samantha doll will be near the main blue basis. Interact with it, then shoot the other dolls.
