# general

Revelations is the final Black Ops 3 Aether map and works almost like a remix of multiple previous Zombies maps stitched together inside Agartha. Instead of building its identity around one single original area, the map is made of connected fragments from older maps, with portals, jump pads, corruption engines, and shared quest mechanics linking everything together.

A practical way to think about Revelations is that it has 3 major layers:

- **core setup**
- **special equipment and upgrades**
- **main easter egg progression**

The map combines elements from:
- **Nacht Der Untoten**
- **Kino Der Toten**
- **Origins**
- **Mob of the Dead**
- **Verruckt**
- **Der Eisendrache**
- **Shangri-La**

## core setup flow

A normal early progression on Revelations usually looks like this:

1. activate the 4 ritual altars
2. unlock **Pack-a-Punch**
3. build the **Dragon Shield**
4. build the **Keeper Protector**
5. start working on the **Apothicon Servant upgrade**
6. begin the main easter egg if desired
7. work on side easter eggs and masks depending on the run

## Pack-a-Punch

Pack-a-Punch is trapped inside the Apothicon at the start of the match.

To unlock it, you must:
- activate the 4 ritual altars
- open the 4 main portal routes
- go to **Nacht Der Untoten**
- use the console upstairs when it flashes
- enter the Apothicon
- shoot the 3 glowing objects holding Pack-a-Punch in place

So unlike some other maps, Pack-a-Punch is not just a one-room unlock. It is tied into map progression and portal access.

## Apothicon Servant

The **Apothicon Servant** returns in Revelations, and this time it can finally be upgraded.

The base weapon still comes from the **Mystery Box**, but the upgrade requires a separate quest involving 115 stones around the map and then Pack-a-Punching it inside the Apothicon.

Because of that, the Apothicon Servant is one of the most important weapons on the map for both survival and easter egg progression.

## major equipment

### Dragon Shield
The map has a shield buildable, assembled near Nacht Der Untoten. Like other shields in BO3, it is one of the main survival tools and is useful during setup and the easter egg.

### Keeper Protector
The **Keeper Protector** is another important support tool. It follows you, attacks enemies, and is directly required for part of the main easter egg.

### masks
Revelations includes multiple secret masks, each with its own bonus. Some are easy, while others require very specific challenge conditions.

Because there are many masks and each one is a smaller self-contained unlock, they work best as a grouped side system rather than separate major files.

## map identity and enemies

Revelations is chaotic because it mixes many enemy types and mechanics from across the Aether story:
- zombies
- furies
- margwas
- panzersoldats
- spiders
- corrupted keepers
- parasites
- elemental hazards
- anti-gravity and portal traversal

Because of this, the map often rewards:
- strong wonder weapon control
- movement knowledge
- familiarity with portal shortcuts
- efficient setup

## main easter egg overview

The Revelations easter egg is long, but much of it is structured around object hunts and repeated map traversal rather than extremely technical puzzles.

Major elements include:
- grave step
- Keeper Protector ritual step
- Lil' Arnies step inside the Apothicon
- bone collection with the upgraded Apothicon Servant
- S.O.P.H.I.A beam step
- Samantha's room / Kronorium step
- egg and rune collection
- boss fight
- final Shadowman phase

This means the map can feel confusing at first, but once each section is isolated, it becomes much easier to follow.

## practical note

Revelations looks overwhelming because it mixes so many old-map spaces together, but the knowledge base works well if the map is split like this:

- **Pack-a-Punch**
- **Apothicon Servant upgrade**
- **Dragon Shield**
- **Keeper Protector**
- **main easter egg**
- **masks**
- **side easter eggs**

That is the cleanest way to make the map understandable for quick gameplay questions.
