# Power, lunar landers and Pack-a-Punch

## Preparation - Activating Power & Accessing Pack-a-Punch

The main power switch is located on top of the central building. Three gates will block you, no matter which side you choose from spawn.

Related image: images/source-guide/8578fb33bf2a3e42aed4f33db443c08536b61258-5c53bc7615.webp

In order to open the Pack-a-Punch, you will have to fly the moon lander to 4 locations.

Besides the power lever, there is a red button with a rocket's diagram. The diagram is separated in 4 parts colored in red, each time you land the Moon Lander on a newly visited platform, one part will turn green.

Related image: images/source-guide/d4befd0dadac0878e6eb2d6cb9b5420173d0713c-22ad6fa0ab.webp

The landing spots are located:

- In the spawning room with the centrifugation machine
  Related image: images/source-guide/ab4534f6528830ddf3c1c23064a8b9ba0debbdbd-9ff199d32b.webp
- Up the stairs leading to Widow's Wine:
  Related image: images/source-guide/a39e67cffb2021f74d769ca9f785da37fd165ead-52d83f5d87.webp
- In the courtyard facing Stamin-up:
  Related image: images/source-guide/336be28f2313143a0be6f466bcaa745ef4e2dbdb-bb2b3e7b53.webp
- In the area before the corridor with Speed Cola:
  Related image: images/source-guide/eeb9417b15a06a33c196703d7ab986d1818b3050-147c4014b0.webp

When the whole diagram is green , you can push the button, launching the Rocket, clearing the Rocket pad with the PaP machine at the bottom.

Related image: images/source-guide/7eaa3d291aa1431e6f40b08e1498f783b0b57d09-ef5e47f2af.webp

Note that you can destroy the Rocket while it’s in the air by shooting at it to obtain a power up.
