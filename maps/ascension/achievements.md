# Ascension achievements

## Achievements

The Eagle has landers

Difficulty: Easy

Simply use all three Moon Landers. This is a part of the main Easter Egg steps. Also you can check their location in the part "Activating Power - Accessing Pack-a-Punch"

Chimp on the Barbie

Difficulty: Easy

Simply wait a bonus wave to occur and bait some monkeys to come after you. Then activate a fire trap to kill them. They are commonly located around gates between PaP and Widow's wine.
