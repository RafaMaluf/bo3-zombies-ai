# music 1 - dead again

## overview

The song **Dead Again** by Kevin Sherwood / Elena Siegman can be activated by interacting with 3 teddy bears around the map.

## teddy bear locations

- Samantha's room, sitting on a chair
- rocket launch site, inside the truck near the Wundersphere
- above spawn, next to Juggernog, in the connector leading to the underground pyramid room

## steps

1. Go to Samantha's room and interact with the teddy bear on the chair.
2. Go to the rocket launch site and interact with the teddy bear inside the truck.
3. Go above spawn, near Juggernog, and interact with the last teddy bear.
4. After all 3 are activated, **Dead Again** begins to play.
