# hallucinations

## overview

If you breathe spore gas for too long on Zetsubou No Shima, you can trigger hallucinations at certain map locations.

This is a visual side easter egg rather than a gameplay reward.

## known hallucination spots

The guide lists the following hallucination points:
- the green tanks in the bunker
- near the Lab A crafting table
- near the Lab B crafting table
- on the catwalk going up toward Lab B

## steps

1. Stay in spore gas long enough to trigger the hallucination effect.
2. Move to one of the known hallucination locations.
3. Watch for the visual hallucination.
