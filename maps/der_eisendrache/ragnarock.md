# ragnarock dg-4

## overview

The **Ragnarok DG-4** is Der Eisendrache’s specialist weapon and is also required for the main easter egg. To build it, you must collect **3 parts**:

- one dropped by a Panzer Soldat
- one obtained from the **Death Ray + Wundersphere** step
- one obtained from the **rocket test site teleporter box** step

After collecting all 3 parts, take them to a crafting table and build the Ragnarok DG-4.

## part 1 - Panzer Soldat drop

The first Ragnarok part comes from the Panzer Soldat.

### steps
1. Wait for the Panzer Soldat to spawn.
2. Kill the Panzer.
3. Pick up the Ragnarok piece it drops.

Related image: images/ragnarock/panzer_drop_piece.jpg

## part 2 - Death Ray + Wundersphere piece

The second part is tied to the **Death Ray**. When you activate the Death Ray, the Ragnarok piece appears floating in the air above one side of the area. You then need to use the correct Wundersphere to grab it while airborne.

### steps
1. Go to the Death Ray area.
2. Turn on / activate the Death Ray.
3. Look up and find the floating Ragnarok part.
4. If the part is on the spawn-side route, use the Wundersphere above Double Tap.
5. If the part is on the Death Ray side, use the Wundersphere near that side.
6. Grab the part while traveling through the air.

Important note:
- You do not collect this piece from the ground.
- You must use the correct Wundersphere depending on where the part is floating.

## part 3 - rocket test site teleporter box

The third part is obtained at the **rocket test site**, but it is not collected immediately after pulling the first lever. That first lever only starts the process.

### steps
1. Go to the rocket test site.
2. Wait for the voice announcement that the rocket test phase is becoming active.
3. As soon as the test begins, pull the lever near the **left staircase**.
4. Go into the **safe room** and wait until the rocket test ends.
5. Go near the **teleporter** and look at the nearby box with 3 indicator lights.
6. Wait until all 3 lights turn green.
7. Pull the lever on that box.
8. The Ragnarok piece will appear inside the teleporter.
9. Collect the part.

Important notes:
- Pulling the first lever only starts the rocket-site Ragnarok process.
- The actual piece is collected only after the test ends and the teleporter box finishes cycling to all green lights.

## crafting the Ragnarok DG-4

After collecting all 3 parts, go to a crafting table and build the weapon.

### crafting table locations
- Undercroft, near the pyramid
- Above Double Tap

### steps
1. Collect the Panzer piece.
2. Collect the Death Ray + Wundersphere piece.
3. Collect the rocket test site teleporter-box piece.
4. Go to a crafting table.
5. Build the **Ragnarok DG-4**.

## practical note

The Ragnarok DG-4 is not only a strong specialist weapon for survival. It is also required for the main easter egg, so in a full setup run you usually want to build it as soon as your core map setup is stable.
