# Re-Damned secret song

## Side easter egg - Secret song 3

Final secret song is Nightmare from Avenged Sevenfold. It is triggered when doing the One Giant Leap achievement. (See part bellow)

Quick summary, you need to have another player to do this. Both excavators must breach Tunnel 6 and Tunnel 11. One player must stay in the spawn before the tunnels being blocked thus, becoming trapped in the spawn. He must then go down and bleed out.

Music will trigger on respawn.
