# dragon wings

## overview

The Dragon Wings are a special wearable item on Gorod Krovi.

They give:
- 30% fire damage reduction
- 30% explosive damage reduction
- free instant travel to the Hatchery

To use them, interact with the small wings near a dragon platform and you will teleport to the Hatchery without paying for a dragon ride.

## requirements

Before unlocking the Dragon Wings, you need:
- the **Siegfried Gauntlet**
- to use all 3 dragon travel stations

## required dragon stations

You must travel from:
- Dragon Command
- Supply Depot
- Tank Factory

## steps

1. Obtain the Siegfried Gauntlet.
2. Use the dragon from Dragon Command.
3. Use the dragon from Supply Depot.
4. Use the dragon from Tank Factory.
5. Go to the top floor of Department Store.
6. Pick up the Dragon Wings from the mannequin.

## result

After unlocking them:
- you can travel to the Hatchery for free
- you interact with the small dragon wings near a dragon pad instead of paying for a ride
