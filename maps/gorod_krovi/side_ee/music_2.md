# music 2

## overview

The second secret song on Gorod Krovi is **Ace of Spades** by Motörhead.

To activate it, interact with 3 Ace of Spades cards.

## card locations

- on a blackboard beside Double Tap in Tank Factory
- on a desk on the first floor of Dragon Command, next to a Mystery Box spot and bomb location
- in the Hatchery, on a desk to the right of Pack-a-Punch

## steps

1. Interact with the Ace of Spades card in Tank Factory.
2. Interact with the Ace of Spades card in Dragon Command.
3. Interact with the Ace of Spades card in the Hatchery.
4. After all 3 are activated, **Ace of Spades** begins to play.
