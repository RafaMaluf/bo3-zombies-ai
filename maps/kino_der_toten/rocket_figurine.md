# Rocket shield figurine

## Side easter egg - Rocket figurine

In the "Five" side room, stands a rocket minifigurine that can be activated. This is completely useless, but still is a funny side feature.

Once the power is on, head to the dressing room. There are 2 mannequin, you must jump 5 times in front of the left mannequin.

Related image: images/source-guide/cf2a85fa3ccf4bb4ab661389e54e91d32d09b0ae-661c2135f7.webp

Now, use the teleporter. After leaving the Pack-a-Punch room, you will be in the pentagon's room. Approach the rocket figurine, and press action. It will launch through the table.
