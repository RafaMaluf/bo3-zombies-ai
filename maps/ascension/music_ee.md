# Abracadavre secret song

## Side easter egg - Teddy bears

Three teddy bears are hiding within the complex. Upon activation, they will trigger a secret song : Abracadavre https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bevws2UDtpw

They are located:

- In the spawning room, on the first floor, on the right coming from the stairs, on a barel
  Related image: images/source-guide/b70a879a10a12250de00f45e8abf96af66dfede8-6be49f04fd.webp
- Right above a gobblegum machine, on the right of the corridor, after Speed Cola.
  Related image: images/source-guide/ab48ff561e49c30f05d1f2fe41afec365a4ade1e-f5c15f1bc1.webp
- Behind a grid, on the left of the Moon Landing pad, after Stamin-up
  Related image: images/source-guide/fc36cb7205fa8adc88ac6d39827a1dd30bb8b41f-8552a6144f.webp
