# music 1

## overview

The first secret song on Gorod Krovi is **Dead Ended**.

To activate it, interact with 3 vodka bottles around the map.

## bottle locations

- Department Store, just after the first door opened from spawn
- in the courtyard after Juggernog, on the water in front of the Operations Bunker entrance
- Supply Depot, on a shelf in front of the entrance

## steps

1. Interact with the first vodka bottle in Department Store.
2. Interact with the second vodka bottle near the courtyard / Operations Bunker area.
3. Interact with the third vodka bottle in Supply Depot.
4. After all 3 are activated, **Dead Ended** begins to play.
