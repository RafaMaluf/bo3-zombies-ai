# mangler helmet

## overview

The Mangler Helmet is a wearable item on Gorod Krovi.

It gives:
- 30% more damage against Manglers
- 50% less damage taken from Manglers

## unlock requirement

To unlock it, you must destroy:
- 5 Mangler helmets
- 5 Mangler arm cannons

A creepy sound plays when each condition is completed.

## steps

1. Fight Manglers as normal.
2. Shoot off 5 Mangler helmets.
3. Shoot off 5 Mangler arm cannons.
4. After both counts are completed, go to Department Store.
5. Enter from the spawn stairs.
6. Look immediately to the left.
7. Pick up the helmet from the mannequin.
