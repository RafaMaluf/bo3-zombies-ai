# music 2

## overview

The second secret music easter egg on Zetsubou No Shima is activated by filling wall pockets with the correct amounts of water.

This plays **Samantha's Lullaby**.

## pocket location

The water pockets are located underground, near the **purple water** source, on the neighboring wall.

## required water amounts

From left to right, the pockets must receive:

- 1
- 3
- 5
- 6
- 7
- 5

thirds of a bucket.

Important notes:
- you can knife a pocket if you put too much water into it
- after filling them correctly, interact with the **7th pocket** to start the music

## steps

1. Go underground near the purple water source.
2. Find the wall with the water pockets.
3. Fill the pockets from left to right with the correct water amounts.
4. If you make a mistake, knife the pocket to reduce / reset that amount as needed.
5. Once all amounts are correct, interact with the 7th pocket.
6. The song starts playing.
