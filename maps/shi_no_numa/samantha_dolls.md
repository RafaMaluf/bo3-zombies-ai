# Samantha dolls

## Side easter egg - Samantha's dolls

This easter egg will give you a max ammo, aswell as playing a secret song.

Head to the Fishing Hut (See the map above)

You need to shoot on 4 plates scattered in the house, with the M1911 starting pistol only :

- On your right when entering
  Related image: images/source-guide/85bfd6742cbec22b9a3e8538203adca54a114c3b-61db33f8bb.webp
- On the wall behind the perk machine
  Related image: images/source-guide/225fe04e0bf61a2ddb8b4038ed909efb7bd6a3f7-73c1415b0c.webp
- On the right of the door, on the left side of the hut
  Related image: images/source-guide/0f24016745dd86963d9d65811be9ae6020e3cfa6-2ae55988ef.webp
- On your left when entering, on the left of the mystery box spot
  Related image: images/source-guide/45a6fbc79b7bc2872a31672851837a2fd2d71e97-9636e09441.webp

Now head to the small room behind the fishing hut and interact with the samantha's doll.

Related image: images/source-guide/dd9c30ca5a4df76c930e7824c526a69b2f942f4d-00d709cc0c.webp

Go outside the hut on the bridge. A doll will be on the wooden fence, you'll need to shoot it. A bunch of others samantha dolls will appear on the bridge, destroy them all.

Related image: images/source-guide/c4b42606454b1ebc5eb74b38903768949eebb701-d9cfd9e537.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/3ad147a4383255e6d2a0c8bacf09447aab2de21f-59c0a3bcc6.webp

Head back behind the fishing hut, and press action. Enjoy your reward !
