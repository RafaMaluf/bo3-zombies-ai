# void bow

## overview

The Void Bow upgrade turns the Wrath of the Ancients into **Kreegakaleet lu Gosata'ahm**. It is usually considered one of the longest bow upgrades because it has several distinct steps:
- start the quest by charge-shooting the void symbol
- melee kill a zombie on the purple square to release the jar
- find and interact with the 6 skulls
- feed 6 crawlers to the circle in the trophy room
- shoot the flying jar and memorize the 3 spoken words
- collect 6 purple symbols dropped by zombies
- use the statues in the corridor to convert the word code into symbol code
- shoot the glowing symbols in the correct order
- collect the rebuilt arrow
- place it on the altar and charge it

## step 1 - start the quest

To begin the Void Bow quest, charge-shoot an arrow at the void symbol on the roof of the tunnel near Double Tap, just above the 1000-point gate.

### steps
1. Obtain the base bow, **Wrath of the Ancients**.
2. Go to the tunnel near Double Tap.
3. Look up at the void symbol above the 1000-point gate.
4. Fire a charged shot at it.

Related image: images/void_bow/1wheretoshootstart.jpg

## step 2 - purple square and jar

After starting the quest, a purple square appears in the trophy room beneath the clock tower. You must melee kill a zombie on that glowing square. Then interact with the broken tile to release the jar.

### steps
1. Go to the trophy room beneath the clock tower.
2. Find the glowing purple square.
3. Melee kill a zombie while it is on the square.
4. Interact with the broken tile.
5. Release the jar.

## step 3 - interact with the 6 skulls

You must now find 6 skulls around the map and interact with all of them.

### skull locations
- on a broken wall near Mule Kick
- on a window outside the church, to the right of the entrance
- in a toy chest in Samantha's room
- on a wall near Double Tap
- in a sink in the teleporter room
- on the truck to the left of the landing pad

### steps
1. Go to each skull location.
2. Interact with each skull.
3. Repeat until all 6 skulls have been collected.

## step 4 - feed 6 crawlers to the circle

After picking up the 6 skulls, go back to the purple circle in the trophy room. You must bring 6 crawlers into the circle. The skulls will eat them.

### steps
1. Make crawlers.
2. Bring each crawler into the circle in the trophy room.
3. Let the skulls eat a total of 6 crawlers.

Related image: images/void_bow/10crawlergettingeated.jpg

## step 5 - shoot the flying jar and remember the 3 words

After the crawler step, shoot the flying jar with a charged arrow. A dark voice will say 3 words. You must remember them in the correct order.

Possible words include:
- DOOR
- ROE
- HORN
- HEART
- CROWN
- GRIFFIN

### steps
1. Charge the bow.
2. Shoot the flying jar.
3. Listen carefully to the 3 spoken words.
4. Write them down in order.

Important note:
- the order matters

## step 6 - collect 6 purple symbols from zombies

Now you must kill zombies and collect 6 purple symbols that randomly drop from them.

### steps
1. Kill zombies anywhere on the map.
2. Watch for purple symbol drops.
3. Pick them up quickly.
4. Collect all 6 before they disappear.

Important notes:
- the symbols remain on the ground only a few seconds
- you can track your progress on the purple circle in the trophy room

## step 7 - use the statues in the corridor

Once all 6 symbols have been collected, go to the corridor between the power lever and Samantha's room. There are 6 armors / statues there, each with a symbol in front of it.

You must use the 3 statues that correspond to the 3 words the voice said earlier. The order of interaction matters, because it determines the order of the output symbols.

### steps
1. Go to the corridor between power and Samantha's room.
2. Find the 6 statues / armors.
3. Match the 3 spoken words to their corresponding statues.
4. Interact with the correct 3 statues in the exact order of the spoken words.
5. Memorize the resulting output symbols.

## step 8 - shoot the glowing symbols in the correct order

Return to the purple circle and shoot the glowing symbols in the order generated by the statue step.

### steps
1. Go back to the purple circle.
2. Look at the glowing symbols.
3. Shoot them in the correct order.

Important notes:
- if you enter the wrong code, the skulls attack
- leave the room and try again if you fail
- if done correctly, the voice says: **it is my name**

Related images:
- images/void_bow/17shootglowingsymbols.jpg
- images/void_bow/18arrowrebuilt.jpg

## step 9 - collect the rebuilt arrow

After the code is entered correctly, interact with the tile until you receive the rebuilt arrow.

### steps
1. Stay in the trophy room.
2. Interact with the tile.
3. Pick up the rebuilt arrow.

Related image: images/void_bow/18arrowrebuilt.jpg

## step 10 - place the arrow on the altar

Go back to the altars room underground and place the arrow on the corresponding Void altar.

### steps
1. Go to the pyramid room.
2. Place the arrow on the Void altar.

## step 11 - charge the altar and claim the bow

Kill zombies close to the altar until it is fully charged. Then pick up the upgraded bow.

### steps
1. Kill zombies close to the altar.
2. Wait until the altar is fully charged.
3. Interact and pick up **Kreegakaleet lu Gosata'ahm**.

## full progression summary

1. Charge-shoot the void symbol above the 1000-point gate near Double Tap.
2. Go to the trophy room and melee kill a zombie on the purple square.
3. Interact with the broken tile to release the jar.
4. Find and interact with the 6 skulls.
5. Bring 6 crawlers to the trophy room circle.
6. Shoot the flying jar and memorize the 3 spoken words in order.
7. Kill zombies and collect 6 purple symbol drops.
8. Go to the statue corridor and interact with the correct 3 statues in the right order.
9. Return to the trophy room and shoot the glowing symbols in the correct order.
10. Interact with the tile and collect the rebuilt arrow.
11. Place the arrow on the Void altar in the pyramid room.
12. Kill zombies close to it until charged.
13. Pick up the upgraded Void Bow.
