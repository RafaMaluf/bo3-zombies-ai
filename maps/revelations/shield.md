 # shield

## overview

Revelations has a buildable **Dragon Shield**. Like other BO3 shields, it is one of the most important survival tools on the map.

You collect 3 parts, then build it near **Nacht Der Untoten**, behind Juggernog.

## part 1 - origins area piece

The first shield part is in the **Origins** area, just after leaving spawn.

### possible locations
- immediately on your left when entering Origins from spawn
- on the right, near a **Wunderfizz** machine
- in front of the truck with glowing lights and radio sound

## part 2 - der eisendrache area piece

The second shield part is in **Der Eisendrache**.

Important note:
- this piece is on a wall and requires **anti-gravity** to grab

### possible locations
- on one anti-gravity wall spot
- on another anti-gravity wall spot in the same area
- on the third anti-gravity wall spot in that section

## part 3 - verruckt area piece

The last shield part is in the **Verruckt** area.

### possible locations
- on a pillar near **Speed Cola**
- on a table in the kitchen
- in the corner of the small room after the larger stair room

## build location

Once all 3 parts are collected, build the shield near **Nacht Der Untoten**, behind **Juggernog**.

Related image: images/shield/table.jpg

## steps

1. Go to Origins and collect the first shield part.
2. Go to Der Eisendrache and use anti-gravity to collect the second part.
3. Go to Verruckt and collect the third part.
4. Return to the build table behind Juggernog in Nacht.
5. Build the **Dragon Shield**.

## full progression summary

1. Collect the Origins shield piece.
2. Collect the Der Eisendrache anti-gravity shield piece.
3. Collect the Verruckt shield piece.
4. Return to Nacht Der Untoten.
5. Build the shield behind Juggernog.
