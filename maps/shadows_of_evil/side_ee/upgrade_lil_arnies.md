# upgraded Lil' Arnies

## Requirements
- obtain Lil' Arnies from the Mystery Box
- complete all 4 rituals
- get 100 kills with the base Lil' Arnies
- there is no sound cue for the 100 kills, so you need to track it yourself

## Steps
1. Throw an Arnie at the walking cane leaning against the couch on the first floor of the Ruby Rabbit.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lil_arnies_cane.jpg
2. Throw an Arnie at the top hat on the mannequin head in Nero's lair.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lil_arnies_hat.jpg
3. Throw an Arnie at the bow tie in the locker room of the boxing gym.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lil_arnies_bowtie.jpg
4. Go to the Burlesque.
5. Interact with the ritual podium.
6. Receive upgraded Lil' Arnies and watch their dance.  
   Related image: images/side_ees/lil_arnies_dance.jpg
