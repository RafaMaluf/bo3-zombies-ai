# Origins main easter egg

## Main easter egg quest

Firstly you will need to have those prerequirements:

- All 6 generators activated
- One Inch Punch (Step 6)
- G-Strike (Step 3)

The steps are named the same way the steps are in the mission "Vorkuta" in Call of duty Black Ops 1 campaign.

Step one: Secure the keys Craft and upgrade all the 4 staffs. This will take time, check the parts above to do this.

Step two: Ascend from Darkness You should now notice that a fifth receiver has appeared in front of the excavation site's altars. Three other receivers have spawned as well in the giant robots heads. You must place all 4 staffs in these altars to complete the step.

To see how to enter the giant robots heads, check the wind staff part.

- The fire staff needs to be placed on the excavation site receiver

Once done the staffs should respawn and be able to pick up bellow the excavation site, where you crafted them.

Related image: images/source-guide/fc5e246ffceb5235460e5b959f290fcee7eb4a1f-bfaee39405.webp

Step three: Rain fire Now you have to reach generator 5 area. You will need to throw a G-strike outside the map on a stone tile. But before this, you need to press a red button inside a robot's head.

In solo, you need to go into Odin's head and press it, then you'll have limited time to do your thing. In multiplayer however, you can wait in front of the tile when someone else presses the button.

Step four: Unleash the horde You need now to send the Maxis drone (Check part bellow) into the hole. It will release a hole bunch of PanzerSoldaten which you need to take down.

Related image: images/source-guide/9171e5ae5e904a9e14e8966fb000fde6e1e6ef8d-6e44254adc.webp

Step five: Skewer the winged beast You will need to use a Zombie Blood bonus for this step. While in zombie blood, you will see a glowing plane similar to the one you saw to build the fire staff . Take it down.

Related image: images/source-guide/3a6dd04a19c68b104f9739b5ff6ce1556c6e2202-3bf652273c.webp

Now, an invisible zombie will run around the excavation site clockwisely which will be only visible in zombie blood . When you find him just kill him, he will drop the maxis drone, pick it back up.

(This is from the theater mode, without zombie blood you will only hear the zombie and not beeing able to see / kill him)

Step six: Wield a fist of Iron You need to equip the One Inch Punch which is an upgrade of your standard melee attack. Check the dedicated part in the guide.

Head back under the excavation site, besides the staff altars. Crusader zombies will come at you, with light mist around their arms. You must hit zombies with your fist to make the mist disappear. Once it is gone you can freely kill them.

Related image: images/source-guide/37dace8b1823900ab0bdc25eb6b2f0630eb4a881-f74e10a44a.webp

When done, a stone tablet will drop. Picking it up will upgrade your fist. Every player must upgrade their fists.

Related image: images/source-guide/0b7a954200c1f1ee5446f3062507ec3c76775d2d-65e37b03a9.webp

Step seven: Raise Hell Head to the Crazy place. Simply but no easily, you must place all the staffs on their elemental altars in the crazy palce, then killing a lot of zombies. When done, you will eventually have a white flash on your screen and beeing able to take the staffs back.

(Sorry for the visual glitches, blame the theater mode)

Related image: images/source-guide/1c813db2e78040d0cf480e9cf268fabfcbadf19c-918267b209.webp

Step eight: FREEDOM ! Still in the crazy place, you need to send maxis drone up on the roof. Once it is gone into the portal, all players must stand in the middle and press action, to end the game and to watch the ending cutscene.
