# Moon main easter egg

## Main easter egg quest - Part 1

Requirements:

- 1 to 4 players
- Hacker tool equipement
- Wave gun
- Quantic Entanglement Device
- Gersh Device

First step: Once the power is on, (see part above) head back outside the spawning room . On your right while facing the Earth, is an area with 4 computers.

Related image: images/source-guide/801d2674d63b818045be5d847e8ba225c9fa3269-4445f5b0c3.webp

Interact with them to start a memory game . The 4 terminals will glow one by one, showing you their color. Each computer will glow twice, so you can remember their colors.

Then, all 4 terminals will show you the same color. If it's blue, you must interact with the computer that is linked with the blue color. Each time you are correct, the will glow green , before repeating the sequence, with one color added.

Related image: images/source-guide/a1d0183ee9e719aeeee1fa46c1d04ce11d87f047-c6e97f0ca7.webp

Do this little game 5 times. When they turn black, the step is done.

If you are doing wrong, they will turn red and the step will come back to it's starting point.

Related image: images/source-guide/51b6620c3d1a6b1430c1925c76a39666205e580e-f0df6f7789.webp

Second Step: Head now to the laboratories , up the stairs in the MPD room. Those labs are separated into 3 floors. On the second floor stands on a wall 4 buttons . ( on the left of the stairs leading to the third floor )

Related image: images/source-guide/640e357d94037104658210a30b321987fe72aa68-c024615264.webp

The first button to the left must be hacked for 500 points Doing so, you will have a limited time (about a minute) to hack 4 panels with shiny lights on them . There are 8 of them scattered around the three floors, but only 4 will glow.

Related image: images/source-guide/0371aa31ad8e921aee2f1aed7fb7e68f7f86d56a-e6561edde1.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/1c7bbb701abca8eff8d008de103a3155d00ad424-068813086d.webp

The possibly glowing pannels are located:

- Three on the first floor
  Related image: images/source-guide/7afd95bc7faf3161a39f83ca0b67c506c6ccc7f6-76f47fb5a2.webp
- Two on the second floor
  Related image: images/source-guide/5a77e927d78499a0483eafbf9cedc221d0b9db3f-62b3f4c90e.webp
- Three on the third floor
  Related image: images/source-guide/5a724b8dba0870d04ba4dd7c43989dbf82281910-414fcf12b6.webp

Once done, head back to the buttons on the wall. You must activate all of them very rapidly. Making it easier if you are 4 players. If you are alone, just move from left to right while pressing action.

If done correctly, they will all glow.

Related image: images/source-guide/c5edb41cb8988588fdc795bb4873b7aaecb2cd42-d5dd56365e.webp

Third Step: For this step you will need patience. Three excavators might randomly activate to approach and breach the integrity of the base. One of them, the excavator Pi (π), will try to break tunnel 6 . Let it do so. Take then the hacker tool and use it on the affiliated console on the spawn .

Related image: images/source-guide/16fed6b5ca81bade8aecd1d66a91cd4f0e33efb0-c9ac379d25.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/1e3546ac3f75a98fff1fa1eaf20edcf47aa21ebc-3a5d778cc9.webp

Once the excavator is pushed back, head to the tunnel which just break . You should find lying on the ground, a dark metal ball. It is a Vrill Sphere. In order to move it, you will have to knife it. Follow the ball and keep knifing it when it stops. If it is stuck on a door, simply open it.

Related image: images/source-guide/562b25690ed470e976e8c1f1cb4f3a2932c4f9bb-63b70e6b99.webp

The ball will eventually stick on the antenna, on top of the spawn. In order to make the ball move on, you will need to shoot it down with the Wave-Gun.

Related image: images/source-guide/8e2702c237b505a1296e9ba5a9f16613b7b2a59e-739a9dddb2.webp

Keep following the ball on the other side of the spawn. It will eventually stick again in the ceiling , in the room around Stamin-up. You will need an explosive (Ray gun / Nades ...) to get it moving again.

Related image: images/source-guide/af16e76447caf786b0da33aedfa279a160f8ccec-7a1e433e51.webp

The step ends with the ball fitting on the MPD's front circular plate.

Related image: images/source-guide/ff5d2f73508a0442b73cd9efeb2abee2ff30f379-72a3f30ee3.webp

Fourth step: A glass container should have risen on the right side of the pyramid. You will have to kill 25 zombies near it , to fill the glass cylinder with their souls.

Related image: images/source-guide/efe87f7d61a5619c561445a1c73620bcdec854e0-1ccd1171d1.webp

Once done a particular sound will play, and you'll have to interact with the lever , on the right side of the pyramid.

Related image: images/source-guide/8a334b2f84ab704efb9be6ac42ad931cb9c2a564-a3495816e1.webp

The MPD will open, revealing Samantha Maxis which is trapped in the pyramid. You will also get a 90 seconds death machine, but don't worry this was only the first part of the EE.

Related image: images/source-guide/4eb60c56c5de7b1f76829857bc065d90994813d8-1bcddb8967.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/9ef31aafac778922df3617c705872bdcf68a0026-8ebfc453a5.webp

Fifth step: Teleport back to Area 51. On your right while standing on the teleporter and facing the PaP, you should see two hexagonal plates on a shelf.

Related image: images/source-guide/8f3cd03ae30b0e7b9ca3e4318d72de15b91dd866-923c1ff4ee.webp

Get them down with a grenade, then throw a Gersh device on them . If done correctly, they will be on the teleporter now.

Related image: images/source-guide/f27fc19d92837c3e61bfabc2c96cf23c2a1b28b3-5e1a4abf81.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/36cf8741bc35c1a46b3f5e3a122e072f5877b711-6755e16414.webp

Get back to the Moon, and throw a Q.E.D at the plates you just teleported with you . They will set on a machine near a computer, in a corner of the spawn.

Related image: images/source-guide/2ef93bbbc423de631fe83f3c142c48f4b863ed26-7ef6a467d0.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/24e63e90291d4fe64fdabbd6a4ecbca3dea353b0-70bb0c59d3.webp

It is commonly located in the laboratories on any floor, most likely in corners or near workbenches , beneath the desks where you can get the hacker tool, on the left of the door leading to the BioDome, or near the crates outside the labs, leading to Mule kick.

Now head back to the spawn area , and place the wire between thecomputer and the 2 plates you teleported steps ago. It is located in a corner of the spawning room.

The player beeing Richtofen will have to place the Golden rod between the plates , then repeatedly using action in front of the computer . Keyboard sounds will be heard, then a dialog will play. The screen will glow red then green a couple of time.

## Main easter egg quest - Part 2

Seventh Step: Return to the M.P.D. You will notice that 4 tubes will have risen. Like previously, you will need to kill 25 zombies near each tube . Each one will make Samantha screams.

Related image: images/source-guide/f04495eed646f03b29f5f8c7e8da741e2d2f2b9b-c38f19bdb6.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/3efa8bd64aa50fbc74a614862a9de7cfe958aabe-76a96b3921.webp

Once completed, place the Golden Rod in the Vril Interface (The front plate of the pyramid). Richtofen and Samantha will swap their souls, giving all perks permanently to this player.

Related image: images/source-guide/d0b7de1f58933bf75b07cf73766331a86c5d9355-dc3a32e990.webp

Eight Step: Throw a Q.E.D onto the vril sphere , in front of the pyramid. It will move, activating computers.

Head back to step 1 computers spot. You will need to do again a memory game, but this time you will have more colors to remember.

Related image: images/source-guide/67337c5bd15e21bcab317c6418d76e49092f4aed-9b820f1e30.webp

Each time you complete a memory game, a rocket will lift up, beeing ready to launch.

Related image: images/source-guide/5f2820dc13dab6cde89b118256236272124e8e4e-86648ff46f.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/f1a9d39585ca5fccfa0789e076e07c45ebddf926-a4b5f7dab0.webp

Related image: images/source-guide/7d5232828218bcd4a78c4ae0f661991d5ba6f655-e00974522c.webp

Once done, throw a Gersh device at the Vril Sphere , located on the close right to the computers. Dialog will follow, a countdown will start, Maxis will fires three missiles, obliterating the Earth.

Every players will receive permanent perks, and the Cryogenic Slumber Party achievement. GG !
