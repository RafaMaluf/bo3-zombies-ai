# music 3 - samantha sorrow

## overview

**Samantha Sorrow** is a special side easter egg on Gorod Krovi that works like a short hide-and-seek mini game.

It rewards you with:
- a **Max Ammo**
- the **Samantha Sorrow** music track

This one is less straightforward than the other music easter eggs.

Video reference provided by the user:
[YouTube reference for Samantha Sorrow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5i1f4M8Nug)

## step 1 - activate the starting condition

First, you must throw Monkey Bombs into 3 different areas that are currently being lit by dragon fire.

### steps
1. Wait for dragon fire to hit valid areas.
2. Throw Monkey Bombs into 3 separate dragon-fire areas.
3. Listen for the completion sound.

## step 2 - pick up the doll in spawn

After the setup is done, go to **Belinski Square**.

A doll should appear:
- in the corner between the spawn fountain and the graveyard
- in front of a tomb

### steps
1. Go to spawn.
2. Find the doll by the tomb near the fountain / graveyard corner.
3. Pick it up.

## step 3 - play the hide and seek sequence

Now you must find and shoot **5 dolls**, one after another.

Important notes:
- each doll appears one at a time
- they play music to help you find them
- there is a timer
- if you fail, Samantha laughs and you must interact with the grave again to restart

### known doll locations
The guide lists these possible locations:

- on top of a wooden pole next to the graveyard
- inside Department Store, below the second staircase, visible from the first staircase
- in the Infirmary, on a table in front of the Mystery Box and Stamin-Up
- below the Infirmary, on a table on a broken floor when going down toward the flooded cave
- in Dragon Command, on top of the unbroken pillar left of the staircase
- in front of Dragon Command, on the tank cannon
- in front of Dragon Command, on a metal platform high to the left of the entrance
- outside Supply Depot, behind a window beside the dragon-calling device
- inside Supply Depot, above the yellow console
- between Armory and Supply Depot, in the second unreachable train wagon, visible from the bridge wagon
- outside Armory / Supply Depot, on the first lamp on the right above the large Valkyrie sign
- outside Tank Factory, hanging in a sewer pipe
- in Tank Factory, on a beam to the right while waiting for the dragon
- in the flooded Operations Bunker, outside the window near Mule Kick

### steps
1. Listen for the doll music.
2. Search the map quickly.
3. Shoot each doll before it disappears.
4. Repeat until all 5 dolls are found.

## step 4 - return to spawn

After finding all 5 dolls, a sound will play.

### steps
1. Return to the same doll location in spawn where you started.
2. Interact with the doll again.
3. End the mini game.
4. Receive the reward.

## result

When completed successfully:
- you get a **Max Ammo**
- **Samantha Sorrow** plays
