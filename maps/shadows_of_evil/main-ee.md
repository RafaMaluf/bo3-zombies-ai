# main ee

## overview

The main easter egg can be progressed with fewer players, but the final completion requires 4 players.

## step 1 - unlock Pack-a-Punch

Complete the 4 district rituals, collect the Gateworms, place them on the altar in the Sacred Place, rebuild the Pack-a-Punch area, and complete the ritual there.

## step 2 - get and upgrade the sword

Get the Apothicon Sword and upgrade it into the Reborn Keeper Sword.

## step 3 - interact with Nero's book

Head to Nero's room. You need to find a book on the ground that was not there previously. Interact with it. It will start flying before vanishing. It is a large book on the ground, right to the left of the door.

Related image: images/main-ee/neros_book.jpg

## step 4 - flag step

### Important behavior during the step
- meatballs come from all directions
- the Shadow Man appears to damage the flag
- you need to pay attention to him because he is one of the main threats to the defense

### General loop
1. Pick up the flag underground.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/flag_underground.jpg
2. Take it to the required location.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/flag_spot_location.jpg
3. Place it.
4. Defend it from the incoming enemies.
5. Watch for the Shadow Man damaging the flag.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/shadowman_attacking_flag.jpg
6. Complete the defense and move to the next location.
7. Defend it again and take it to the ritual room.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/flag_ritual_room.jpg
8. Repeat until all required flag defenses are done.

## step 5 - fight the Shadow Man

1. Go to the Pack-a-Punch room.
2. Interact with the 4 Keepers there. They turn from purple to orange / red and release the Shadow Man from the bubble.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/keepers_red_after_interacting.jpg
3. The Shadow Man will start teleporting.
4. Keep shooting him until he is pulled toward the table.
5. Interact with the table to trap him there.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/shadowman_trapped.jpg

### Important notes
- You cannot stop shooting him after he is released from the bubble if you want this to work.
- This is a very easy step, but the team needs to stay coordinated and keep shooting him the whole time.
- If you fail, he regains his shield, the Keepers turn purple again, and you must repeat the Keeper interaction to break his shield again.

## final step

The final step only triggers if 4 players are in the lobby. This is as far as the easter egg can go with fewer players. If you are in a 3-player game or less, defeating the Shadow Man is the last step and there is no cutscene.

### Steps
1. After the Shadow Man is trapped, the map enters the final sequence.
2. Players periodically get the purple poisoned screen and need to pass through the glowing white orb.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/white_orb.jpg
3. Purple-mouthed Margwas keep spawning.
4. Killing one unlocks the infinite Beast Mode phase.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/infinite_beast_mode.jpg
5. Players split to shock the boxes near the train / metro areas so the rail turns green.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/shock_box_train.jpg
6. Another player calls the train and uses Beast Mode to shock his side.
7. A player in Junction shocks the Keepers.  
   Related image: images/main-ee/keepers_to_shock.png
8. If all shocks are done correctly, the cutscene triggers.

### Important notes
- The `shock_box_train.jpg` image is just one of the boxes. The other boxes look the same and are placed on the ceiling of the train area.
- Basically, you need to have the 3 parts of the rails and the 3 Keepers shocked while the tram is moving.
- The easiest way is having 2 people ready to shock their box, 1 in the middle, and 1 in human form.
- The human player calls the tram and tells everyone when to shock their part.
- At the same time, this player runs to a Beast altar, transforms, and shocks his side.
- If everyone times it correctly, the step should work fine.
