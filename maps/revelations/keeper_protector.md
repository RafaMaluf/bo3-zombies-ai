# keeper protector

## overview

The **Keeper Protector** is a support device that summons a Keeper companion to follow you and attack enemies.

It costs **5000 points** to summon, but the altar can be charged by multiple players, so one person does not need to pay the full amount alone.

To build it, you need 3 parts:
- a **flag**
- the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- a **purple gem**

After collecting them, you build the altar on one of the wall locations around the map.

## part 1 - flag

The flag piece is found in the **Origins / Mob of the Dead** side of the map.

### possible locations
- near the giant robot footstep
- on top of the excavation site in Origins
- in the cafeteria of Mob of the Dead, beside **Mule Kick**

## altar build locations

Once the 3 parts are collected, you can build the Keeper Protector altar on a wall location.

### possible altar locations
- in the mannequin area in **Kino Der Toten**, left when facing the stage
- in the cave with the Primis statues in **Der Eisendrache**
- in the giant robot footstep area in **Origins**
- on the right side of the green tank room in **Verruckt**

## summoning the keeper protector

After the altar is built, it can be charged with points.

### important notes
- total cost is **5000 points**
- multiple players can contribute to the same altar
- once summoned, the Keeper will follow you and attack enemies

## steps

1. Collect the flag piece from the Origins / Mob area.
2. Collect the skull piece from Verruckt.
3. Collect the purple gem from Der Eisendrache / Kino.
4. Go to one of the valid altar wall locations.
5. Build the Keeper Protector altar.
6. Deposit points into the altar until it reaches 5000.
7. Summon the Keeper Protector.

## full progression summary

1. Find the flag.
2. Find the Skull of Nan Sapwe piece.
3. Find the purple gem.
4. Build the altar at one of the wall locations.
5. Pay for the Keeper Protector.
6. Use it as a support companion.
