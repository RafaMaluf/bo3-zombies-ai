# time trial

## overview

Revelations has timed round challenges similar to Gorod Krovi.

If you hit certain round milestones fast enough, you unlock melee weapons that can be bought from the well between:
- Shangri-La stairs
- Primis altar in Der Eisendrache

## time goals and rewards

### round 5 under 5 minutes
Reward:
- **Nunchucks**
- one-shot until round 10

### round 10 under 12 minutes
Reward:
- **Skull Splitter**
- one-shot until round 15

### round 15 under 19 minutes
Reward:
- **Buzzcut**
- one-shot until round 25

### round 20 under 32 minutes
Reward:
- **Nightbreaker**
- one-shot until round 35

## tips

- use Gobblegums aggressively
- buy weapons early
- avoid opening too much of the map too fast
- same general speedrun-style ideas as Gorod Krovi

## result

- unlock melee weapon purchases from the well
- gain some XP as well
