# general

Zetsubou No Shima is one of the most system-heavy maps in Black Ops 3 Zombies. Instead of focusing mainly on one central mechanic, the map spreads its progression across several interconnected systems: buckets and colored water, plants, power activation, bunker progression, Pack-a-Punch, the Skull of Nan Sapwe, the KT-4, the Masamune, trials, and the main easter egg. Because of that, the map can feel messy at first, but once the systems are separated, its structure becomes much easier to understand.

A large part of the identity of Zetsubou No Shima comes from preparation and resource management. The map constantly asks you to gather materials, grow plants, move between labs, use water sources, and unlock equipment before you can fully stabilize your run. Unlike simpler maps, this one rewards planning much more than raw speed.

The map is set around 3 major gameplay spaces:
- the spawn / swamp area
- Lab A and Lab B
- the main bunker and its underground sections

From a progression point of view, the usual setup route is:
- get a bucket
- learn the water sources
- activate the power
- open the bunker
- unlock Pack-a-Punch
- obtain the Skull of Nan Sapwe
- craft the KT-4
- upgrade it into the Masamune
- complete trials and required equipment
- begin the main easter egg if desired

## key map systems

- **Buckets and water** are core to the map. You use them for generators, plant growth, and special item progression.
- **Plants** are one of the map’s biggest systems. Depending on the water used, and whether KT-4 / Masamune is used to fertilize them, they can give very different results.
- **Power** is not activated by a simple switch at first. You must first fill both generators with water, then progress through the bunker to reach the final power switch.
- **Pack-a-Punch** requires 3 parts and a short activation sequence around the machine.
- **The Skull of Nan Sapwe** is the specialist weapon of the map and is unlocked through 4 ritual-style skull cleansings.
- **KT-4** is the wonder weapon of the map and is crafted from 3 parts.
- **Masamune** is the upgraded KT-4 and is required for the main easter egg.
- **Trials** are important because completing them unlocks rewards and allows you to electrify the shield.
- **The Gas Mask** is extremely useful because it protects you from toxic gas and gives you more time underwater.
- **The Shield** is important for survival and for several side easter egg interactions.
- **The main easter egg** is not especially hard compared to some other BO3 maps, but it requires a lot of setup because most of the map systems feed into it.

## general practical note

Zetsubou No Shima is much easier once you stop thinking of it as one long chaotic quest and instead treat it as several smaller systems:
- water and buckets
- plants
- power and bunker access
- wonder weapon crafting
- support equipment
- trials
- main quest progression

That is the best way to organize both the map itself and the knowledge base for it.
