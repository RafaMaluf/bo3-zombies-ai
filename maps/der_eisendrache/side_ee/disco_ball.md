# disco ball

## overview

A secret disco easter egg can be activated in the Church's command room. When done correctly, the room lights up and disco music starts playing.

There is a globe on the ceiling of the room, with a moon and a rocket orbiting it. You must first shoot the moon to stop its rotation, then shoot the rocket when it is next to the moon.

## steps

1. Go to the Church's command room.
2. Look at the globe on the ceiling.
3. Shoot the moon to stop the rotation.
4. Wait until the rocket is next to the moon.
5. Shoot the rocket.
6. The disco effect and music will activate.
