# kt4

## overview

The **KT-4** is the wonder weapon of Zetsubou No Shima. It is crafted from 3 parts:

- **Spider Venom**
- **Green glowing flask**
- **Flower**

After collecting all 3, you build the KT-4 at the special crafting table in the bunker underground.

The KT-4 is also one of the core prerequirements for the map, since it leads into plants, side easter eggs, and the **Masamune** upgrade.

## part 1 - spider venom

Once the power is activated, go back to **Lab A** and interact with the control panel to lower the spider cage.

Then wait for a spider to enter the cage. When it does, the cage closes automatically. Go back upstairs and use the panel again.

After that, the spider explodes and drops the purple venom.

### steps
1. Turn on the power first.
2. Go to **Lab A**.
3. Interact with the cage control panel.
4. Lower the cage.
5. Wait for a spider to walk into it.
6. Once trapped, use the panel again.
7. Wait for the spider to explode.
8. Pick up the spider venom.

### practical note
A good time to set this up is before or around the first spider round, since spiders naturally begin to appear early and this makes the step much easier.

## part 2 - green glowing flask

The second KT-4 part is dropped by a special glowing zombie near **Lab B**.

Once the power is activated, keep playing around Lab B until a zombie spawns with a green glow. Kill that zombie and collect the dropped flask.

### steps
1. Turn on the power first.
2. Stay near **Lab B**.
3. Watch for a zombie with a green glow.
4. Kill it.
5. Pick up the green flask it drops.

## part 3 - flower

The third KT-4 part is a flower found deep in the underwater cavern network.

To get it:
- enter the bunker
- open the front door
- go downstairs
- open the door facing Mule Kick
- explore the underwater caverns
- at the bottom of the deeper blue-ish tunnel, pick up the flower

### steps
1. Enter the bunker.
2. Open the front path deeper inside.
3. Go downstairs.
4. Open the door facing **Mule Kick**.
5. Explore the underwater cavern system.
6. Find the deeper blue-ish tunnel.
7. Go to the bottom.
8. Pick up the flower.

### practical note
Be careful underwater. Juggernog helps, and you can also shoot spores to refresh your breath.

Related image: images/kt4/5planttopickup.jpg

## crafting the KT-4

Once all 3 parts are collected, go to the special crafting table in the bunker underground, near one of the ritual altars.

### steps
1. Collect the spider venom.
2. Collect the green glowing flask.
3. Collect the flower.
4. Go to the KT-4 crafting table.
5. Build the **KT-4**.

Related image: images/kt4/6wheretoassemble.jpg

## full progression summary

1. Turn on the power.
2. Use the Lab A cage to trap and process a spider for the venom.
3. Kill the glowing zombie near Lab B for the green flask.
4. Go through the underwater caverns and collect the flower.
5. Return to the bunker underground crafting table.
6. Build the **KT-4**.
