# general

Der Eisendrache is one of the most complete and popular maps in Black Ops 3 Zombies. It combines a relatively intuitive layout with a large amount of quest content, making it a map that works both for casual survival and for more advanced easter egg runs. The map is built around the castle, the courtyard, the teleporter system, the rocket test site, the Death Ray, the Wrath of the Ancients bow, the 4 elemental bow upgrades, the Rocket Shield, the Ragnarok DG-4, and the main easter egg quest.

Compared to simpler maps like The Giant, Der Eisendrache has many more layered mechanics, but its systems are still organized well enough that the map remains easy to learn once you understand the basic routes. The first part of the progression is usually focused on opening the map, turning on the power, activating Pack-a-Punch access, feeding the 3 dragon heads, and obtaining the base bow. From there, the map opens up into bow upgrades, buildables, side easter eggs, and the full main quest.

One of the reasons Der Eisendrache is so important for the app is that it contains a strong mix of systems:
- standard core progression such as power, Pack-a-Punch, and teleporting
- weapon quests through the Wrath of the Ancients and the 4 elemental upgrades
- buildables such as the Rocket Shield and Ragnarok DG-4
- a long main easter egg with multiple distinct steps
- several side easter eggs and utility rewards

## Key map systems

- Power must be turned on to activate the map’s main systems.
- Pack-a-Punch is tied to the teleporter room / undercroft progression and the anti-gravity area.
- The Wrath of the Ancients is the base bow and must be obtained before any upgraded bow can be made.
- There are 4 upgraded bows: Lightning, Wolf, Void, and Fire.
- The Rocket Shield is a buildable with important defensive value.
- The Ragnarok DG-4 is a specialist buildable required for the main easter egg.
- The rocket test site and teleporter are major parts of both movement and quest progression.
- The Death Ray is both a gameplay tool and a main easter egg system.
- The map includes multiple side easter eggs, utility rewards, helmets, and hidden interactions.
- Der Eisendrache can be played simply as a survival map, but most of its identity comes from its layered quest structure.
